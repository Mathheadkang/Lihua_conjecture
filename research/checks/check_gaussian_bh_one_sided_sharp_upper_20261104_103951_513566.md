- Obstruction: **Rank-one covariance cannot attain the proposed scale.** Every admissible rank-one unit-diagonal Gaussian covariance has a representation $T_i=\theta_i+s_iZ$ with $s_i\in\{-1,1\}$. A false rejection at level $q<1/2$ requires $s_iZ\geq\overline\Phi^{-1}(q)$, because its mean is nonpositive and its BH threshold is at most $q$. Thus any false discovery lies in two Gaussian tails of total probability $2q$, and $\operatorname{FDR}\leq2q$ uniformly over dimension, signs, and means, including negative true-null means and arbitrary alternative means. The antipodal tied-block subfamily in [the boundary candidate](../candidates/cand_gaussian_bh_one_sided_sharp_upper_r1_s2_antipodal_negative_null_boundary.md) has the stronger exact bound $\operatorname{FDR}\leq q$. Both bounds vanish after division by $q\sqrt{\log(1/q)}$.
- Obstruction: **The prescribed common-gate source-quantile family crosses too early to violate the bound.** This obstruction concerns [the weak-common-gate candidate](../candidates/cand_gaussian_bh_one_sided_sharp_upper_r1_s1_weak_common_gate_quantiles.md), with $B=\lceil q^{-1}\rceil$, $\alpha=q/B$, $k=\lceil\alpha^{-2}\rceil$, null loading $r=1/k$, gate loading $\epsilon=(\log(1/q))^{-1/2}$, block size $m=k^{k^3}$, and the retained source secondary means discretized by midpoint quantiles. A single block's secondary nonnulls force a global BH crossing and cap the rejection cutoff; the same cap holds after setting any null p-value to zero. Averaging two block gates supplies a Gaussian cap whose correlation with the null block's factor stays uniformly below one. The resulting conditional null-tail envelope is integrable, and the discarded factor events cost $o(q)$, giving $\operatorname{FDR}=O(q)$ for these exact finite arrays. The candidate includes null means $0$ and $-q$; the conditional tail bound also dominates more negative means whenever the same nonnull-only cap is preserved. This family therefore has zero ratio on the proposed scale.
- Obstruction: **The balanced planar angular grid has only order-$q$ FDR.** In [the dense planar candidate](../candidates/cand_gaussian_bh_one_sided_sharp_upper_r2_s3_dense_planar_directions.md), all means are zero and $N=\lceil q^{-3}\rceil$ equally spaced unit directions project a two-dimensional standard Gaussian vector. Consequently $\operatorname{FDR}\leq5q$ for these exact finite arrays, so NOT fails and their ratio to $q\sqrt{\log(1/q)}$ tends to zero. This excludes the balanced zero-mean grid, rather than every rank-two covariance model.

- Obstruction: **Independent all-null pairs cannot accumulate inflation on the proposed scale.** In [the negative-pair candidate](../candidates/cand_gaussian_bh_one_sided_sharp_upper_r4_s4_independent_negative_pairs.md), each pair has correlation $-1/2$, pairs are independent, all means are $0$ or $-q$, and the total covariance has rapidly growing full rank. The argument remains valid for arbitrary nonpositive null means and the sole alternative's allowed finite mean and joint Gaussian dependence on the nulls; it does not cover dependent centered nulls or a growing population of alternatives.

## Limitations

- **The two logarithmic-rank sign-cube models remain unresolved test cases.** The [all-null sign orbit](../ideas/idea_17bd1034395f.md) and [mixed-mean sign cube](../ideas/idea_bd097b4b530f.md) are draft ideas, not failed candidates or surviving refuters. The round's report and their current records do not settle their BH asymptotics, and no sign-cube obstruction is asserted here.
- The missing sign-cube calculation is the simultaneous $q\downarrow0$ behavior of the actual BH FDR while rank, dimension, means, and the finite testing arrays vary with $q$. The [conditional sign-cube free-energy approach](../ideas/idea_4cbdb4b7e0d9.md) remains a draft route: a conditional rate or fixed-parameter likelihood calculation alone does not establish the Gaussian-integrated global BH limit. Uniform tail prefactors and discretization errors on the $q\sqrt{\log(1/q)}$ scale remain necessary, together with the actual global cutoff and the distinction between an all-null rejection event and mixed-mean FDP. A fixed-model or fixed-level limit does not justify an interchange of these limits.
- Inconclusiveness of the current bounds in the examined factor representations does not prove that every equivalent factor representation is unhelpful. Covariance rank, common-factor dimension, residual variance, and oriented-null complexity remain distinct. The available approved entropy and radial bounds keep their stated coverage; these test cases do not invalidate them or establish that their hypotheses fail in every representation.
- The pair comparison does not extend here to dependent blocks or mixed alternatives, and the single-alternative argument does not extend here to general dependent nulls or many alternatives. Neither obstruction supplies a general monotonicity theorem allowing arbitrary negative true-null means to be replaced by zero.
- No verified one-sided asymptotic counterexample was obtained in this round. The unrestricted upper bound and its proposed constant $1/\sqrt\pi$ remain open; the two scoped order-$q$ exclusions and the unresolved sign-cube test cases have different evidentiary status.
