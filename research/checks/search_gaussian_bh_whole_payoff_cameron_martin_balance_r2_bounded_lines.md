## Obstructions

- An unbounded countably orthogonal Gaussian background does not itself create an unbounded translation along a fixed deterministic direction. The particlewise displacement satisfies |tau (t-u) <w,v>| <= tau |t-u| ||v|| for every unit-ball loading. Thus the proposed unbounded-background family does not defeat the common-factor score comparison merely because the background field lacks a bounded samplewise supremum. No such supremum was used, and no plausible marked-set violation was identified.
- Unbounded lawful means and infinite support do not enlarge that fixed-direction displacement. Strictly positive private variance, c(w) >= s > 0, also prevents a literal unsmoothed atom from arising in these proposals. These mechanisms blocked the proposed routes based solely on unbounded means or support; the searches did not turn this observation into a proof of conditional differentiation or of its exchanges for an arbitrary directing law.
- Tied suffix minima and the rank-one endpoint supplied no plausible violating object in this round. Ties alone do not contradict a pointwise right envelope formula retaining every active minimizer; they can obstruct stronger uniform convergence of derivative quotients, which the statement does not assert. The bounded-shift mechanism blocked the proposed endpoint-escape route, but common compact localization and endpoint domination remain proof obligations.
- A zero direction, or a direction orthogonal to the loadings almost everywhere under the directing law, leaves the translated scores and conditional payoff stationary. These families therefore cannot produce a nonzero directional-response counterexample by translation alone.
