The cap contains the global-null Gaussian Simes bound under arbitrary (including negative) correlations at order q(1+u): with no alternatives and few highly correlated partners per coordinate, every null is rare and the cap's left side is the Simes size. Searches found no counterexample (identical-null clusters cost at most q, orthogonal staircase copies stay below about 22q). What would get past it: a uniform Gaussian Simes bound, such as the draft linear Simes bound, which would settle the no-alternative case, plus an argument for the competition between rare clusters and alternatives.
