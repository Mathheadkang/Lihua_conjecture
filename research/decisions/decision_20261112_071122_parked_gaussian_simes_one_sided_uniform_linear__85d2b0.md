Missing step: sparse crossings. A Gaussian tilt with Lehec's semi-log-convex tail bound settles dense-rank crossings with order q for arbitrary correlation signs, and Slepian handles pairwise nonpositively correlated crossers, but very high thresholds with few crossers cost about q per threshold window over about log N windows, and entropy bounds fail for spread-out weakly correlated mixed-sign configurations. It suffices to show that the supremum over all dimensions and correlation matrices of the Simes size at one fixed level is below one, or any dimension-free bound tending to zero. This bound is the shared crux of the sign-splitting route and of both rare caps.
