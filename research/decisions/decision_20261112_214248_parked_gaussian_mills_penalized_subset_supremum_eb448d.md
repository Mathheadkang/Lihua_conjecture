Set aside: this lemma is equivalent, up to constants, to a uniform fixed-level Gaussian Simes cap, so it restates the open problem rather than shrinking it. Per-scale bounds hold (convexity and Ehrhard give probability at least one half for each subset size), but independent blocks of exact clones put probability about 1/(eA) on every single scale, so no per-scale bound uniform in the correlation sums over the dyadic scales. What would get past it: a collective argument over scales, for example a bound on the expected penalized maximum (Ehrhard would then finish), or a design-dependent budget over scales that charges clone blocks and spread negatively correlated designs together.
