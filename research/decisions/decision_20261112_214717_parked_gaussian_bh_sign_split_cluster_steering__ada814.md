Set aside: in the special case of independent nulls, singleton clusters and a constant carrier, this lemma is the open class-E conjecture (FDR at most Cq for independent nulls with arbitrary Gaussian alternatives), the core steering estimate of the main theorem. The sub-Poisson calibration needs the full alternative vector in the conditioning, which can reveal the nulls and costs q log(1/q); Su's linking bound gives the same loss. Searches found no counterexample. What would get past it: a Gaussian-specific proof of class E that uses the per-alternative budget of squared loadings on the nulls (at most one) to stop alternatives from tracking the extreme null.
