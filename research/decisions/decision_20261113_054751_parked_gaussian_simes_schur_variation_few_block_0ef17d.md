Set aside without a plan: with the approved clone-block bound, this compression alone implies the linear Simes bound, so it is at least as hard as the main conjecture. Few-block models are closed (no approximation), the path laws are not mixtures of few-block Gaussians (conditioning creates means), and clustering or subsampling directions changes the Simes profile at order one; the remaining tools sum pair or crossing terms that grow with dimension. Searches found no counterexample (variation at most about 0.27q in all families tried). What would get past it: a dimension-free mechanism reducing many directions to few for the Simes event, for example a monotone functional along the Schur-square path or an exchangeable-array compactness argument; the extremal-array route tries the latter.
