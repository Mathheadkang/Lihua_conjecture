## Definition

Fix a lawful deterministic directing law and fixed positive regularizers in the [private-smoothed population](gaussian_bh_private_smoothed_directing_population.md). Use the barriers, active sets, densities, $g_X,w_{X,\pm},m_t$ and $E_0$ of the [signed near-active convention](gaussian_bh_signed_near_active_gradient_cones.md). All laws and particles are chosen before Gaussian innovations. Put
$$
\widetilde E=\mathbb R\times\{v\in\mathcal H:\|v\|\le1\}\times\{0,1\},
\qquad K_{X,x}(z)=S((z-\theta-\tau_sX(v))/c_s(v)).
$$
The strong parameter topology is the product of the usual real topology, Hilbert norm topology and discrete tag topology. The lawful parameter space is the subset $E_{\rm ball}$ in the approved population definition; using $\widetilde E$ to describe kernel extensions permits no unlawful perturbation. Set
$$
L_{q,a}=\sup_{a/2\le r\le1}\frac q{\phi(u_q(r))},\quad
W_a=\|\chi_a'\|_\infty+1/a,\quad T_a=1+\log(1/a).
$$

For $j\ge1$, a hybrid selector is a jointly measurable map $t=t(X,r)$, $a<r<1$, satisfying almost everywhere one of the following alternatives:

- $t=r$ and $B_X(r)=M_X(r)$;
- $t\in\mathbb Q$, $r<t<1$, $B_X(t)\le M_X(r)+1/j$ and $|B_X'(t)|\le1/j$.

Write $\mathcal S_j$ for this family. Values on exceptional null sets may be arbitrary. Derivatives here are ordinary rank derivatives. No assertion about nonemptiness or approximation is part of this convention.

When the expressions exist, associate with each selector and either sign the pair $(G_\pm^t,c_\pm^t)$ given by
$$
G_\pm^t(x)=\frac1\sigma\mathbb E_X\int_a^1
w_{X,\pm}(r)\phi(M_X(r))
\frac{K_{X,x}(Q_X(t(X,r)))}{h_X(Q_X(t(X,r)))}\,dr,
$$
$$
c_\pm^t=\frac1\sigma\mathbb E_X\int_a^1
w_{X,\pm}(r)\phi(M_X(r))m_{t(X,r)}\,dr.
$$
Expectations at each fixed particle use a measurable isonormal representative; simultaneous validity is required for the specified countable particle and endpoint families. Continuity and representative independence, where needed beyond those families, are conclusions to prove.

The ambient potential space consists of pairs $(G,c)$ with $c\in\mathbb R$, $G:\widetilde E\to\mathbb R$ continuous in the strong parameters, continuously differentiable in $\theta$, and with $\partial_\theta G$ also strongly continuous. Give it the locally convex topology generated by all evaluations of $G$, of $\partial_\theta G$, and of $c$. Thus its continuous linear functionals use finitely many such coordinates. Fix any countable strong dense subset $\mathscr D\subset\widetilde E$; the symbol $D$ remains reserved for the full FDP. Evaluations on $\mathscr D$ will metrize the topology on a family only after the needed uniform equicontinuity and boundedness are proved; neither the strong Hilbert ball nor the entire ambient potential space is declared compact.

Whenever the generating pairs belong to this space, define
$$
\mathscr C_j^\pm=\overline{\operatorname{co}}\{(G_\pm^t,c_\pm^t):t\in\mathcal S_j\},
\qquad \mathscr C^\pm=\bigcap_{j\ge1}\mathscr C_j^\pm,
$$
with closure in the declared evaluation topology. This notation assumes no compactness, nonemptiness, support formula or stationarity. For integrable $G$ define its centered version $\overline G=G-\int G\,d\pi$. For a finite signed measure $\zeta$ for which the integral exists, and $\lambda\in\mathbb R$, write
$$
h_{\mathscr C}(\zeta,\lambda)
=\sup_{(G,c)\in\mathscr C}\left\{\int G\,d\zeta+\lambda c\right\}.
$$
For the mass directions $\zeta=\rho(\nu-\pi)$ used in the associated results, centering leaves this pairing unchanged. Continuity of such integral pairings on the sets in question requires proof, rather than following from the ambient topology alone.
