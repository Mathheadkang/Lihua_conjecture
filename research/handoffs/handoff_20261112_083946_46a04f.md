- gaussian_simes_one_sided_uniform_linear_bound parked (agent_gave_up): No new plan for the linear Simes bound: the Gibbs (exponential-kernel) form I directed loses a Mills factor of about the square root of log N on sparse crossings, and its soft version is already false for independent coordinates at fixed level.
The usable replacement is a hinge kernel: rejection forces the supremum over thresholds of the normalized excess (1/N) sum (T_i - x)_+ over E(G - x)_+ to be at least a constant times sqrt(log 1/q), a union of half-spaces on top-fraction averages whose means are controlled for every correlation.
Missing: a dimension-free bound, summed over dyadic scales, for the probability that this ratio exceeds a, of order 1/a; it holds for independent, fully correlated and block models but fails without Gaussian structure, and no available tool gives it. Recorded as an idea; the result goes back to set aside.
- gaussian_bh_near_boundary_frozen_ray_transfer parked (agent_gave_up): The transfer reduces to the leave-one-out BH cutoff tracking each null's statistic inside a thin layer just above the cutoff; for a fixed null and fixed residual this tracking loses the full Benjamini-Yekutieli factor, so any gain must come from averaging over the residual or summing over nulls. Cameron-Martin smoothing fails for clone or antithetic alternatives, and a budget on how many nulls an alternative can steer becomes counting cutoff crossings along moving lines, the obstruction already met on the main result. Getting past it needs a collective anti-concentration of the leave-one-out cutoffs across nulls (for example an exchangeability or second-moment bound on simultaneous tracking), not a per-null estimate.
