- gaussian_bh_near_boundary_frozen_ray_transfer parked (agent_gave_up): The transfer reduces to the leave-one-out BH cutoff tracking each null's statistic inside a thin layer just above the cutoff; for a fixed null and fixed residual this tracking loses the full Benjamini-Yekutieli factor, so any gain must come from averaging over the residual or summing over nulls. Cameron-Martin smoothing fails for clone or antithetic alternatives, and a budget on how many nulls an alternative can steer becomes counting cutoff crossings along moving lines, the obstruction already met on the main result. Getting past it needs a collective anti-concentration of the leave-one-out cutoffs across nulls (for example an exchangeability or second-moment bound on simultaneous tracking), not a per-null estimate.
- gaussian_simes_sparse_crossing_fixed_level_cap parked (agent_gave_up): Set aside: no executable plan. What would get past it: a collective maximal inequality for the normalized excess, for example a positive lower bound on the Gaussian measure of the hinge acceptance polyhedron uniformly in the correlation, or an active-set surface bound for hinge upcrossings. The other two routes to the Simes bound do not need this lemma.
- gaussian_simes_sparse_positive_class_hitting parked (agent_gave_up): Set aside: when every positively correlated class is small, no fixed prior hits the sparse crossings in proportion, and this lemma reduces (up to a small error) to the sparse-crossing fixed-level cap with value one quarter, which is set aside for the same reason. What would get past it: a collective maximal inequality charging the supremum over thresholds once for weakly correlated, mixed-sign configurations; the same tool would also settle the sparse cap.
- gaussian_mills_penalized_subset_supremum_acceptance parked (agent_gave_up): Set aside: this lemma is equivalent, up to constants, to a uniform fixed-level Gaussian Simes cap, so it restates the open problem rather than shrinking it. What would get past it: a collective argument over scales, for example a bound on the expected penalized maximum (Ehrhard would then finish), or a design-dependent budget over scales that charges clone blocks and spread negatively correlated designs together.
- gaussian_bh_sign_split_cluster_steering_bound parked (agent_gave_up): Set aside: in the special case of independent nulls, singleton clusters and a constant carrier, this lemma is the open class-E conjecture (FDR at most Cq for independent nulls with arbitrary Gaussian alternatives), the core steering estimate of the main theorem. What would get past it: a Gaussian-specific proof of class E that uses the per-alternative budget of squared loadings on the nulls (at most one) to stop alternatives from tracking the extreme null.
