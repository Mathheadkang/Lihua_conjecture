Unproved as a node but elementary: on {Lambda>1} the maximizer satisfies Lambda <= c_0^{-1} int_{\hat w}^{\hat w+1} L_a da (curvature of log L_a at least -1). Hence for every W>0, E[min{c,Lambda}; \hat w<=W] <= 1 + c_0^{-1}(W+1), using only E L_a = |omega| <= 1. With W = K sqrt(log c) the target reduces to the large-scale part E[min{c,Lambda}; \hat w > K sqrt(log c), Lambda>1], where the posterior is far from the prior: KL(p_{\hat w} || omega) = \hat w^2/2 - log Lambda >= (K^2/2-1) log c. The one-factor family (\hat w about u/sqrt q, posterior spread, self-overlap about q) shows this large-scale part still contains spread posteriors with large Lambda, so the reduction does not remove the obstruction of the two failed plans.
