The researcher requests three distinct architectures before a long proof attempt. This portfolio is unproved, creates no completion root, and preserves every original quantifier.

A. Rejected-index information and Gaussian overshoot. Formulated from the original finite model before using historical architectures. Let p=FDR>0 and m0 be the true-null count. The probability law Q(i,dg)=1{i is a rejected true null} gamma_d(dg)/(p R(g)) retains the common largest BH rank and the original labels. Relative to Gaussian measure times the uniform null label its exact KL divergence is E_Q log(m0/(pR)). Prove this finite/singular identity, then seek a Gaussian selected-score entropy estimate charging adaptive selection against log(m0/R), with only a small-q overshoot cost left. Combine with the actual BH threshold and Mills estimates for the target order. The score-entropy estimate is genuinely unproved: ordinary entropy inequalities introduce selected-score variance and hence log N. Rewriting the desired FDR bound as an entropy inequality is not a mechanism. A dimension-free bound is conceivable because the exact density contains the same 1/R that pays label-selection complexity. Tests: independent all-null N tending to infinity at fixed q, perfectly correlated nulls, antipodal clone blocks, and critical alternatives with negative null means. The approved entropy-trace counterexample forbids substituting a positive trace proxy for Q. Existing model definitions suffice for initial work.

B. Multiscale covariance decomposition. Represent T_i=theta_i+v_i.G with unit vectors and split coherent versus diffuse directions at scales tied to sqrt(log(1/q)). Use Gaussian comparison or conditional rare-count estimates on diffuse parts and weighted trigger budgets on coherent parts. Essential unproved gates: a PSD-compatible decomposition with total rejection-weighted cost independent of dimension; a diffuse contribution estimate at the actual BH crossing; and a coherent-block estimate retaining correlated random backgrounds. This mechanism is structural covariance analysis, not entropy of Q or signed payoff transport. Potential dimension-free payment comes from original test fractions rather than a net size or cluster count. Applicable approved tools: directional-entropy bound on genuinely controlled pieces, central-window core, iid frozen-ray theorem only for truly independent equal-weight pieces. Test near-equicorrelation at scale 1/log(1/q), simplex negative correlations, many almost orthogonal clusters, heterogeneous block sizes and vanishing private noise. Count concentration for the full model is not assumed. Compare the approved Mehler-congestion counterexample at its actual q-dependent loading scale. Any uncontrolled graph degree, inverse noise or log N is fatal.

C. Global signed transport of the complete finite BH payoff. Retain numerator jumps and the negative denominator response together at the common largest qualifying rank; use Gaussian surface estimates only after this cancellation. Essential gates: finite signed identity with original null labels and ties; a rank-compatible allocation of net global flux with total Gaussian surface/frame cost O(q sqrt(log(1/q))); uniform singular approximation. The central allocation remains unproved, not a result hidden under a new name. Dimension-free payment might use the bounded full payoff and actual Gram covariance rather than a free positive contraction. Approved finite signed p-path, fractional-frame surface kernel and central-window core are potentially useful only within their verified scopes and native dependency rules. Tests: approved positive-jump, translated-cell and antipodal-trace examples, independent staircase resets and singular ties. Per-face positive parts or anchor-wise transport would repeat known failures.

Comparison: fixed numerical losses are acceptable; complexity growth and every-constant counterexamples are not. A gets the first bounded analytic gate test because its exact rejected-index law preserves 1/R without the old face charging. B is a mathematically different reserve. C is only worth revisiting if an actual new global allocation mechanism is found. None presently constitutes an executable proof of the main theorem. State and falsify the first missing gate before a long attempt. Preserve the difference between failure from sharp constants, a disproved stronger inequality, and an essential estimate still open.
