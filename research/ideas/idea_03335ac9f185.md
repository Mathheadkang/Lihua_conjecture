For the draft spherical population target, a restricted proof step from round 9 excludes any dimension-four mixture whose total nonnull mass epsilon=1-pi0 satisfies epsilon <= Q(2u)/q, with u>=104 and q=2Q(u). No support or moment condition on its nonnull means is needed. This is a family obstruction, not a proof or verification of the unrestricted target.

For any r,x, F_r(x)<=2H_r(x)+epsilon. If r<=2u has finite first contact with positive FDP, then u<=x_r<r<=2u; the contact identity and epsilon<=Q(2u)/q<=Q(x_r)/q imply H_r(x_r)/Q(x_r)>=1/(2q). In d=4, for u<=x<r and h=r-x, the cap density gives H_r(x)<=(4 sqrt(2)/(3 pi))(h/r)^(3/2). Mills' lower bound then gives

$$
\frac{H_r(x)}{Q(x)}\leq C(1+u^{-2})\frac{e^{r^2/2}}{r^2},\qquad C=\frac{8\sqrt3}{\sqrt\pi}e^{-3/2}<2.
$$

Here one uses x+x^{-1}<=r(1+u^{-2}), e^{-rh+h^2/2}<=e^{-rh/2}, and max_{h>=0} h^(3/2)e^(-rh/2)=(3/r)^(3/2)e^(-3/2). Hence every such positive-FDP radius obeys g(r)=r^2 e^(-r^2/2)<4(1+u^(-2))q. Since g decreases for r>=u and g(u)>4(1+u^(-2))q, there is a unique b>u with equality, and all those radii exceed b. The exact chi-four tail yields P(R>=b)<=2(1+u^(-2))(1+2u^(-2))q<(5/2)q. Separately P(R>2u)<Q(u)=q/2, using Mills' bound and u>=104. Since FDP<=1, FDR<3q. The small-mass premise implies pi0>1/2, while M_4=1/pi gives the target coefficient greater than 7; therefore this family satisfies the target strictly.

The displayed candidate epsilon=e^(-u^4), alternative mean u+u^(-1), satisfies the premise because Q(2u)/q >= (1/5)e^(-3u^2/2)>e^(-u^4). The argument leaves appreciable or intermediate contamination, growing dimension, and global contact switching for arbitrary W open. It does not assert a finite Gaussian-vector approximation.
