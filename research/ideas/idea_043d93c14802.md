UNVERIFIED companion path after the supporting spherical population theorem. Fix q,d>=4,W first. Consider deterministic finite arrays T_j=mu_j+v_j·Z, ||v_j||=1, mu_j>=0, zero residual; each finite vector is exactly Gaussian with PSD unit diagonal. Suppose their conditional total p-subCDF and null subCDF converge uniformly in p∈[0,1] for almost every Z to the spherical W×uniform-direction population F and pi0*2H. Such deterministic sequences exist by drawing iid (mu,v) from W×sphere, applying SLLN to rational thresholds at each fixed Z, continuity of conditional p-CDF, and Fubini to select a sequence good for almost every GaussianZ; nullmass convergence handled with the same indicator. No random-covariance mixture may be called one Gaussian model: fix one good deterministic realization.

Potential exact statement: limsup_N FDR_BH(q;array_N) <= the explicit spherical population bound, with no uniform rate of finite approximation asserted. Needed proof: in core radii [r0,L] from the candidate, every population zero f(r,x)=F(r,x)-2Q(x)/q has either f_x>0 or f_r>0, since f_x=0 implies D>0 by the pointwise budget. Thus zero set is a C1 curve locally. The critical values of projection to r (tangencies f_x=0) have Lebesgue measure zero by the one-dimensional Sard fact for C1 functions, which can be proved directly using small derivative intervals; countable cover handles unbounded x/W. Outside exceptional radii, finite first selection is a strict upcrossing; uniform empirical-CDF convergence plus vanishing finite BH grid spacing forces selected p thresholds to converge to the largest population threshold. If population has no positive root, finite selected thresholds must go to0. Since zero-residual nulls satisfy |v_i·Z|<=R, all null p-values are >=2Q(R)>0 at every fixed finite R; therefore such ghost roots have zero FDP eventually. This is the key advantage over noisy-clone ghost roots.

On lower radii r<r0, even if tangencies prevent actual firstroot convergence, any positive subsequential selected threshold gives limiting FDP q*pi0*H_r(x)/Qx for some x>=u; the original radial Bayes envelope has integral<=q*pi0 here. Thresholds going0 again have no nulls. Above L, FDP<=1 and chi-tail bound applies. On core, limit equals actual populationFDP almost everywhere; bounded reverse Fatou then yields limsup finiteFDR <=same three-piece bound. This would connect the continuous model to actual Gaussian finite arrays, but only as a fixed-q,d,W large-N limsup, not a uniform all-N theorem. After proving it, q-dependent d,W can be followed by a sufficiently accurate diagonal N(q); no arbitrary exchange of limits or rates.

Every step, especially Sard regularity for arbitrary W, uniform CDF convergence, no-root argument and null-label handling, requires an independent proof. Do not write it as established or apply to arbitrary finite arrays. Both original unrestricted targets remain unchanged.
