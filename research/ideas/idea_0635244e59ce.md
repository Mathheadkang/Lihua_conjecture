Primary scoped source check: Marco Barchiesi, Alessio Brancolini, Vesa Julin, Sharp dimension free quantitative estimates for the Gaussian isoperimetric inequality, arXiv:1409.2106v3, https://arxiv.org/html/1409.2106v3. Introduction, Main Theorem: with mass Φ(s), perimeter deficit D_iso=Pγ(E)−exp(−s²/2), and barycenter asymmetry β=min_ω |b(E)−b(Hω,s)|, β<=12sqrt(2π)(1+s²)D_iso. Their Pγ uses density (2π)^(-(n−1)/2), not the standard n-dimensional Gaussian-density surface convention; their φ denotes the CDF. The stated squared normal excess equals 2D_iso+2sqrt(2π)β. Only these statements and the introductory outline were read; no complete proof adaptation is claimed.

Own applicability assessment: actual finite BH FDP superlevel sets are finite threshold-arrangement unions, so set-level Gaussian perimeter tools can potentially be applied after checking finite-perimeter details. However, the target coherence 1−x measures selected-null bulk/heat directions and is NOT the boundary normal excess or isoperimetric deficit. A bridge from the actual signed null-marked rank-face ledger to these nonnegative geometric quantities is an essential new proof obligation; dropping labels or FDP denominator jumps is invalid. The theorem bounds asymmetry from ABOVE by perimeter deficit and does not automatically upper-bound the target trace or supply the needed q scale. It does not require the finite-Fisher premise that blocked earlier smooth-density stability tools. A future plan may inspect a layer-cake/coarea bridge, but must derive it before treating this paper as an applicable dependency.

Related primary check: Eldan, A two-sided estimate for the Gaussian noise stability deficit, arXiv:1307.2781v3, Theorem2, https://arxiv.org/html/1307.2781v3, has constants depending on set mass and additional small-deficit conditions. Those constants cannot silently be made uniform as the BH/FDP event mass tends to zero. No source asset, bibliography change, or proof is asserted here.
