A genuinely different route after the failed positive-flux envelope: keep means theta fixed and use C_{t,eta}=[t Sigma+(1-t+eta^2) I]/(1+eta^2), t in[0,1], eta>0. The ordinary BH FDP f is constant on the finite Gaussian-statistic grid with thresholds u_k=barPhi^{-1}(qk/N). Derive the exact Gaussian interpolation derivative from signed mixed two-coordinate boundary increments of f, retaining all largest-rank active events. Define a symmetric H_t with zero diagonal and off-diagonal boundary terms scaled by 1/[2(1+eta^2)], checking the factor from trace double counting, so d E f/dt=tr(Sigma H_t). Do not assume H_t is PSD or a convex-potential Hessian. The proposed first hard gate is an explicit measurable diagonal completion D_t-H_t PSD with integral tr(D_t)dt <= A q(1+U), U=barPhi^{-1}(q), A finite universal and independent of eta,N,theta,Sigma. This sufficient gate is unproved and stronger than an endpoint FDR difference. If it holds, unit diagonal and PSD of Sigma give tr(Sigma H_t)<=tr(D_t), while the independent starting FDR is <=q*pi0, not generally equal for negative null means. Only after such uniform control may eta tend to zero for each finite original model; finite BH threshold-boundary probabilities are zero by the continuous univariate marginals, so dominated continuity covers singular Sigma without exchanging a supremum with a limit. Failure of completion or any unresolved dimension/noise/rank dependence must be recorded at the first gate, not hidden as an assumption. No optimal constants are pursued; neither the completion nor the original goal is proved by this proposal.
