Draft auxiliary counterexample derived from idea_a47114025878, pending independent check; not a counterexample to the total-FDR conjecture. It tests whether one can bound EACH actual boundary-null contribution by C(q/m)sqrt(log(1/q)), uniformly over all the other means.

Take 0<q<1/2, K>=2, m=2K, a=q/(2K), z_v=barPhi^-1(v), one Z~N(0,1). Set T_1=Z (boundary null), T_i=Z-z_a for 2<=i<=K (negative nulls), and T_{K+j}=theta_j-Z for 1<=j<=K, theta_j=z_{aj}+z_{a(j+1)}>0. This exact rank-one Gaussian covariance has unit diagonal; all true nulls have means<=0 and all alternatives positive.

For the forced-zero BH rank of coordinate1, use its actual p U=barPhi(Z), uniform. For U>=a all other null p-values are>=1/2>q. Alternative p-values are ordered by j and p_{K+j}(U)<=a(j+1) iff U>=aj. Hence r_1(U)=j+1 on [aj,a(j+1)) for j=1,...,K-1, and r_1=K+1 on [aK,1). On every interval [aj,a(j+1)], j=1,...,K, the null is rejected because U<=a r_1(U). Its actual FDR contribution I_1=E[1{P1<=a r1}/r1] therefore lies between a(H_{K+1}-1) and a H_{K+1}; the upper adds at most a for U<a, and for U>a(K+1) it is not rejected.

Choose K=ceil(exp(1/q)). Then I_1/(a sqrt(log(1/q))) diverges: the candidate universal per-null sharp-scale estimate is false EVEN FOR AN ACTUAL ZERO-MEAN NULL, not merely the old Uniform surrogate at a negative null. This obstructs applying the same individual contribution estimate independently and summing over all nulls with arbitrary remaining means.

No main-conjecture violation: all negative null false discoveries require Z>=z_a+z_q and have total FDP contribution<=barPhi(z_a+z_q)<=a. Thus total FDR lies between a(H_{K+1}-1) and a(H_{K+1}+1), which is o(q sqrt(log(1/q))) for the chosen K. This example instead shows that a full proof needs a global allocation/cancellation over the heterogeneous null means or another method. Check the forced-zero rank formula, endpoints/ties and all inequalities before relying on this draft risk.
