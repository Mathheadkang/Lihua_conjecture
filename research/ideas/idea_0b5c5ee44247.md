## Sources-stage finding

A focused primary-source search found no result suitable as a black box for the complete common-loading Wasserstein continuity target. This is a scoped negative search result, not an exhaustive literature claim. No paper-sourced asset, paper-index section, or bibliography entry was added.

## Exact applicability gaps

1. Pierre Neuvial, *Asymptotic properties of false discovery rate controlling procedures under independence*, Electronic Journal of Statistics 2 (2008), 1065–1110, DOI [10.1214/08-EJS207](https://doi.org/10.1214/08-EJS207). Original checked: [arXiv:0803.2111v2 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0803.2111), title page and §3.1–3.3, especially Definition 3.7 and Conditions C.2–C.3 on printed p. 1074; Theorem 7.13 on printed p. 1100. Definition 3.7 requires a strict inequality between the CDF derivative and rejection-curve slope, and C.2–C.3 impose existence and uniqueness of an interior right crossing. Theorem 3.2 assumes C.1, including a positive limiting threshold and Hadamard differentiability; it does not establish these for the target here. Conditional empirical-process arguments for independent p-values do not supply continuity of the mixed-label FDP for an arbitrary Gaussian residual field. Competing largest non-strict crossings, score atoms, endpoint rank one, and vanishing positive rejection mass still need an original argument.

2. Helmut Finner, Thorsten Dickhaus and Markus Roters, *Dependency and false discovery rate: Asymptotics*, Annals of Statistics 35(4) (2007), 1432–1455, DOI [10.1214/009053607000000046](https://doi.org/10.1214/009053607000000046). Original checked: [arXiv:0710.3171v1 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0710.3171), §2, reprint pp. 4–8, especially the D-EX model, equation (2.3), and Theorem 2.1. The model has independent residual variables given the common disturbance and Dirac alternatives with p-value zero. Its LCP definition after (2.3) requires strict opposite signs on neighborhoods of the crossing; the theorem is restricted to the good disturbance values D_zeta. This cannot be silently applied to arbitrary residual dependence, general tagged means, or arbitrary W1 perturbations. The paper recognizes the difficulty of zero LCPs; its special exchangeable-model treatment is not the target's uniform microscopic-rank estimate.

## Consequences for proof planning

Any use of regular-root asymptotics must separately verify all hypotheses, rather than import its conclusion for arbitrary parameter laws. The present target still needs: an argument exploiting the atomless common Gaussian shift for the actual largest rank without assuming FDP monotonicity; preservation of the original common-cutoff true-tag numerator under perturbation; the conditional-marginal Stieltjes bound near rank zero; and finite rational canonical-label approximation. A positive fixed common loading is essential to the planned microscopic argument, and no limit as that loading tends to zero follows from these sources.
