Proof step suggested by refutation round 1: after localizing the variation to the original BH coordinate-threshold faces and controlling their intersections, assemble the actual largest-rank FDP jumps as signed increments before taking the expectation or any estimate. The mixed-label test in the native round-1 context pack has equal positive and negative face contributions and derivative zero. Replacing each jump by its positive part discards this cancellation and cannot establish the claimed exact derivative. This is a proof-route constraint, not a proof of the target; the general shrinking-neighborhood, unbounded-mark, and normalized-score remainder obligations remain unresolved.
