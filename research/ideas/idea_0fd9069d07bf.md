Round 2 suggests splitting a possible budget argument into two steps: identify a family where every actual selected-null jump is downward, so the original weighted positive charge equals the signed charge, then use orthant domination and a union bound to control its expectation. The checked zero-mean all-null family with nonpositive off-diagonal correlations has null-averaged budget at most e^2 q/(1-e q), uniformly in dimension for 0<q<=S(1); the regular-simplex family is included. This is an approach motivated by a restricted-family search obstruction, not a proved general black box, approved lemma, or new dependency. Mixed-sign correlations and heterogeneous means may lose both monotone jump direction and the needed orthant comparison; no general extension is established. The finite signed identity remains separately subject to verification.
