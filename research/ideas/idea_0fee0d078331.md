For the draft target, restrict to W([0,u])=1 (u>0, in particular u>=104). The following argument yields FDR_pop <= q*pi0 only for this support-restricted family. It was reconstructed from the definitions during round-seven search summary; it is not a proof-verification verdict for the unrestricted target.

1. For x>=u and 0<=mu<=u, both x-mu and x+mu are nonnegative. Since H_r(t)=H_1(t/r) is nondecreasing in r at every t>=0, F_r(x) is nondecreasing in r. Hence the admissible contact sets C_r are nested as r increases. Their smallest contacts x(r)=x_r in [u,infinity], including infinity when C_r is empty, are nonincreasing and Borel measurable.

2. Let h_d denote the chi_d density and, for finite x>=u, let A(r,x)=P(R<=r | Z_1>=x). Rotational independence gives its radial density
$$
f_x(r)=h_d(r)H_r(x)/Q(x).
$$
To justify stochastic monotonicity rather than assume it, take V uniform on (0,1) and S chi-squared with d-1 degrees of freedom independently, and set
$$
G_x=Q^{-1}(VQ(x)),\qquad R_x=\sqrt{G_x^2+S}.
$$
The variable G_x has the law of Z_1 conditional on Z_1>=x. Since x>0, G_x>=x and both G_x and R_x are nondecreasing in x under this coupling. Thus A(r,x) is nonincreasing in x. Define A(r,infinity)=f_infinity(r)=0. Also f_x(r)=0 when x>=r.

3. This order supplies a global transport bound with jumps and no-contact radii included. Fix 0<a<b and a partition. For successive endpoints s<t, monotonicity of x gives
$$
A(t,x(t))-A(s,x(s))
\ge A(t,x(s))-A(s,x(s))
=\int_s^t f_{x(s)}(z)\,dz.
$$
Summing telescopes to at most one. Along refining left-endpoint partitions, x(s) tends to x(z-) and equals x(z) outside the countable discontinuity set of the extended monotone function (compactify if needed). The density f_x(z) is continuous in finite x, and if x tends to infinity its value is eventually zero at each fixed z. Domination on [a,b] is explicit: if x(s)>z, the density is zero; otherwise x(s)<=z<=b gives Q(x(s))>=Q(b), so
$$
0\le f_{x(s)}(z)\le h_d(z)/Q(b).
$$
Dominated convergence therefore proves integral_a^b f_{x(z)}(z) dz <=1. Exhausting (0,infinity) yields the same bound for the full nonnegative integral.

4. The stipulated contact FDP now gives
$$
\operatorname{FDR}_{\mathrm{pop}}
=q\pi_0\int_0^\infty f_{x(r)}(r)\,dr
\le q\pi_0.
$$
The integrand is zero at infinite cutoff. This bounds the full expectation for W([0,u])=1 without differentiating the first contact or assuming regular contact branches. The argument fails to establish monotonicity of F_r(x) once means above x are allowed; it leaves arbitrary strong-mean mixtures and the full proposed theorem unresolved.
