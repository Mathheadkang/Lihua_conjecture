New proposed algebraic consequence to check AFTER the global particle certificate is proved; not an established lemma or a shortcut to the main bound. Write Pcal=P0+Gplus-Gminus and Lambda=cplus-cminus. For an exact active selector t, B'=[1/h-m(u_t)/t]/sigma. Interior active t>r has B'=0; on a boundary record t=r one has B'=M' a.e. If M'(r)>0 the active point must be the boundary; when M'=0 all active choices contribute zero record term. Thus the hybrid compact-limit construction should give integral(Gplus-Gminus)dπ = Lambda + E_X integral_a^1 w(r)*r*phi(M(r))*M'(r) dr, independent of the matching selectors. Separately, writing p0=h0/h and r f'=p0-f, the bulk potential integral is integral P0 dπ=E[chi(R)(f(R)-p0(Q(R)))]=-E[chi(R)*R*f'(R)]. The actual conditional rank law has density phi(M)M' on (a,1), so the f' terms cancel, leaving integral Pcal dπ = Lambda + J_a, where J_a=E[R*chi_a'(R)*D]>=0. Consequently certificate height kappa=Lambda+J_a-eta*qU*M2π. All limit/centering/density/endpoint arguments need proof; no exact selector need realize the limit. For cutoff families with a*||chi_a'|| bounded, J_a <= 2a||chi_a'|| E[D*1{0<R<=2a}], so the approved microscopic-rank bound makes J_a vanish uniformly over laws/dimensions at fixed q,sigma as a→0. This could control the global potential height by the level equation, rather than leaving it as a free constant. It still gives no q-uniform normal-flux bound, and passing stationarity/certificates through regularizer limits remains a separate obstacle.
