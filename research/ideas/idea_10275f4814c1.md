Scope: keep this approach OPEN and UNPROVED. This is a diagnostic for the unchanged original one-sided Gaussian BH target, not a new asset, completion root, pending proof plan, or proved comparison. Keep all finite N, original labels I0={i:theta_i<=0}, arbitrary means and unit-diagonal PSD covariance including singular models, q-dependent parameters, multiplicities, non-strict comparisons, and the actual largest qualifying BH rank. Restrict 0<q<1/2 so [q,2q] is an admissible level interval.

For each realized p-vector write f_t=V_t/(R_t vee 1). BH rejection sets are nested in t, so R_t>=R_q and V_t>=V_q for t>=q. Define
$$
J_q={1\over\log 2}\int_q^{2q}{R_q\over R_t\vee1}{dt\over t},\qquad
A_q={1\over\log2}\int_q^{2q}f_t{dt\over t}.
$$
When R_q=0 put J_q=0, as the displayed integrand already does. If R_q>0, f_t>=V_q/R_t=f_q R_q/R_t. If R_q=0 then f_q=0. Hence pathwise A_q>=f_q J_q and 0<=J_q<=1. Splitting according to J_q>=1/2 gives the exact deterministic comparison
$$
f_q\le2A_q+f_q\mathbf1_{\{J_q<1/2\}}.
$$
By boundedness and Tonelli,
$$
\operatorname{FDR}(q)\le {2\over\log2}\int_q^{2q}\operatorname{FDR}(t){dt\over t}
+\mathbb E[f_q\mathbf1_{\{J_q<1/2\}}].
$$
More generally, for every a in (0,1), replace 2 and 1/2 by a^{-1} and a. The unresolved sufficient Gaussian gate is a finite universal B and common q0>0 such that E[f_q 1{J_q<1/2}]<=Bq for every original admissible model and q<=q0. This would supply a dimension-free local-average comparison with an additive q term. It is sufficient, not asserted necessary and not proved here. BH nesting and marginal superuniformity alone do not establish it; all selected-rank masks and signed dilution remain relevant. No finite-bootstrap/sample-size approximation is used.

Bounded diagnostics: all-null models have f_t=1{R_t>0}, so their FDR is nondecreasing. Singular rank-one models T_i=theta_i+s_i G, s_i in {-1,1}, have FDR(q)<=P(|G|>=S^{-1}(q))=2q for q<1/2, because any selected true null forces its centered score above S^{-1}(q). Thus neither class can refute a coarse comparison with additive q. The established small-null-loading lower construction in plan_one_sided_order_fdp_entropy_mills_coherence_20261107, Progress P4a, is a concrete adversarial diagnostic to rederive at levels lambda q; its standard profile does not immediately create a cliff. Its source results are not a black-box proof of the present gate. A modified rare-alternative profile that preserves order-q sqrt(log(1/q)) at q but suppresses the log-level average would refute this approach; no such Gaussian family has been constructed here. Failure to find one does not establish this gate or exhaust the main project.
