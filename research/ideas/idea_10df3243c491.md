A concrete finite Gaussian family to CHECK independently. This is not yet a verified counterexample and says nothing against either original BH bound. The suspected failure is the auxiliary choice of very fine Mehler variance v=1-exp(-2/log(1/q)).

For integer L->infinity set q=exp(-L), eta=1/L, m=ceil(exp(L^3/10)), N=2m, d=m+1. Let G_0,G_1,...,G_m be independent standard Gaussians. The first m scores are true nulls
$$
T_i=\eta G_0+\sqrt{1-\eta^2}\,G_i,\quad\theta_i=0.
$$
The last m scores have loading -e_0 and positive means
$$
T_{m+j}=(1+\eta)u_j-G_0,\qquad
u_j=S^{-1}(qj/N),\quad j=1,\ldots,m.
$$
Thus every score has variance one, the full covariance is the explicit PSD Gram matrix, and the true-null set is exactly the first m physical tests. Use the target's original forced-rank masks and H_q, and evaluate its Mehler average at center x=0. Write Y_j~N(0,v) independently, v=1-exp(-2/L).

Suspected quantitative violation:
$$
E H_q(Y)\ \ge c\,q L^{3/2}
$$
for an absolute c>0 along all sufficiently large L, so its ratio to q sqrt(L) diverges. Here is a proposed elementary certificate, all of which still needs independent checking.

Select integer ranks k with L<=u_k<=L^{3/2}/3. They lie far inside 2,...,m-1 and their smallest value grows like exp(c L^3). Put Delta_k=u_k-u_{k+1}; these positive gaps decrease with k because S^{-1}(qt/N) is convex while its values are positive. For source point Y+u_k a_i (a true-null direction), the common score coordinate is Y_0+eta u_k. If other true nulls are absent at the relevant high thresholds, the false scores plus forced i have EXACT largest BH rank k whenever
$$
\Delta_k<Y_0\le(1+\eta)\Delta_{k-1}.
$$
For the later-rank inequalities use that (1+eta)u_{l-1}-u_l decreases in l. In particular
$$
J_k=((1+\eta/4)\Delta_k,(1+\eta/2)\Delta_k)
$$
is an interior interval of length eta Delta_k/4 where that false-only mask is k.

Control other true nulls UNIFORMLY, not by pretending independence of the full forced rank. At source Y+u_k a_i, each j!=i true score is
eta Y_0+eta^2 u_k+sqrt(1-eta^2)Y_j.
On J_k the common shift is at most b_L=1/L+1/(3 sqrt(L)) for large L. With probability tending to one over the other private coordinates, (i) none reaches threshold .8L: its union bound exponent is (1/10- .8^2/4+o(1))L^3<0; (ii) at every lower grid threshold u_l<.8L their count is <=l/4. For (ii), q S((u_l-b_L)/sqrt(v(1-eta^2)))/S(u_l)<=exp(-c L^2) uniformly u_l>=u_N, then apply a binomial upper tail and union over the N grid ranks. The smallest l in this lower-threshold range is already exp(L^3/10-O(L^2)).

At those lower thresholds, false count is at most
$$
(N/q)S((u_l+Y_0+\eta u_k)/(1+\eta))
\le l\,e^{-.19u_l}\le l/4
$$
eventually, since u_k>=L and u_l<.8L. Together with forced i this excludes all later qualifying ranks there. At higher thresholds the no-other-null event and the exact scalar mask exclude every l>k. Hence the FULL forced rank really is k on these events, with all original labels and multiplicities.

Finally integrate only Y_0 in J_k and Y_i in [0,1/u_k]. Then a_i.Y>=0, u_k a_i.Y<=2 eventually, and independence of these coordinates from the other private coordinates gives
$$
E[h_{ik}(Y)]\ge c\,k^{-1} e^{-u_k^2/2}
\frac{1}{\sqrt v\,u_k}\frac{\eta\Delta_k}{\sqrt v}.
$$
Mills gives e^{-u_k^2/2}/u_k>=sqrt(2pi)qk/N. Summing m tags and the selected ranks, with eta sum Delta_k asymptotic to sqrt(L)/3, yields c q sqrt(L)/v >=c' q L^{3/2}.

Check every uniform event, rounding endpoint, forced-rank equivalence and gap estimate before labeling survived. No large scientific workload is required: the family is specified analytically. If it works, it refutes only this overly fine-bandwidth auxiliary gate. Constant Mehler variance would remove this particular extra factor 1/v, and remains a distinct possible repair with the original BH main scope unchanged.
