Draft family-specific result, not an unrestricted upper bound. The exact finite quantile arrays below have FDR=O(q), including the prescribed weak common-gate schedule. The assertion is limited to the fast null-loading schedule and source-tail-resolving deterministic quantiles; no assertion is made for arbitrary finite source arrays or every r(q).

## Scoped model

Let B=B(q)>=2 be any integer schedule, alpha=q/B, k=ceil(alpha^{-2}), r=1/k, m=k^{k^3}, N=Bm. Write p=PhiBar, y=sqrt(2log(1/alpha)), eta=1/y. The one-sided source formulas (native paper lei2026_gaussian_bh, PDF page44, equations146-151) are
$$
b=rp^{-1}(r^2),\quad W_r(a)=(1-\alpha e^{a^2/2})p(a/r)/\alpha,\quad b<=a<=y,
$$
tau=W_r(b), pi_0=1-r-tau, p(c_0)=alpha(r+tau)/(1-alpha pi_0).
The primary mean is c_0+b and its source loading is -1; these are the ONE-sided formulas. Each block has mr primary copies and ceil(m tau) secondary means a_h/r+a_h, where a_h are midpoint quantiles of the probability measure with survival W_r(a)/tau. Its remaining coordinates are true nulls with any specified deterministic means theta_i<=0.

With independent standard W,Z_1,...,Z_B and null residuals, replace the source nonnull loading -Z_j by -Y_j, Y_j=epsilon Z_j+sqrt(1-epsilon^2)W. Here epsilon=epsilon(q) may be any number in [0,1]. Null coordinates remain theta_i+rZ_j+sqrt(1-r^2)E_i. All marginals have unit variance and the full vector is exactly jointly Gaussian. Nonnull means remain positive. The concrete round1 slot1 candidate uses B=ceil(q^{-1}), epsilon=1/sqrt(log(1/q)), and null means alternating between0 and -q.

## Exact finite nonnull cap

On the factor box H={|W|,max_j|Z_j|<=2y}, suppose G={Y_*=min_jY_j<=y-2eta}. Set A=eta+(Y_*)_+, C=A/r and d=(A+rY_*)/(1+r). Since r~alpha^2, ry^2->0 and b<<eta, uniformly b<eta/2<=d<=A<=y-eta. In the minimizing block, a secondary mean a/r+a-Y_* exceeds C exactly for a>=d.

The empirical secondary tail proportion is at least W_r(d)-1/m, by the deterministic midpoint-quantile discrepancy and ceil(m tau)/m>=tau. Since d<=y-eta, 1-alpha exp(d^2/2)>=1-exp(-1/2)>0 for small q. The negative logarithm of W_r(y-eta) is O(y^2/r^2)=O(k^2 log(1/alpha)), while log m=k^3 log k, so mW_r(y-eta)->infinity and this empirical proportion is at least W_r(d)/2.

Put h=(A-Y_*)/(1+r), so d/r=C-h, h>=eta/(1+r) and h/C=O(ry^2)=o(1). Integrating the Gaussian hazard lambda(t)>=t gives
$$
\frac{\alpha W_r(d)}{2p(C)}
\geq\frac{1-e^{-1/2}}2\,\frac{p(C-h)}{p(C)}
\geq\frac{1-e^{-1/2}}2\exp\{\eta^2/(4r)\}>1.
$$
This is the GLOBAL normalized crossing supplied by one block, since q/B=alpha. Thus ordinary BH, and every null leave-one-out version of it, has Gaussian cutoff at most C. No conditional-null empirical CDF concentration or inherited scalar source crossing is assumed.

The exceptional events cost o(q):
P(G^c)<=p(y-2/y)=O(alpha/y), and
P(H^c)<=2(B+1)p(2y)=O((B+1)alpha^4/y)=o(q).

## Pair-average Gaussian envelope

For a null in block j choose any other block l. Let V_j=(Y_j+Y_l)/2, v^2=Var(V_j)=1-epsilon^2/2 and U_j=V_j/v. Then U_j is standard normal and
$$
\operatorname{Corr}(Z_j,U_j)=\frac{\epsilon}{2\sqrt{1-\epsilon^2/2}}\leq1/\sqrt2.
$$
The cap satisfies rC<=eta+(V_j)_+<=1+|U_j|. This uniformly nonparallel pair is why a vanishing own-block epsilon is not required for the argument.

For every theta_i<=0, its conditional null tail is at most the zero-mean conditional tail. On H, uniform Mills bounds for c>=p^{-1}(q), a=rc<=y and |Z_j|<=2y give
$$
P(T_i>=c\mid W,Z)/p(c)\leq2\exp(-a^2/2+aZ_j).
$$
The Mills prefactor error vanishes and the exponent error is O(r^2y^2). These conclusions are uniform in B>=2 because alpha<=q/2 and r~alpha^2.

For standard Gaussian pairs (Z,U) of correlation rho in [0,1/sqrt2], the expectation
E sup_{0<=a<=1+|U|} exp(-a^2/2+a|Z|) is bounded by an absolute finite K. One direct check uses x=|Z|,t=|U| and bounds the supremum by e^x K_0, where K_0=e^{x^2/2} on x<=t and K_0=e^{-t^2/2+xt} on x>t. Multiplying by the folded Gaussian density leaves a strictly negative quadratic exponent uniformly in rho: in the first region it is -(t-rho x)^2/[2(1-rho^2)], with t>=x; in the second the precision determinant is (1-rho)/(1+rho)>0, uniformly away from zero on the compact correlation range. The factor e^x is integrable against both uniform Gaussian bounds.

Conditional on all common factors, null p-values are independent. The exact global BH leave-one-null identity bounds each expected FDP contribution by q/N times its conditional null CDF-to-threshold ratio. The nonnull cap remains valid after that replacement. Summing the above envelope and integrating yields E[FDP 1_{H intersect G}]<=2Kq. The exceptional probabilities are o(q), so FDR<=2Kq+o(q), uniformly in every epsilon(q) in [0,1] for this specified quantile family. In particular its FDR/[q sqrt(log(1/q))] tends to zero.

This exclusion relies on at least two blocks and the common retained source mean ladder with sufficiently accurate finite tails. It does not prove the sharp one-sided conjecture, exclude all q-dependent null loadings, or replace the global cutoff by separate local FDRs.
