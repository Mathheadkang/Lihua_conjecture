Side-chat proposal of 11 November 2026, built on the capped conditional-PRDS lemma of idea_420827cf1b7e. It is a candidate new plan for the unchanged one-sided target, not a new completion root. Everything below is unverified.

## Definition

A carrier for a model $(\theta,\Sigma,I_0)$ is a decomposition
$$
\Sigma=AA^{\mathsf T}+\Gamma,\qquad \Gamma\succeq0,\qquad \Gamma_{ij}\ge0\ \text{ for all } i\in I_0 \text{ and all } j .
$$
In a Gram representation $\Sigma_{jk}=\langle u_j,u_k\rangle$ this is a symmetric $0\preceq P\preceq I$ with $\langle u_i,(I-P)u_j\rangle\ge0$ for every null $i$ and every $j$. The choice $P=0$ is allowed exactly when the model is PRDS on the nulls; $P=I$ always works and gives nothing. The carrier holds the dependence that positive-dependence arguments cannot treat. What matters afterwards is the geometry of the null rows of $A$, that is their norms $r_i=\|A_i\|$ and their directions. The rank of $\Sigma$, the factor dimension of the alternatives, and a private-noise floor do not enter: $\Gamma$ may be singular.

## Consequences expected from idea_420827cf1b7e

(B1) One carrier direction on the nulls. If $A_i=a_iv$ for all $i\in I_0$, with arbitrary rows for the alternatives, the conditional bound is the display of Lei's Lemma 4.1 with conditional PRDS on the nulls in place of conditional independence. The proof of the [one-sided common-factor upper bound](../assets/lei2026_one_sided_common_factor_upper.md) then uses only that display, $\mathrm{FDP}\le1$, and the per-null integral of [Lei integrated conditional Gaussian tail-ratio bounds](../assets/lei2026_integrated_conditional_tail_ratios.md). Expected statement:
$$
\mathrm{FDR}\le q\Big\{2+\pi_0\Big(\tfrac12+\frac{u_q}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\Big)\Big\}
$$
for every $\Sigma=aa^{\mathsf T}+\Gamma$ with $\Gamma$ as above. This contains Lei's class, every model that is PRDS on the nulls (with a non-optimal constant), and mixtures of the two: a weak common null factor, opposite-sign alternatives, and any singular positive dependence among nulls and between nulls and alternatives. To check while writing it: the two sign groups and their $2q$ remainder, and composite nulls through the CDF domination already recorded in the integrated tail-ratio asset.

(B2) Several carrier directions. The [directional-entropy bound](../assets/directional_entropy_one_sided_fdr_bound.md) and the radial bounds should extend with conditional independence replaced by the same hypothesis on $\Gamma$, because they use the same conditional display.

(B3) Dense rejections. If $\mathbb P(R<r_0)\le\eta$ for a deterministic $r_0$, then
$$
\mathrm{FDR}\le\eta+q\pi_0\max_{i\in I_0}G(r_i,q,c_{r_0}),
$$
with $G$ the capped envelope of idea_420827cf1b7e. For $r_0=Nq^{C}$ one has $c_{r_0}\le\sqrt{C+1}\,u_q\{1+o(1)\}$. With null loadings bounded by $r<1$ this gives order $q\sqrt{\log(1/q)}$ with a constant depending on $C$ and $r/\sqrt{1-r^2}$ only, for carriers of any dimension and any geometry. With $r=1$ only the logarithmic value $1+C\log(1/q)$ remains.

(B4) Weak loading without a cap. With $r_0=1$ the same bound reads $\mathrm{FDR}\le q\pi_0\{2+\bar r\sqrt{2\log(N/q)}/(s\sqrt{2\pi})\}$ for an average loading $\bar r$. It is dimension-free in the carrier and equals the target order exactly when $\bar r\lesssim\sqrt{\log(1/q)/\log(N/q)}$, which is the loading scale of the lower-bound family.

(B5) Exclusion. If the carrier has independent coordinates each with its own ladder of alternatives, every ladder that is not being cut is rejected in full, so the rank floor is at least the largest inactive ladder and the cap level is at most the minimum of the ladders' own caps. Several independent steering directions therefore do not add. The min structure found for equal synchronized blocks in idea_11028fd850f5 becomes an inequality for unequal blocks, unequal ladders and PRDS residuals.

## Regime map

- Dense steering regime: the rank is fixed by the carrier up to a bounded factor in $\log$-rank and null loadings stay below one. Closed by (B3) once a carrier exists. This is the regime of every known inflation mechanism.
- Sparse regime: small ranks driven by the nulls themselves. The lemma loses here, because the supremum is inside the null sum. The all-null case is the Simes problem, see [the draft Simes bound](../assets/gaussian_simes_one_sided_uniform_linear_bound.md). The compound calibration handles it under conditional independence only.
- Carrier existence: an exactly PRDS residual can force a large carrier. A null with one negatively correlated partner must be fully inside the carrier unless something else absorbs that correlation. This is the reason for the defect accounting in the companion idea.

## Proposed order of work

1. Native verification of the lemma in idea_420827cf1b7e.
2. A draft asset for (B1), verified against the proof of Lei's Theorem 4.10 with the new conditional display. This would be the first sharp-order statement of the project that allows singular residual dependence.
3. A draft asset for (B3) with explicit constants.
4. Compute minimal carriers. Finding $\Sigma'=AA^{\mathsf T}$ with $\Sigma'\succeq0$, $\Sigma-\Sigma'\succeq0$ and entrywise constraints on the null rows is a semidefinite feasibility problem. Run it on the approved counterexample families and on small random models, and record how large the null block of $\Sigma'$ has to be and what the capped envelope then gives.
5. Only then decide between the defect route and a null-side route for the sparse regime.

## Failure criterion

Drop the plan if step 4 shows that models with a visibly harmless FDR need carriers whose capped envelope is of order $\log(1/q)$ or larger even after the defect allowance, with no structural reason. Keep (B1) and (B3) as supporting results in that case.
