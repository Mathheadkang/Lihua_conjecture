## Admissible finite model

Let X,Y be independent standard normals. Take N=3, theta=(0,0,1), and scores T_1=X, T_2=Y, T_3=1+(X-Y)/sqrt(2). The first two original labels are null and the third is an alternative. Every marginal variance is one, and the covariance is the PSD Gram matrix

$$
\Sigma=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&1/\sqrt2\\0&1&-1/\sqrt2\\1/\sqrt2&-1/\sqrt2&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Thus the original null covariance is zero. Let H=sigma(T_3), which is also the sigma-field of the complete alternative p-value because the Gaussian survival transform is invertible. Writing W=(X+Y)/sqrt(2) and y=T_3-1, the final conditional null representation is

$$
(T_1,T_2)\mid y=((W+y)/\sqrt2,(W-y)/\sqrt2),\qquad W\sim N(0,1).
$$

The residual covariance is

$$
Q=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its positive off-diagonal entry violates the helper's Gaussian sufficient condition after the full alternative has been revealed.

## Marginal calibration remains K=1 near y=0

Set z=20 and q=barPhi(z). For any 0<s<=q, let z_s=barPhi^{-1}(s)>=20. The two final conditional null CDFs are barPhi(sqrt(2)z_s-y) and barPhi(sqrt(2)z_s+y). If |y|<=20(sqrt(2)-1), both are <=s simultaneously for every such s. Hence the helper's prescribed K=max{1,sup_s sum_i F_i,H(s)/(3s)} equals exactly one throughout this interval.

## Explicit exponential-count failure

At y=0, the two conditional null scores coincide, so C(q)=2B where B is Bernoulli with success probability f=barPhi(20sqrt(2)). Choose lambda>0 by exp(lambda)=2/(3q). Then

$$
\mathbb E[e^{\lambda C(q)}\mid y=0]=1+f\left(\frac{4}{9q^2}-1\right)>\frac{4f}{9q^2}.
$$

Elementary Gaussian Mills bounds give, for z=20,

$$
\frac{\overline\Phi(\sqrt2 z)}{\overline\Phi(z)^2}\ge\frac{2\sqrt\pi\,z^3}{2z^2+1}>\frac{16000}{801}.
$$

Indeed, use barPhi(x)>=phi(x)x/(x^2+1) in the numerator and barPhi(z)<=phi(z)/z in the denominator. Consequently the conditional count moment is greater than 64000/7209>8>e^2. But the proposed sub-Poisson expression with N=3 and K=1 is

$$
\exp\{3q(e^\lambda-1)\}=\exp(2-3q)<e^2.
$$

This is not only a failure at one conditioning value of probability zero. The conditional moment is continuous in y: away from the finitely many W threshold points its integrand is continuous in y and is bounded by exp(2lambda). The strict inequality therefore persists for all |y|<epsilon for some epsilon>0. Shrink epsilon below 20(sqrt(2)-1), retaining K=1. Since y is standard normal, that conditioning event has positive probability.

## Meaning and limits

Nonpositive unconditional null covariance and conditionally superuniform null marginals do not suffice for the new count tool when alternatives are correlated with nulls: final conditioning can create positive residual dependence and violate the exponential-count hypothesis, even with prescribed K=1. The approved helper is unaffected because its residual sign assumption is expressly imposed after the full final conditioning. No labels, p-values, or actual BH ranks are modified in this diagnostic.

This is not a counterexample to the unrestricted main order assertion. The admissible model has N=3 and two nulls, and the marginal harmonic bound gives FDR<=q(2/3)H_3=11q/9 for every q, so its normalized ratio tends to zero. No candidate file is warranted.
