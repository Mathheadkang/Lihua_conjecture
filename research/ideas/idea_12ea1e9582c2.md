Candidate supporting reduction derived by root during the nonsharp phase; requires fresh independent checking and is not a proof of the main order target. Let S=barPhi, phi=-S', 0<q<=S(1), U=S^{-1}(q)>=1. In any original finite one-sided Gaussian model let I_-={i:theta_i<=-1}, with ordinary BH count R and p_i=S(T_i). No assumptions on the other means or dependence beyond the original model are needed. On each realization, including R=0, self-consistency and R<=N give
sum_{i in I_-} 1{i rejected}/(R vee 1) <= (q/N) sum_{i in I_-} 1{p_i<=q}/p_i.
For each i in I_-, write delta=-theta_i>=1. Its marginal expectation is exactly
E[1{p_i<=q}/p_i]=integral_U^infinity phi(t+delta)/S(t) dt
<= exp(-1/2) integral_U^infinity (t+1/t) exp(-t) dt
<= exp(-U-1/2)(U+1+1/U)
<= exp(-U-1/2)(U+2) <= 3 exp(-3/2).
Here phi(t+delta)<=phi(t+1)=phi(t)exp(-t-1/2), and the standard Mills bound phi(t)/S(t)<=t+1/t for t>0 can be reproved by integration by parts: S(t)=phi(t)/t-integral_t^infinity phi(x)/x^2 dx >=phi(t)/t-S(t)/t^2. The last function (U+2)exp(-U-1/2) decreases for U>=1. Thus the contribution of ALL theta_i<=-1 to FDR is at most q (|I_-|/N) exp(-U-1/2)(U+2) <=3exp(-3/2) q, uniformly over N, all means, all unit-diagonal PSD covariances including singular, and parameters depending on q. There is no log N or smallest-eigenvalue loss. All integrals are finite by the displayed exponential bound; no independence or rank monotonicity is used. This only removes the strongly negative null contribution from the analytic objective. It does NOT allow deleting those coordinates from BH or relabeling them as false nulls. The remaining contribution from -1<theta_i<=0 is still an unresolved uniform problem for the ORIGINAL BH procedure with all coordinates retained. A future main proof may rederive this elementary margin estimate inline; a stand-alone asset would require its own native search/proof/verification. Do not cite it as native-approved.
