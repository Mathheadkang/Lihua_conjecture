Global-null special case of gaussian_bh_rare_direction_cap. Take any unit-diagonal PSD Sigma, all theta_i<=0, and the unit-loading representation T=theta+BZ with B=Sigma^{1/2}, so r_i=1 and w_i^T w_k=Sigma_ik. Then mu_i=#{k: Sigma_ik>=cos(kappa_2/u)}/m. If every coordinate has fewer than q^{C_2} m partners with correlation >= cos(kappa_2/u) (for example all off-diagonal Sigma_ik<cos(kappa_2/u) and m>q^{-C_2}), every null is rare. With no alternatives V=R, so the left side equals P(R>0), the size of the Simes test. Hence the cap implies: Simes size <= A_2 q(1+u) for every such Gaussian vector, negative correlations included. Consequences for planning: (1) competition through alternatives of idle blocks is unavailable here, so it cannot be the whole mechanism of a proof; the competition must come from the nulls themselves. (2) Per-null conditional bounds (Lei ratio) lose log m in the window Y_i>u when r_i is near 1, and the library only has the Simes inequality under positive Gaussian dependence (lib_chi2024_positive_gaussian_simes). A proof of the cap therefore needs a Gaussian Simes bound under negative dependence of order q(1+u), which no approved node or library result supplies.
