Concrete proposed repair of the derivative-transfer obstruction; not proved/approved. Work at sigma>0, private standard deviation bounded below by s>0, and a smooth rank cutoff chi_a which is zero on [0,a], one on [2a,1], nondecreasing and C1. Optimize the actual cutoff-weighted full FDP, not a finite-copy derivative, BEFORE removing s,a. Conditional on a background x independent of Z and private noise, let a tagged score kernel be a mixture of Gaussians with centers A and standard deviations c>=s. A deterministic bounded lawful mean velocity b, chosen before x/noise, gives centers A+epsilon b. Let h,h_b,h_0,h_0b be total/velocity-weighted/true/true-velocity-weighted mixture densities. These kernels are smooth and h>0. If Q(r) is the upper score quantile, v(r)=true tail at Q(r), f=v/r, candidate fixed-r derivatives are Qdot=h_b(Q)/h(Q) and fdot=[h_0b(Q)-h_0(Q)h_b(Q)/h(Q)]/r. Uniform score coupling gives |Q_epsilon-Q|<=epsilon K; |v_epsilon-v|<=2epsilon K/(s sqrt(2pi)). These estimates hold for arbitrary center/tag distributions, including atomic means, because the positive private variance smooths scores.

Set B(r)=[S^{-1}(qr)-Q(r)]/sigma, M(r)=min_{t in [r,1]} B(t). Q(1)=-infinity and B(1)=+infinity; every suffix minimum for r<1 is attained at a compact subset below1. The actual largest rank satisfies P_Z(R>=r)=S(M(r)) and actual FDP=f(R). For fixed bounded b the RIGHT envelope derivative should be Mdot(r)=min_{t in argmin_[r,1] B}[-Qdot(t)/sigma], retaining all competing minima; no unique or nondegenerate fold is assumed. For a simultaneous q_epsilon=q(1+epsilon c), replace the numerator by -c m(S^{-1}(qt))-Qdot(t), where m=S/phi. Retain the minimum over active suffix minimizers, not an arbitrary old branch.

The proposed COMPLETE cutoff-FDR derivative is
E_x[ E_Z[chi_a(R) fdot(R)] - integral_a^1 (chi_a f)'(r) phi(M(r)) Mdot(r) dr ].
This follows by splitting the change of f at the perturbed rank from the change of the baseline payoff under the perturbed rank, then using the suffix-minimum identity and Danskin-type directional differentiation. It retains smooth branches and birth/switch/tie contributions in one expression. For mean transport alone a global difference-quotient bound is 2K/(a s sqrt(2pi)) + K(1+log(1/a))/(sigma sqrt(2pi)), since |f'|<=1/r and TV(chi_a)<=1. This should justify all background expectations without nondegenerate critical-point counting. Prove the quantile algebra, minima attainment, local uniform implicit derivatives, rank convergence outside countably many Z jump levels, and domination; these remain theorem obligations.

Bridge to cross-dimension maximizers: first prove uniform random-submodel compression and marked-array compactification (idea_4de18419c49d). Add a private-noise smoothing of the base array: off-diagonal residual correlations are scaled by 1-s^2/(1-sigma^2), diagonal remains1. Its image is closed on moment-bounded compact sets. The cutoff-FDR functional should also be continuous there: strengthen sampling comparison to normalized rank as well as full FDP, then multiply by Lipschitz chi_a. Thus a quadratic-penalized cutoff maximum can be attained. The exact all-d value limit is recovered as s->0 by finite-model covariance continuity and as a->0 by the approved uniform micro-FDP modulus; this is VALUE recovery, not passage of old derivatives. q-normalized/log-penalized maxima can be treated on the coercive q interval. Prove any final upper bound at these regularized maxima uniformly in the regularizers, then pass VALUES only.

Representation gate: a deterministic tagged unit-ball directing measure with private diagonal correction follows from an appropriately marked Dovbysh-Sudakov representation, which must be sourced and verified. Candidate reduction from the known unmarked theorem: encode the Borel mark (theta,ell,b) injectively as f(mark) in [0,1], add it to the diagonal of the PSD array, apply the general DS mixture representation, and recover each mark from ||h||^2+a-1. Original off-diagonal entries remain <=1, which forces ||h||<=1 almost surely (two iid points in a sufficiently small ball around a point of norm>1 would have inner product>1). Affinity of expected finite/cutoff FDP and the moment lets one choose a deterministic directing component at a maximum. The DS theorem must NOT be reproved or silently assumed: primary verified starting reference is Panchenko, arXiv:0905.1524v1, Proposition1 on p2, https://arxiv.org/pdf/0905.1524 . Marked recovery, conditional Gaussian equivalence and component selection still need full checks.

This framework does NOT yet construct the special paired Mills transport, derive linear mean complementarity at nonsmooth multiple minima, or pay the q-scaled zero-null flux. The min envelope makes responses nonlinear in b at ties, so opposite signs need not cancel. It offers a well-defined exact signed variational calculation on a dense regularized class, rather than assuming the old finite-PD derivative holds at an arbitrary limiting law.
