Round 1 near-misses for gaussian_bh_two_sided_exact_pivotal_identity suggest separating the deterministic forced-zero BH rank identity from Gaussian integration. For perfectly tied all-null clusters, the small forced-zero threshold occurs only where its numerator is zero; increasing cluster size therefore does not create a large pivotal contribution. A proof route should establish the deterministic pointwise rank identity for arbitrary p-value vectors, including ties and R=0, before deriving the Gaussian residual baseline and branch representation. This is an approach suggested by the round summaries, not a proof or a verification of the target, and does not bound the pivotal excess in general.
