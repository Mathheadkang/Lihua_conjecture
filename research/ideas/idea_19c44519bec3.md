UNCHECKED TEST CASE; neither a counterexample nor a failed candidate. Take N=d=2, v_1=e_1, v_2=rho e_1+sqrt(1-rho^2)e_2 with 0<rho<1, original means (0,mu), mu>0, and the ordinary BH rule. Let u_1=normal-survival-inverse(q/2)>u_2=U, c_j=1/h(u_j), and H=h(U). The only true null is X=G_1 and the alternative is Y=mu+rho G_1+sqrt(1-rho^2)G_2. Its exact true-discovery cells are A_1={X>=u_1,Y<u_2}, f=1, and A_2={X>=u_2,Y>=u_2}, f=1/2. Thus B=e_1[c_1 1_A1+(c_2/2)1_A2], F=P(A_1)+(1/2)P(A_2)>0, and b takes c_1 e_1 or c_2 e_1 under Q. All model hypotheses are checked for finite mu,rho and arbitrarily small positive q.

For t<1 let A_j(t,z) be the actual conditional remaining-Gaussian probabilities of those cells, with residual covariance (1-t) times the original covariance, and pi_2=(A_2/2)/(A_1+A_2/2). The exact heat posterior is a=e_1[c_1+(c_2-c_1)pi_2], so the full trace is (c_2-c_1)pi_2(1-pi_2) partial_{z_1} log(A_2/A_1). Its global expectation integrated in time, including its sign, remains unchecked for rho>0. A local positive false-null face must not be substituted for this full quantity. The rho=0 family has been fully checked separately: the trace is pointwise negative, for every mu and every nonpositive null mean.

Some exact rate constraints are checked. Put p=Q(A_1)=P(A_1)/F and delta=1-H c_1 in (0,1). Since a_t is the Q-path posterior martingale of b, Jensen gives
  delta(2-delta)p <= 1-x <= 2 delta p-delta^2 p^2.
The lower bound uses x<=H^2 E_Q||b||^2; the upper bound uses x>=H^2||E_Q b||^2. The same martingale quadratic-variation identity gives |M|<=F(c_2-c_1)sqrt(p(1-p)), by applying Cauchy--Schwarz to K_11. For fixed q and rho, as mu goes to infinity, p goes to zero, F goes to q/2, D goes to log(1/q), x goes to 1 and M goes to zero. These facts do not determine the ratio M_+/[q sqrt(1+D)(1-x)]. Its actual signed rate must be computed; the coarse sqrt(p) trace bound cannot prove divergence. A universal-constant refutation would require a legal family with this ratio unbounded and with q arbitrarily small, possibly choosing mu and rho as functions of q. No long numerical heat-semigroup integration was performed.
