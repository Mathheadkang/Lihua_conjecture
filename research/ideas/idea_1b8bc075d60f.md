Primary-source check for the proposed clone-invariant approximate-maximizer route: I. Ekeland, On the Variational Principle, Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications47(2)(1974),324–353, DOI10.1016/0022-247X(74)90025-0. Author PDF https://www.ceremade.dauphine.fr/~ekeland/Articles/OnTheVariationalPrinciple.pdf , §1 Theorem1.1 printedp324; metadata cross-checked on publisher https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0022247X74900250 . The theorem requires a complete metric domain and a proper lower-semicontinuous objective bounded below; its comparison slope is the original objective gap divided by the chosen distance radius. For maximizing FDR it must be applied to negative FDR, requiring upper semicontinuity on an explicitly complete clone-invariant model space. The theorem does not keep the comparison point finite-dimensional/atomic/PD, and does not supply derivatives of an upper-semicontinuous envelope. Those are unsolved project hypotheses, not consequences of the source. No theorem imported or assumed as a main dependency; use only after proving the domain and variation compatibility obligations recorded in idea_fb51d8b6e225.
