Route 6 of the researcher-authorized parallel round of 12 November 2026. Computer-assisted triage, not a proof route by itself.

**Core idea.** After the approved compactification, low-rank worst cases are finite-dimensional optimization problems over directions, means and masses. Use numerical search (and, for any decisive claim, interval-arithmetic certification as in E. Dobriban, arXiv:2607.12208, which certified a two-sided counterexample) to:
1. test the rare-cluster cap L2 of Routes 1 and 5 on families with many small clusters at angle about 1/U (sign-cubes, simplex frames with negative null-alternative correlations), tracking FDR/(q log(1/q)) and FDR/(q sqrt(log(1/q))) as q decreases;
2. check whether near-maximizers look ultrametric (Route 3);
3. fit candidate dual certificates or potentials.

**Use in the loop.** Realize as counterexample searches on the lemmas that Routes 1, 4 and 5 introduce (the loop already searches every result), with these families as the researcher's priority test cases.

**Value.** A verified counterfamily to L2 would kill Routes 1 and 5 in their present form within days; survival is evidence, never proof.
