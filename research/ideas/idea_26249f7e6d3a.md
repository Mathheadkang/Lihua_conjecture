Primary source checked: Suhas Vijaykumar, A scale-free density bound for Gaussian maxima, arxiv2605.29066v2, https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29066v2 , Proposition3 and Corollary4 (§2.1), Proposition5 (§2.2). Scale-free here means no minimum marginal-variance condition; it is not dimension-free. For centered Gaussian maxima the density at positive t is bounded by 4 log(p)/t; the unsigned bound retains log(2p)/(sigma_max vee t). The geometric refinement counts effective coordinates, which may still be unbounded. Thus this source may aid a fixed-model perturbation estimate but supplies neither universal q-scaled BH face cost nor a uniform cloning approximation. In particular arbitrary original nonzero means and actual BH rank unions cannot be replaced by a centered maximum without a proved reduction. No source dependency or main proof step was added.
