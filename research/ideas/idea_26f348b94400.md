Route 4 of the researcher-authorized parallel round of 12 November 2026. UNPROVED draft direction.

**Core idea.** Lei (2026) notes one-sided inflation needs negative correlations between nulls and non-nulls; with nonnegative null-null correlations, Su's FDR-linking theorem plus a linear Simes bound gives q(1 + log(1/q)), and a project note records the remaining gap there as exactly sqrt(log) versus log. Split by correlation sign: (a) sharpen the linking integral using Gaussian tails to lose only sqrt(log(1/q)); (b) handle negative null-null correlations separately.

**External inputs.** W. Su, The FDR-Linking Theorem, arXiv:1812.08965 (PRDN level alpha(1 + log(1/alpha)), stated optimal under PRDN); draft gaussian_simes_one_sided_uniform_linear_bound (no published C alpha Simes bound for arbitrary Gaussian correlation was found in the 12 Nov 2026 search); Chi-Ramdas-Wang (approved library node lib_chi2024_positive_gaussian_simes) for the positive side; R. F. Barber and R. J. Samworth, FDR control with compound p-values, arXiv:2507.21465 (1.93 alpha under independence) as a model for averaged validity. Literature checked at abstract level only.

**Gaps.** Optimality of Su's level under PRDN means (a) must use Gaussian structure, not PRDN alone. Part (b) has no current tool.

**Milestones.** q log(1/q) on the class with nonnegative null-null correlations (needs the Simes bound); then sqrt(log) there; then general signs.

**Saturation check.** The refined linking weight must be an explicit Simes-type probability, never the FDR itself.
