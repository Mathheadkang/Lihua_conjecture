Scope: checked single-factor profile obstruction for gaussian_bh_one_sided_universal_order_upper, not a proof for arbitrary covariance. Candidate cand_gaussian_bh_one_sided_universal_order_upper_r1_s1_exponential_survival gives an exact finite family with q_j=exp(-j^2), r_j=1/j, m_j=ceil(exp(j^2)), N_j=ceil(exp(3j^2)), zero-mean nulls r_j Z+sqrt(1-r_j^2)epsilon_i, and positive-mean alternatives mu_j,l-Z. Its actual alternative survival is W_j(a)=N_j^{-1} min(m_j,floor(m_j exp(-(a^2-1)/(4r_j^2))+1/2)) for a>=1, generated by a_j,l=sqrt(1+4r_j^2 log(m_j/(l-1/2))) and mu_j,l=(1+r_j)a_j,l/r_j. The alternative fraction is asymptotic to exp(-2/r_j^2). This leaves the bounded fixed-profile regime of idea_97f590b891a2, while preserving the full discrete survival and original largest BH crossing.

The obstruction is independent of the alternative profile. Let 0<r<=1/2, s=sqrt(1-r^2), b=1/s, and condition on Z=z. The null density f_z of N(rz,s^2) obeys exactly

f_z(x)/phi(x)=b exp(z^2/2-r^2(x-z/r)^2/(2s^2))<=b exp(z^2/2).

Hence every upper-tail CDF ratio F_0,z(t)/t is at most b exp(z^2/2); for any number of these nulls M_q(z)<=pi_0 b exp(z^2/2) and K_q(z)<=b exp(z^2/2). All null residuals are independent given Z; arbitrary alternative coordinates that are deterministic functions of Z are also conditionally independent (their laws are degenerate). The approved gaussian_bh_conditional_compound_calibration therefore applies with the original rejection count, giving FDR<=E min(1,A exp(Z^2/2)), A=2qb. This argument permits arbitrary alternative means, multiplicities, exponentially r-dependent profiles and tangential competing crossings within this model class. No approximation of the selection event is involved.

For A<1, put x=sqrt(2 log(1/A)). Exact integration gives E min(1,A exp(Z^2/2))=sqrt(2/pi) A x+2 S(x)<=sqrt(2/pi)A(x+1/x). For the displayed candidate j>=2, b<=2/sqrt(3) and j<=x<=sqrt(2)j, so FDR<=4q_j j=4q_j sqrt(log(1/q_j)). Thus its NOT check fails. The same envelope supplies a profile-uniform O(q sqrt(log(1/q))) bound whenever null loadings are bounded away from one in this shared-factor, conditionally independent-residual class.

Remaining boundary: this does not bound an arbitrary multi-factor null geometry, correlated residuals given Z, or the r-to-one noise-floor degeneration uniformly. It does not prove the unrestricted target. Merely choosing more extreme rare-alternative weights in the small-null-loading source family cannot evade this exact envelope; further search needs a different null geometry or the unresolved noise-floor boundary.
