This is an exactly specified UNFINISHED asymptotic test family, not a counterexample and not an asserted improvement of the source lower bound. It preserves finitely many parameter types but independent residual noise in a large null block; the finite-type duplicate-p-value obstruction therefore does not apply. It addresses the full unrestricted q-limit rather than a finite-q numerical comparison.

Put L=log(1/q), take q<e^{-10}, r=L^{-1}, u=PhiBar^{-1}(q/2), rho=1/sqrt(2), a_1=u/3 and a_2=2u/3. Write p(c)=2 PhiBar(c). Let N=ceil(exp(L^4)); this is a finite integer at each q and no array of this size needs to be allocated. Define weights w_0=r, w_l=p(a_l/r)/(4q) for l=1,2, counts n_l=floor(N w_l), and n_null=N-n_0-n_1-n_2. The weights are positive, their sum is less than one for this small-q range, and n_l diverges since log N=L^4 dominates the secondary log inverse weights of order L^3. Set deterministic alternative means mu_0=p^{-1}(qr)+1 and mu_l=a_l/r+a_l for l=1,2. These are three non-null types, not a random mean mixture. The secondary means and masses are a coarse, deliberately finite discretization inspired by the source construction; their optimality is not presumed.

Rank-two version: Z=(Z_1,Z_2) is standard normal; the null coordinates are r Z_1+sqrt(1-r^2) epsilon_i, with mutually independent standard residuals independent of Z. The three non-null types have zero residual noise, means mu_l, and unit loading vectors
v_0=(-rho,rho), v_1=(rho,rho), v_2=(-rho,-rho), with the indicated n_l copies. Thus each coordinate is theta_i+A_i Z+sqrt(1-||A_i||^2) epsilon_i and Sigma=AA^T+diag(1-||A_i||^2) is positive semidefinite with unit diagonal. All design parameters are deterministic and the observation is exactly jointly Gaussian. This is not the scalar common-factor class: for example v_0 and v_1 are orthogonal but both are correlated with the null block.

Rank-three version: split n_null as evenly as possible between the loading vectors r e_1 and r e_2, retaining independent null residuals. Use the same alternative means/counts and the unit alternative loadings v_0=(-rho,0,rho), v_1=(rho,0,rho), v_2=(0,-rho,-rho). This gives five parameter types and three Gaussian factors, with the same exact covariance construction.

The null proportion tends to one and its loading norm r tends to zero. In the approved radial bound the weighted gamma coefficient tends to one, so that bound only gives leading coefficient 1/sqrt(pi), twice the target, and does not reject these families. At r=0 the nulls would be independent of all other coordinates and classical BH has FDR=q pi_0, but that endpoint cannot be substituted uniformly here: means and secondary tail masses simultaneously diverge/vanish.

Required analysis: in rank two, conditional on z, the exact mean empirical CDF at threshold c is
G_z(c)=(n_null/N)[PhiBar((c-r z_1)/sqrt(1-r^2))+PhiBar((c+r z_1)/sqrt(1-r^2))]+sum_{l=0}^2 (n_l/N) 1{|mu_l+v_l dot z|>=c}.
In rank three replace the null term by the two corresponding weighted kernels. Analyze the GLOBAL first crossing inf{c>=u:q G_z(c)/p(c)>=1}; do not add separate local block FDRs. Determine whether its Gaussian integral is O(q) as the finite signal-type heuristic suggests, or can exceed the proposed q sqrt(L)/(2sqrt(pi)) constant. A true refutation requires a strict asymptotic excess and a finite-N empirical-crossing transfer uniformly at the vanishing thresholds, including ties and absent crossings. The very large deterministic N is chosen so concentration could dominate exp(-O(L^3)) threshold masses, but this transfer and the q-limit have NOT been proved.
