Derived first-gate diagnostic for [the pre-Gaussian determinantal proposal](idea_48bbc21ced19.md). This is a draft observation, requiring independent checking before use; it does not refute all determinant mixtures or prove the main theorem.

Fix lambda>0 and original unit true-null vectors v_i. Put M_A=N^{-1} sum_{i in A} v_i v_i^T, M=M_{I0}, and D_lambda=det(I+lambda M). Sample a finite L-ensemble independently of G with L=(lambda/N)(v_i dot v_j)_{i,j in I0}. Positive-semidefinite principal minors are nonnegative. Expanding det(I+L_A) into principal minors and applying det(I+BB^T)=det(I+B^TB) proves exactly
Q_lambda(A):=P(emptyset != S subset A)=[det(I+lambda M_A)-1]/D_lambda.
The empty-subset mass 1/D_lambda must be subtracted.

For every PSD B, det(I+B)>=1+tr B and det(I+B)<=exp(tr B), by multiplying its nonnegative eigenvalues. Here tr M_A=|A|/N and tr M<=1. Consequently
lambda exp(-lambda)|A|/N <= Q_lambda(A) <= lambda exp(lambda)|A|/N.
Thus for A equal to the actual selected-null set, R the actual largest BH rank and zero convention at R=0, lambda=1 gives the genuine deterministic comparison FDP <= e (N/R) Q_1(A). Emptyset degeneracy therefore does NOT defeat the first rankwise comparison.

However, write H_lambda=E[(N/R)Q_lambda(A)] with the same zero convention. Every singleton has L-ensemble probability lambda/(N D_lambda), because each original vector has unit norm. The entire singleton contribution to H_lambda is exactly
(lambda/D_lambda) E[|A|/R]=(lambda/D_lambda) FDR.
All contributions from |S|>=2 are nonnegative. The preceding determinant bounds also show lambda exp(-lambda) FDR <= H_lambda <= lambda exp(lambda) FDR. This transformation alone therefore supplies no independent Gaussian payment: its singleton term already retains the unknown complete masked BH payoff.

Mandatory tests make the issue explicit. For all-null orthonormal vectors and any realized rank r=|A|, D_lambda=(1+lambda/N)^N and
Q_lambda^{>=2}(A)=[(1+lambda/N)^r-1-lambda r/N]/D_lambda.
For fixed lambda, this is at most (lambda r/N)^2 exp(lambda r/N)/(2D_lambda). Removing singletons therefore makes (N/r)Q_lambda^{>=2}(A) tend to zero when r/N tends to zero, although FDP=1 on every r>0 cell; it is identically zero at r=1. This disproves a uniform comparison FDP <= C(N/R)Q_lambda^{>=2}(A) for fixed lambda, rather than the broad DPP proposal. With all-null rank-one clones every principal minor of size>=2 vanishes, D_lambda=1+lambda, Q_lambda(A)=lambda|A|/[N(1+lambda)], and H_lambda=lambda FDR/(1+lambda) exactly.

Remaining gate: independently pay the singleton/actual-rank term, or give a different scale/rank mixture plus an explicit rank-compatible Gaussian probability estimate that handles sparse independent rejection cells and clone collapse. No such payment follows from the determinant identity, no logarithmic lower bound on the main FDR is claimed, no full plan is executable from this calculation, and the broad source proposal remains open.
