Draft feasibility obstruction to one specific generic concentration route; no main-target counterexample or native proof approval is claimed. This was a fresh alternative calculation after the latest free-envelope and exact-gradient obstructions were read.

For any finite Gaussian model write T=theta+AZ, where Z is standard Gaussian and every row A_i has Euclidean norm one; this includes singular covariance via a finite square root. Let S=barPhi, u_k=S^{-1}(qk/N) and T_(k)^downarrow be decreasing ordered statistics. Define

$$
M_N(Z)=\max_{1\le k\le N}\{T_{(k)}^{\downarrow}-u_k\}.
$$

Ordinary largest-rank one-sided BH has R>0 exactly when M_N>=0, with the prescribed non-strict inequalities. Each coordinate T_i is 1-Lipschitz in Z. Every ordered coordinate is 1-Lipschitz under the sup norm on score vectors, so M_N is 1-Lipschitz in the latent Euclidean norm. This exact margin therefore appears suitable for Gaussian concentration without a rank/factor prefactor.

However, fix any q in (0,1/2) and take independent zero-mean unit-variance Gaussian coordinates. Set u=S^{-1}(q/N) and L_N=max_i T_i-u. For fixed epsilon>0, u tends to infinity, and elementary Mills ratios give

$$
N S(u\pm\varepsilon)\sim q\frac{u}{u\pm\varepsilon}\exp(\mp\varepsilon u-\varepsilon^2/2).
$$

The plus expression tends to zero and the minus expression to infinity. Therefore a union bound gives P(L_N>epsilon)<=N S(u+epsilon)->0, while independence gives P(L_N< -epsilon)=[1-S(u-epsilon)]^N<=exp[-N S(u-epsilon)]->0. Thus L_N->0 in probability and M_N>=L_N. In particular every fixed c(q)>0 satisfies P(M_N<=-c(q))->0. This excludes the dimension-uniform negative median/quantile margin that a simple generic concentration proof around such a negative center would require. It does NOT establish M_N->0, a statement deliberately not asserted. It also does not rule out a quantitatively selection-aware concentration or anticoncentration theorem.

The exact rejection-event representation loses FDP/null-label information for mixed models. Reintroducing actual selected-rank and label weights still needs a new finite-Gaussian estimate. Merely applying Gaussian concentration to the 1-Lipschitz margin supplies no universal-order FDR upper bound. Independent all-null Gaussian BH and the original universal-order conjecture are not contradicted.

Independent scoped audit: a fresh reviewer who did not propose this margin calculation independently checked the exact BH equivalence, Lipschitz constant, both Mills-ratio tail limits and the one-sided logical implication from L_N to M_N. The audit confirmed the stated quantile-margin obstruction only. This remains a draft research observation; no files containing historical plans, proofs, statements or approvals were edited.
