## Exact full-FDP current retaining every background face

Fix the full original finite model and original true-null labels. Write Delta=Sigma-I, Gamma_a=I+a Delta for 0<=a<1, and H(T) for the FULL actual ordinary-BH FDP, with every physical score, largest qualifying nonstrict rank, multiplicities and original means. Set
$$
h_a(g)=H(theta+Gamma_a^{1/2}g),\quad F(a)=E h_a(G),\quad C_a=Gamma_a^{-1/2}Delta Gamma_a^{-1/2},
$$
where G is standard Gaussian. Direct differentiation of the full positive-definite Gaussian density, valid for the bounded measurable H, gives
$$
F'(a)=E[h_a(G)Q_a(G)],\qquad Q_a(g)=\tfrac12[g^T C_a g-tr C_a].
$$
This is an exact representation of the ENTIRE signed current. It retains all physical background interfaces implicitly, rather than dropping terms from the draft [all-score matching](idea_53571eb069c0.md) or [determinant cover](idea_8303a1aba8de.md). It is not a new estimate and carries no uniform claim at singular a=1.

For any adapted zero-displacement control u satisfying the conditional first-moment identity, let M=int_0^1 u_t.dB_t. Then E[M|B_1]=0 implies E[h_a(B_1)M]=0. Thus direct assignment of the nonzero terminal covariance score to this first-moment charge is impossible: Q_a cannot equal E[M|B_1] unless Q_a vanishes. This does NOT exclude a charge carrying an additional nonterminal observable; such an observable and its exact BH coefficient must actually be constructed.

A concrete two-test check makes the distinction visible. With one centered null X and one alternative b+Y, b=S^{-1}(q), a_*=S^{-1}(q/2), and covariance parameter rho, full original BH gives
$$
F(rho)=q/2+\tfrac12 P(X>=b,Y>=0)-P(X>=a_*,Y>=0).
$$
Therefore F'(0)=phi(0)[phi(b)/2-phi(a_*)]<0. The strict sign follows from S(a_*)=S(b)/2 and strict increase of phi(t)/S(t) for t>0. A direct proposed equality F'=E[h M] would incorrectly give zero even in this legal mixed-label model.

## An explicit new second-moment charge, and its first falsification gate

To see exactly how far conditional second moments get, here is a completely explicit adapted control. For a deterministic real matrix A, set
$$
u_t=0\ (t<=1/4),\quad u_t=A B_{1/4}\ (1/4<t<=1/2),\quad u_t=-\tfrac12 A B_{1/4}\ (1/2<t<=1).
$$
Its time integral is zero pathwise. Put
$$
V=(B_{1/2}-B_{1/4})-\tfrac12(B_1-B_{1/2}),\qquad M_A=(A B_{1/4}).V.
$$
The Gaussian vector V has covariance (3/8)I and is independent of the JOINT pair (B_{1/4},B_1): its covariance with each component is zero. Also B_{1/4}|B_1=g has mean g/4 and covariance (3/16)I. Consequently the following identities are elementary Gaussian calculations, without a new source black box:
$$
E[M_A|B_1=g]=0,\qquad
E[M_A^2|B_1=g]=\tfrac9{128}tr(A^TA)+\tfrac3{128}g^T A^TA g.
$$
The same conditional expression is E[int|u_t|^2dt|B_1=g], since the integrated squared drift is (3/8)|A B_{1/4}|^2. If bounded controls are required literally, truncate A B_{1/4} in BOTH intervals identically, preserving zero displacement; the displayed polynomial identities follow in the limit by Gaussian moment domination.

Decompose C_a=C_+-C_- into its positive and negative spectral parts and choose A_+=C_+^{1/2}, A_-=C_-^{1/2}. Then the FULL physical current has the explicit second-moment representation
$$
F'(a)=\tfrac{64}{3}E[h_a(B_1)(M_{A_+}^2-M_{A_-}^2)]-2(tr C_a)F(a).
$$
All coefficients are fixed and verifiable; this is not an endpoint charge assigned by definition. However it is NOT a usable main-proof allocation. With dQ=h_a(B_1)dP/F(a), F(a)>0, its positive total energy satisfies
$$
E_Q[M_{A_+}^2+M_{A_-}^2]
=\tfrac9{128}tr|C_a|+\tfrac3{128}E_Q[B_1^T|C_a|B_1]
>=\tfrac9{128}tr|C_a|.
$$
At the IID starting model a=0, take the legal endpoint Sigma=J_N, with every mean zero and all physical nulls retained. Then C_0=J_N-I_N has eigenvalues N-1 and -1 with multiplicity N-1, so tr|C_0|=2(N-1). The energy of this explicit charge diverges at least as 9(N-1)/64, even at fixed q and hence fixed log(1/q). Its first mandatory screen therefore FAILS already for growing-N iid original scores. No later replication, frustrated-model or positive-path test can rescue the same separated-energy allocation. This is a countercalibration to this SPECIFIC allocation, not to all zero-displacement controls, the source theorem or either BH root.

Normalizing this control to bounded energy scales the recovered current by the inverse normalization; it introduces no BH q factor. Preserving the signed second-moment difference could conceivably do better, but an additional estimate of that masked difference is then exactly unpaid. There is no legitimate inference from a trace bound on a Parseval cover to bounded energy for this full-FDP score: the earlier cover record explicitly warns that its importance-weighted payoff can be large on small-rank events.

## Why entropy control alone still does not supply q

For the full original 0<=h_a<=1, let f=h_a/F(a) when F(a)>0. Its entropy is at most log(1/F(a)), hence at most L=log(1/q) in the only interesting case F(a)>=q. Smooth/localize this full terminal density and take its log-density drift only up to fixed time s<1; return its accumulated displacement linearly over (s,1]. The standard density-martingale energy calculation gives E_Q int|u|^2<=2L/(1-s). This preserves every original label and background through h_a; it proves no relation between that low-energy martingale and Q_a or the all-score face current.

In particular, Cauchy-Schwarz on a charge E[h_a chi M] gives a prefactor F(a), namely F(a)*sqrt(E_Q chi^2)*sqrt(2L/(1-s)), not q. A terminal chi gives zero by conditional first-moment cancellation. For a nonterminal chi the exact BH face/current identity and its q/F(a) normalization remain to be proved. The fixed-time smoothed-density-tail event in the source is not an ordinary-BH rejection/rank event. Substituting the latter without deriving a new identity loses the source's martingale-gradient argument. Conditioning on a selected physical index would also alter the tilt and cannot silently retain the full-FDP entropy bound.

## Decision

No genuinely executable unrestricted main-proof route emerged. The useful source identities preserve the entire terminal weight, but do not by themselves construct the required small BH charge. A direct first-moment match is ruled out by conditional orthogonality; the explicit spectral second-moment construction above fails the iid energy screen; the dimension-free stopped-density alternative lacks an exact current identity and the q factor. The first unproved gate for a different attempt is an explicit bounded-energy NONTERMINAL charge whose pairing with the full original FDP gives the signed actual BH current with a proved q coefficient. Stating that gate as a checklist item would repeat the old masked signed-aggregation gap.

Comparison with the failed [peeling-history plan](../plans/plan_one_sided_order_peeling_history_kernel_20261107.md): that route saturated at the unknown terminal FDR after exact cancellations. The new conditional cancellation is exact too, but does not pay a nonzero covariance current. No old endpoint remainder has been renamed as a bounded martingale. The source's fixed-time theorem is neither a maximal-in-time result nor a proof of the frozen-ray conjecture.
