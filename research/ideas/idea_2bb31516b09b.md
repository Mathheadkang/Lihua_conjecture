Route 2 of the researcher-authorized parallel round of 12 November 2026. UNPROVED draft direction.

**Core idea.** Prove the order assertion of the approved frozen-ray conjecture statement (gaussian_frozen_ray_maximal_likelihood_ratio_conjecture), E min(c, sup_{w>=0} sum_i omega_i exp(w Z_i - w^2/2)) <= C sqrt(log c), by a stopped density-martingale argument, then transfer to BH.

**External input.** Gaussian case of Talagrand's convolution conjecture: Eldan-Lee (with a loglog loss) and Lehec (removed it; approved library-side nodes lehec2015_*). Self-contained martingale proofs with explicit dimension-free constants: A. Shaposhnikov, Some remarks on Talagrand's convolution conjecture, arXiv:2609.11290v1 (10 Sep 2026), Section 2, Lemma 2.3, Corollary 2.4, Theorem 2.5. Robust variant: Y. Chen, Talagrand's convolution conjecture up to loglog via perturbed reverse heat, arXiv:2511.19374. Both checked at abstract/record level only on 12 Nov 2026.

**Agent heuristic (unverified) explaining the gap.** Each f_w is a normalized log-convex density, and d^2/dw^2 log f_w >= -1, so sup_w f_w is at most a constant times the average of f_w over a w-window of length W ~ sqrt(log c). That average is again normalized and log-convex, so Lehec's tail bound gives only E min(c, Lambda) <~ log c. Hence the supremum along the ray costs exactly one sqrt(log) factor: the same gap as q log(1/q) versus q sqrt(log(1/q)).

**Gaps.** (i) A genuinely maximal argument: one stopping time controlling the whole w-family (e.g. stop the Föllmer/density martingale of a w-mixture at the first time some f_w reaches alpha, and bound the overshoot by the weighted Itô isometry). (ii) The BH transfer, currently available only with conditional independence / a private-noise floor (draft gaussian_bh_one_sided_private_noise_floor_order).

**Failure criterion.** A finite (n, Sigma, omega) family with E min(c, Lambda) / sqrt(log c) unbounded; check existing stress tests (idea_57338934f2f7) first.
