Round 2's supplied candidate summaries report a uniform bound P(E_q) <= 2q for independent Gaussian antipodal blocks with arbitrary sign multiplicities and arbitrarily unequal masses, and exact probability q for the balanced antithetic subfamily when 0 < q < 1/2. Thus independent carrier blocks already tolerate growing rank and separated masses without accumulating an unbounded Simes ratio. A possible next proof step is to find a dimension-uniform comparison from suitably represented dependent Gaussian carriers to this calibrated independent reference, or a direct geometric substitute controlling the union over crossing ranks. No such comparison is established: shared Gaussian directions and mixed-sign correlations are exactly the missing step, and the independent-block bound must not be extended to them without proof. These block facts are taken from the current search-summary pack's failure lines; this idea does not independently verify the candidate derivations. The other round-2 construction fails joint Gaussianity, since its coordinate difference has both an atom at zero and nonzero values with positive probability, so its general-copula harmonic inflation cannot supply the missing Gaussian dependence argument.
