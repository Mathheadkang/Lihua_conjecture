Primary-source verification for the growing-private-copy route: Monitirtha Dey, On limiting behaviors of stepwise multiple testing procedures, Statistical Papers 65 (2024), 5691–5717, DOI10.1007/s00362-024-01613-6, published8Oct2024, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00362-024-01613-6 . Publisher HTML Theorem4.3 states the global-null equicorrelated Gaussian BH FDR limit via the infimum over clone fraction t of [Phi^{-1}(1-tq)-sqrt(1-rho)Phi^{-1}(1-t)]/sqrt(rho). Its proof follows the common Gaussian factor and minimum of order-statistic thresholds. This is directly analogous to the ONE-BLOCK private-copy contact curve, not a general finite-background mixed-label clone theorem. The publisher also explicitly corrects a predecessor proof that incorrectly bounded BH by the first Bonferroni crossing; use the largest crossing. Before adapting, independently check the proof's endpoint/uniform-quantile passage; no full proof audit or import has been performed here. The source does not justify interchanging growing copy count with vanishing noise, nor the proposed compensated second contact scale, and it gives no unrestricted main bound.
