Round 1 excludes every signed rank-one replicated Gaussian family: for T_i=s_i Z and sign counts m_+,m_- summing to N, the Simes event for 0<q<1/2 is the union of the disjoint tails overlinePhi(Z)<=q m_+/N and overlinePhi(Z)>=1-q m_-/N, omitting an absent sign class. Its probability is exactly q. This explicit calculation is a proof step for the rank-one subcase and a checkpoint for a proposed dimension-uniform argument: preserve multiplicities and both tails so that replication and sign imbalance are charged in proportion to their coordinate mass. A possible extension is to seek a Gaussian Gram-geometric bound on the union of calibrated excursion caps, recovering this exact rank-one identity. The round pack also reports a bound below 4q for regular polygons in rank two, but arbitrary angular configurations and growing rank remain uncovered. No extension from these subcases to the target has been established; this approach is not a proof or a counterexample.
