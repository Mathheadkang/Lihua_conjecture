A possible proof step for the compensated growing-clone candidate is to establish the deterministic positive-crossing equality separately from the finite-copy limit. At fixed latent Gaussian G and fixed positive private noise, the chosen block's population survival mass is continuous in the rank parameter, while the exact groups add finitely many upward jumps at their non-strict thresholds. A positive largest crossing rho cannot have H(rho)>rho: continuity on its right would give a larger admissible crossing; at a positive upper endpoint the total mass bound rules out strict excess. Thus any well-defined positive population largest crossing satisfies H(rho)=rho. This deterministic observation does not establish empirical largest-root convergence, expectation convergence, control of empirical ranks tending to zero, or the compensated expansion. The round-4 correlated alternative pair illustrates the next local step: retain the actual common largest root and the reciprocal-rank fractional FDP; integrate the upper-branch fractional deficit rather than dropping it pointwise. For N=2, q=1/4, theta=(1,0), correlation 1/2, this yields an integrated O(s^2/t_s^2) deficit and the claimed nonzero coefficient 0.006596569578441303. This estimate is local to that fixed checked model. A general argument must separately control multirank endpoints, root births and switches, rank-face intersections and overlap, dependent labels, and empirical small-rank tails at scale s/t_s. No uniform-in-model or uniform-in-copy-count estimate, limit exchange, or general convergence theorem is asserted.
