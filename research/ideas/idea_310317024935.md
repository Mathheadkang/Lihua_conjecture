Scoped method obstruction, not a refutation of the original uniform FDR conjecture. Complete proof and the exact C9/C10 interface are frozen in Failure of plan_two_sided_signed_forest_rank_cells_20261104 (SHA256 2a940ad8f833d702bee9a87e64b5da61400677de2553664a5448c4cc660c64fd); proof and interface quantifiers were independently checked. Write Q for the standard normal survival function. Choose U>1, q=2Q(U), N=ceil(q/Q(2U)), t_j=Q^{-1}(qj/(2N)), and Z standard normal. There is one true null T0=Z and alternatives Tj=Z−2t_j (j=1,...,N−1); covariance is rank one and all marginal variances are 1. Flipping alternatives gives positive means and the same two-sided p-values if needed. On Z=s in [U,2U], a(s)=2NQ(s)/q>=2; exact largest-rank counting gives ceil(a(s))<=R<=a(s)+2 and proves the null is rejected. Convexity of Q yields the upper rank bound, while the whole interval excludes the opposite-sign re-entry branch. Thus FDR H >= q/(4N) log(Q(U)/Q(2U)) >= 3qU^2/(8N). The plan's C9/C10 together imply NH/q <= 1+U/(2 sqrt(2pi))+C(1+log U), contradicted for large U. This rules out that extra per-null/null-proportion budget even though the original goal has no such proportionality requirement. Fix U,N before taking a positive-definite covariance approximation; bounded-FDP continuity at the finite BH threshold walls preserves the strict gap. Since there is only one true null, H<=P(p_null<=q)=q, hence H/[q sqrt(log(1/q))] tends to zero: this family does not refute the original sharp FDR conjecture.
