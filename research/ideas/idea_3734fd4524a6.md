Round 1 obstruction: the admissible N=1 negative-null family reproduces the claimed compensated coefficient rather than refuting it; two worker records cover this same family. A possible proof step is to derive the local chosen-group crossing displacement near each original rank face, subtract the explicit compensation, and identify its residual at scale s/t_s with log{r h(u_r)} times the original signed FDP face jump. This is an approach only: the singleton calculation does not establish the general step. First prove the finite-copy limit and selection of the actual common largest population crossing; then control root births, switches and multirank cascades and retain the fractional chosen-null mass in the FDP numerator. In particular, establish a remainder that permits integration against the signed jump measures without replacing them by positive parts or exchanging K and epsilon limits. The N=2 mixed-label fractional-FDP diagnostic is unfinished and remains an open test, not supporting proof evidence.
