Suggested by the teaching chat; agent-origin observation and lower-priority alternative route. The formula and proposed use below require independent checking. Do not assume the universal bound or replace the current fold task.

## A concrete finite-model identity to verify first

For a fixed finite positive-definite Gaussian model with fixed actual null labels, let F(q) be ordinary BH FDR, u_r(q)=barPhi^{-1}(qr/N), and mu_{i,r} the signed FULL-FDP coordinate-face measures at that q. Since
d u_r/d log q=-m(u_r), m(u)=barPhi(u)/phi(u),
simultaneously moving the entire threshold grid suggests the exact identity
q dF/dq = sum_{i=1}^N sum_{r=1}^N m(u_r) mu_{i,r}(E_i).

Derive it by the same finite signed face transport used for the approved clone identity, retaining actual largest ranks and controlling intersections of faces using the fixed positive-definite covariance. Do not claim dimension-uniform remainders or extend the formula by continuity to a singular original covariance. The sum is over ALL triggering coordinates, including alternative coordinates; it is not automatically a null-only trace.

This gives the rank-dependent Mills weight a legal global variation that does not move any means or change null labels. It is different from the unweighted common-mean derivative sum_i,r mu_{i,r}. As a check, in the independent all-null model F(q)=q, so the complete weighted sum must be q even though the unweighted zero-mean boundary sum grows at least like q sqrt(log N).

## Possible use if a weighted boundary identity is available

Consider the normalized objective J(q,pi)=F(q,pi)/[q U(q)], U(q)=barPhi^{-1}(q). At an ACTUAL interior stationary point in q, the formal scale relation is
q partial_q F = F[1-m(U)/U].
At an approximate variational point, keep the appropriate metric-slope error. Test whether this weighted relation, the rare-clone inequality, and the one-sided zero-mean KKT constraints can eliminate a boundary multiplier without estimating its bare unweighted total.

This is only a candidate mechanism. Algebra may instead show it is insufficient; reporting that is useful.

## Essential safeguards and executable test order

1. Verify the finite-PD q-derivative identity independently, including signs and all-coordinate summation.
2. Substitute independent all-null models, regularized unbalanced clones, and an available finite lower-bound model. Compare the signs with the clone stationarity relations.
3. Write down the joint inequalities and see whether they actually close a weighted estimate. Do not create a large proof plan before this algebraic check.
4. If optimizing over q, first restrict q to a compact interval [q_min,q_0] with q_min>0. Ekeland on the untruncated normalized ratio over q down to zero would require boundedness above, potentially assuming exactly the desired conjecture.
5. A finite interval may put the optimum at q_min or q_0. A penalty or barrier does not automatically remove endpoint escape for free; record its derivative term and prove it can be paid uniformly before using the interior scale equality.
6. Keep zero-null one-sided constraints. A change in nominal q preserves null labels, but that fact alone does not eliminate their mean-boundary multipliers.
7. Any q_min down to zero limit or passage from finite models to the completed population space remains a separate obligation.

Priority: use as a short diagnostic and possible weighted alternative after the boundary-flux test, not as an asserted closure of the main result.
