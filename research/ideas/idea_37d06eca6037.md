New proof mechanism to evaluate after the mixed-replica attempt, not a proof of its desired budget. The failure of plan_one_sided_order_mixed_trace_replica_cells_20261108 is a thin actual BH cell whose two individual signed face terms stay nonzero while the coherence deficit tends to zero; their full sum cancels. Any new attempt must pair distinct faces before taking positive parts.

Verified relevant source: Frederik Riis Mikkelsen and Niels Richard Hansen, Degrees of Freedom for Piecewise Lipschitz Estimators, arXiv:1601.03524v3 (10 February 2017), https://arxiv.org/pdf/1601.03524v3. Author publication page and publisher DOI 10.1214/17-AIHP822 identify the 2018 AIHP 54(2),819-841 article. Read Assumption 2.2 and Theorem 2.4 on PDF pp.4-5, the signed-integrand discussion on p.8, and the Gauss-Green proof location Appendix A.2 pp.25-26. This is a signed boundary representation, not a universal BH budget. The identities used here must still be reproved inline or admitted only through the native source workflow; no new blackbox dependency is asserted.

Concrete object for a new calculation: write B=sum_{i true} v_i g_i, where g_i=1{i selected}/[R h(u_R)], and A_i(t,y)=P_{1-t}g_i(y)/P_{1-t}f(y). Decompose tr K=sum_i D_{v_i}A_i exactly. For each tag i, condition the future Gaussian on its orthogonal projection to v_i. The actual finite threshold mesh then cuts that real Gaussian coordinate into a finite ordered list of intervals, carrying the original largest BH ranks, labels and means. On that fibre the complete signed jump measure is d g_i-A_i(t,y) d f. It retains both the numerator and inverse-normalizer terms.

Try a deterministic measurable matching of positive and negative jumps along this one-dimensional ordered list, keeping any unmatched net mass explicit. Equivalently track its cumulative primitive g_i-A_i f before estimating Gaussian-weighted integrals. Test the procedure first on the proved thin-cell obstruction, constant-direction clones, the unequal signed clones and an actual nonparallel mixed three-test cascade. Paired Gaussian density differences should be estimated through full truncated-normal moments or a precisely proved Gaussian Hardy inequality, rather than by separately bounding each endpoint. The actual true-null threshold calibration is phi(u_r-theta_i)/(r h(u_r))<=q/N; no random maximizing threshold may be inserted into a deterministic-level bound.

The essential still-unproved gate is whether the calibrated unmatched flux and total pairing cost can be bounded by the mixed square-root/entropy-amplified coherence resource with universal constants. A cumulative integration-by-parts identity that merely recovers P17/P21 or original FDR is not progress at this gate. Preserve all covariance/Gram weights; do not choose an independent worst sign per tag or hide a log N. This is a suggested executable signed-fibre calculation, not a claim that the required matching or cost bound exists.
