Unverified heuristic from the plan stage (12 November 2026). For any continuous process X_t with law N(0,R_t^Sigma), V_-(f_{q,Sigma}) <= E[number of exits of t -> X_t from S_q], since each partition increment is bounded by the probability of an exit in that interval. Two natural couplings both fail uniformly in N. (a) Rotation coupling X_t = sqrt(1-t) Z + sqrt(t) W with Z, W independent: each coordinate is a sinusoid, but at Sigma = I (constant law, V_- = 0) the expected exit count is of order the total influence of S_q, about q sqrt(2 log N). (b) Deterministic Lyapunov flow dX/dt = L_t X with L_t R_t + R_t L_t = Sigma∘Sigma - Sigma: E[exits] is the integrated positive outflux, which loses the sign cancellation of (L_t x)_i at pivotal points; for Sigma = I + E with small random-sign entries of size epsilon/sqrt(N) it is of order epsilon q sqrt(log N), unbounded in N. So any proof must keep the signed sum over pivotal coordinates or pairs (as in the Price formula) before taking positive parts; this is the same lesson as the obstructed pointwise positive-flux gate of idea_c23fac250465, now for the Schur-square path.
