Acceptance P(M_A(T)<=s0)>=delta0 is a statement about a Mills-penalized subset supremum, not about Simes crossings. It implies the sparse cap (indeed the full fixed-level bound P(E_q)<=1-delta0, all ranks) only through the deterministic Mills-penalized subset reduction, which is a separate unproved lemma, for q small enough that (1/2)sqrt(log(1/(Aq)))-1>s0. No direct implication in either direction: the cap does not control M_A.
