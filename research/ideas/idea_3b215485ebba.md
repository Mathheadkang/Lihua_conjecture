Disposition A: rank-sensitive geometry plus extremal localization (idea_a2bf3368f3bd).
N=1 gives FDR=S(u(q)-theta)<=q for one-sided true theta<=0, or exactly q for a two-sided true theta=0. For an alternative alone FDR=0. Uniform physical replication keeps covariance rank unchanged and preserves actual BH FDR, so it does not itself refute the proposed rank-sensitive estimate. Small scalar models below have FDR/q between .30 and 1, supplying calibration only.
The rank-d estimate and the proposed O(log(1/q)) rank localization are two independent unproved essentials. No mechanism was produced to localize the nonconvex mixed-label objective. The approved common-loading continuity theorem is at fixed q and fixed nonzero common loading sigma, in its tagged population model; it neither changes rank at uniform relative FDR cost nor supplies a sigma-uniform main bound. The fractional-frame surface kernel is a local intersection/slice inequality, not such a localization theorem. A rank-sensitive bound alone leaves the main quantifiers unpaid. A is not selected as an executable main-proof direction and is not refuted.

Disposition C: translated-cell Mehler congestion (idea_8b121d2e72a5).
This has a concrete analytic mechanism beyond just restating a global signed budget: full Cameron-Martin shifts make the individual kernels integrable exponential half-lines, and smoothing at variance v=1-exp(-2/log(1/q)) cancels their large Mills factors. The proposed L-infinity inequality is much stronger than the desired integral estimate and remains unproved for correlated scores. It is not supplied by generic OU bounds or the approved fractional-frame kernel: the latter requires compatible simultaneous tail constraints under one common Gaussian configuration and a PSD frame allocation. Different translated BH cells are evaluated at y+c_ir a_i, and no such common-event/allocation identification has been proved. Draft all-score/cofactor identities may be useful bookkeeping but do not already prove this cross-translation congestion inequality.

Exact mathematical checks for C.
1. Measure normalization. For each original true-null rejection cell D_ir=A_ir intersect {a_i.Z>=c_ir}, the translation z=y+c_ir a_i has Jacobian one and Gaussian density ratio exp(-c_ir a_i.y-c_ir^2/2). Therefore integral H_1 d gamma=FDR exactly. For two-sided true nulls, split into the two half-line faces, shifting by c_ir*(+a_i) and c_ir*(-a_i); their common boundary is null. Mehler invariance preserves that integral. There is no extra normal-density normalization or rank factor.
2. N=1 analytic test. With c=u(q)-theta>=u(q)>=1, h(y)=1{y>=0} exp(-cy-c^2/2). At Gaussian smoothing mean m and variance v,
E h(Y)=exp(-c^2/2-cm+c^2 v/2) Phi((m-cv)/sqrt(v)).
Bounding the smoothing density by 1/sqrt(2*pi*v) gives E h(Y)<=exp(-c^2/2)/(c sqrt(2*pi*v))<=2S(c)/sqrt(v)<=2q/sqrt(v), by Mills for c>=1. For the two-sided N=1 model the same calculation applies to both faces at c=u(q/2), giving the same total upper bound 2q/sqrt(v). For L=log(1/q)>=1, v>= (1-exp(-2))/L, so this is a universal constant times q sqrt(L). These are short agent derivations, not independently approved assets.
3. Stronger independent-score calibration. For Sigma=I, conditional on the other smoothed coordinates, R_i with p_i forced to zero is a single rank r independent of coordinate i. Translation in a_i changes only coordinate i. Integrating its exponential half-line kernel gives at most 2q/(N sqrt(v)), uniformly in the arbitrary smoothing center and in all alternative means. Negative one-sided null means only increase c and decrease S(c). In the two-sided case each face has tail level qr/(2N), so the two faces give the same total bound. Summing proves the proposed congestion estimate for independent original scores with constant at most 2/sqrt(1-exp(-2)) for q<=S(1). It does not extend to arbitrary covariance by conditioning on all other scores: conditional variance may vanish and R_i may move under the translation.
4. Why smoothing matters. In the all-null independent original model at y=0, each forced-null rank is 1 and H_1(0)=N exp(-u(q/N)^2/2), asymptotic to sqrt(2*pi) q u(q/N). Thus a pointwise UNSMOOTHED bound of order q sqrt(log(1/q)) is false as N grows. The independent-score calculation above shows exactly the reciprocal-threshold cancellation that fixes this example after the prescribed smoothing.
5. Largest-rank and replication. For q=.1, p=(.06,.08) has largest nonstrict BH rank 2 even though rank 1 fails. All diagnostic code used the largest qualifying rank. Exact K-fold physical replication gives R'=K R and V'=K V. On any nonzero transported rejection cell, stability gives the actual rank, hence only ranks Kr occur after replication, the threshold c_{i,Kr}' equals c_{i,r}, and K copies each carry 1/(Kr). Consequently the FULL transported H is also exactly replication invariant in the same latent embedding, not merely its integral. No forced-single-clone rank event outside the genuine rejection tail is counted.
