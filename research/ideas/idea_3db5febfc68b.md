Finite diagnostic from the fresh signed-fibre planning pass, not a main-target counterexample. Put U=S^{-1}(q), a=S^{-1}(q/2), 0<epsilon<a-U, T1=G with true-null mean zero, and T2=2U+epsilon-G with positive alternative mean. Covariance is [[1,-1],[-1,1]], with unit diagonal. Ordinary largest-rank BH has positive FDP only on [U,U+epsilon] (R=2, FDP=1/2) and [a,infinity) (R=1, FDP=1). On the nonempty open gap (U+epsilon,a) FDP is zero. Its FDR is S(a)+(S(U)-S(U+epsilon))/2, so this does not refute the main target or the mixed trace bound.

The FDP-tilted density has disconnected support even along the actual single true-null fibre. A smooth function can take different constants on the two support components and vary only in the zero-density gap. Its weighted Dirichlet integral is zero but its variance is positive; the unqualified FDP-weighted Poincare/Hardy shortcut therefore has infinite required constant. This does not rule out a different pairing estimate or an inequality with different, explicitly justified weights.

The same planning pass gave the exact measurable Jordan matching of the finite signed fibre jump measure d g_i-A_i d f: match quantiles of positive and negative atom masses up to the smaller total mass, then retain signed unmatched mass. The unmatched net mass generally does not vanish. A_i is normalized over the whole future Gaussian, not separately over each orthogonal fibre. Estimating matched and unmatched pieces by separate positive costs can lose the same cancellation as the failed face method. No new plan was created merely to rename P17/P21.

Verified external tool: Barthe-Roberto, Sobolev inequalities for probability measures on the real line, Studia Math159(3)(2003)481-497, DOI10.4064/sm159-3-9, publisher PDF https://www.impan.pl/shop/publication/transaction/download/product/89465. Read p483 and Proposition2 pp484-485: the two-weight Hardy criterion uses nonnegative measures and an explicit tail-mass times inverse derivative-density integral. Neither the Gaussian reference constant nor its finiteness can be silently transferred to actual FDP weights.
