The compound-Poisson cap bounds the clump-bracket charge (an FDR bound only with the pathwise bracket lemma) for near-boundary nulls -a_q<theta<=0 under neighbourhood sparsity e^{-u} m; the separated cap concerns all nulls in rare clusters of mass < q^{C_2}. Small separated clusters need not be neighbourhood-sparse (in high dimension a ball of radius kappa/u can meet unboundedly many separated centres), so no implication.
