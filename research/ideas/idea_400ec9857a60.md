Strategic direction from the 8 November 2026 brainstorm (ranked 2 of 8). This is the requested "re-planning" direction: a dual (potential/supermartingale) certificate replaces the stepwise Stein/flux identities. UNPROVED beyond the stated routine steps.

Notation: $U=\overline\Phi^{-1}(q)$, $k(w)=N\overline\Phi(w)/q$, $N(w)=\#\{j:T_j\ge w\}$, $M(w)=N^{-1}\#\{i\in I_0:T_i\ge w\}$, $h=\phi/\overline\Phi$, $L=u_R$.

1. Core idea and origin. Exact identity: on $\{R\ge1\}$, $\mathrm{FDP}=q\,M(L)/\overline\Phi(L)$, and $L=\min\{w\ge U: N(w)\ge k(w)\}$ (largest rank = lowest qualifying level). Let the levels be revealed from the bottom up: $\mathcal G_w=\sigma\big(T_j\mathbf 1\{T_j<w\},\ \mathbf 1\{T_j\ge w\}:\ j\in[N]\big)$. Then $N(w)$ is $\mathcal G_w$-adapted and $L$ is a $\mathcal G$-stopping time. Put $X_w=M(w)/\overline\Phi(w)$, a nonnegative adapted process. Under independence, $\mathbb E[\mathbf 1\{T_i\ge w'\}\mid\mathcal G_w]=\mathbf 1\{T_i\ge w\}\overline\Phi(w'-\theta_i)/\overline\Phi(w-\theta_i)\le\mathbf 1\{T_i\ge w\}\overline\Phi(w')/\overline\Phi(w)$ for $w'>w$ and $\theta_i\le0$ (log-concavity of $\overline\Phi$), so $X$ is a supermartingale and optional stopping for nonnegative supermartingales gives $\mathrm{FDR}/q=\mathbb E X_L\le\mathbb E X_U=\pi_0$: a two-line proof of BH control under independence, including negative null means. In general the Doob-Meyer compensator is a hazard comparison:
$$
\mathbb E[dX_w\mid\mathcal G_w]=\frac{dw}{N\,\overline\Phi(w)}\sum_{i\in I_0:\ T_i\ge w}\big[h(w)-h_i(w\mid\mathcal G_w)\big],
$$
where $h_i(w\mid\mathcal G_w)$ is the conditional hazard at $w$ of $T_i$ given the revealed values below $w$ and the exceedance indicators above $w$. Hence $\mathrm{FDR}/q=\pi_0+\mathbb E\int_U^L(\text{drift})$ exactly, and the target is equivalent to: expected positive drift over $[U,L]$ is $\le C(1+U)$. The positive part of the integrand comes only from nulls whose conditional law given $\mathcal G_w$ sits high relative to $w$ (shell-type nulls), so the approved integrated tail-ratio lemma [Lei integrated conditional Gaussian tail-ratio bounds](lei2026_integrated_conditional_tail_ratios) can be inserted termwise. Discipline: optimal stopping and its dual (Rogers 2002, Haugh-Kogan: $\sup_\tau\mathbb E Z_\tau=\inf_{M}\mathbb E\sup_t(Z_t-M_t)$ over martingales $M$; links NOT verified), Lyapunov/potential certificates; in e-value language, a supermartingale certificate is an e-process in the level variable (Wang-Ramdas e-BH, https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.02824, read at abstract level).

Relation to the failed peeling plan plan_one_sided_order_peeling_history_kernel_20261107: that plan's PH-Doob decomposition ran along the rank-peeling iteration and its compensator $D_n$ had no sign; here the filtration is the natural level filtration, the BH mask is the stopping time itself (no largest-rank mask appears in the identity), and the compensator is an explicit hazard difference with a readable sign structure.

Literature (verified links): Wang, Ramdas, False discovery rate control with e-values, JRSSB 2022, https://academic.oup.com/jrsssb/article/84/3/822/7056146 ; Benjamini-Yekutieli 2001 leave-one-out identity (classical, link not verified); Rogers, Monte Carlo valuation of American options, Math. 3. First testable propositions. (OS-1, routine) Verify the stopping-time property of $L$, the exact identity, and the independence proof, including ties and $R=0$. (OS-2, decisive) In the one-common-factor model $T_i=\theta_i+a_iZ+\sqrt{1-a_i^2}\varepsilon_i$, compute the conditional law of a null $T_i$ given $\mathcal G_w$ (Gaussian regression on revealed values plus indicator conditioning) and prove $\mathbb E\int_U^L\frac{1}{N\overline\Phi(w)}\sum_{i\in I_0,T_i\ge w}[h(w)-h_i(w\mid\mathcal G_w)]_+\,dw\le C(1+U)$, recovering Lei's one-sided common-factor theorem (thm:one-upper) up to constants by a new route. If the mixed value/indicator conditioning is intractable, coarsen to $\mathcal G'_w=\sigma(Z)\vee\sigma(N(w'):w'\le w)$ and check whether $L$ remains a stopping time (it does if $N$ is $\mathcal G'$-adapted).

4. Why it bypasses the obstacles. Obstacle 1: no Gaussian interpolation, no integration by parts in a calibrated score; the Gaussian input is one-dimensional conditional hazards, and the "signed largest-rank mask" is absorbed into the stopping time, so the remainder is a compensator, not the FDR itself. Obstacle 2a: only the FIRST crossing of the count with the line matters; no crossing count. Obstacle 2b: no coherence certificate. Obstacle 2c: no cells. Obstacle 2d: the drift is a sum of scalar hazards; no matrix Cauchy-Schwarz, no $\sqrt d$.

5. Failure criterion. Abandon if in the one-factor model the expected positive drift already scales like $\log N$ (conditioning on the indicators above $w$ inflating conditional means of many nulls simultaneously), or if no coarsened filtration keeps $L$ a stopping time while making conditional hazards computable.

6. Payoff: full (universal drift bound) / partial (new proof of the one-factor theorem; e-process formulation usable in the manuscript) / tool.
