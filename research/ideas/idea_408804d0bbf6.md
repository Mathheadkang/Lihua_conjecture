Suggested by the teaching chat and its independent brainstorming workers; origin agent. The researcher explicitly asked for concrete routes to the unrestricted main bound. This is a proposed finite-model reduction for independent verification, not an approved lemma. Preserve the existing targets and history. Priority: first verify the scalar identity, then falsify the proposed quantitative budget before attempting a general proof.

## Why this differs from the current route

Work directly with finite ordinary one-sided Gaussian BH. No common-noise regularizer, private-noise regularizer, rank cutoff, extremizer, or positive-definite covariance assumption is needed for the proposed scalar decomposition. Unlike the approved correlated-clone variation, this conditions on the regression residual of a selected null and moves all correlated scores along their lawful joint conditional path. The main new object is a bounded scalar function whose signed jumps measure excess over the usual q/N contribution.

## Candidate exact identity

Let T_j=theta_j+Z_j, Cov(Z_i,Z_j)=rho_ij, Var(Z_i)=1. Fix a true null i, theta_i<=0. Write Z_j=rho_ij Z_i+xi_j, where the entire residual vector xi is independent of Z_i; singular covariance is permitted. Put u(t)=S^{-1}(t). Conditional on xi, vary the actual p-value of i through t by setting
$$
T_j(t)=\theta_j+\rho_{ij}\{u(t)-\theta_i\}+\xi_j.
$$
Thus T_i(t)=u(t). Let R_i(t) be the ACTUAL largest non-strict ordinary BH rank of this full model at level q<1/2, r_i=R_i/N, and
$$
A_i(t)=\min\{t/(q r_i(t)),1\},
$$
with A_i=1 when R_i=0. Each affine score path crosses each of the N score thresholds at most once unless constant; merge simultaneous crossings, retain constantly tied scores using the original non-strict convention. Hence the rank path has finitely many jumps. At non-jump points,
$$
A_i'(t)=\frac{\mathbf1\{i\text{ rejected at }t\}}{q r_i(t)},
\qquad A_i(0+)=0,\quad A_i(q-)=1.
$$
Values at isolated jump points do not affect the integral.

For theta_i=0 the conditional p-value is uniform, and the candidate exact identity is
$$
\operatorname{FDR}_i=\frac qN\left[1+
\mathbb E_\xi\sum_{t_j\in(0,q)}
\{A_i(t_j^-)-A_i(t_j^+)\}\right].
$$
A downward jump of A_i is caused by an upward jump in the actual rank while the null is or becomes rejected. Preserve the signed jump sum; negative jumps in the charge are useful cancellations.

For theta_i<=0, retain the actual p-density
$$
g_i(t)=\exp\{\theta_i u(t)-\theta_i^2/2\}.
$$
On (0,q), it is nondecreasing and <=1. The stronger exact weighted formula to verify is
$$
\operatorname{FDR}_i=\frac qN\mathbb E_\xi\left[
g_i(q)-\int_0^q A_i(t)\,dg_i(t)
+\sum_{t_j\in(0,q)}g_i(t_j)
\{A_i(t_j^-)-A_i(t_j^+)\}\right].
$$
The first two terms form a baseline in [0,1]. Do not replace a negative null mean by zero in the actual model.

## The explicit unresolved quantitative gate

The primary object is the averaged SIGNED weighted jump charge. A stronger, separately falsifiable sufficient condition uses
$$
B_i=\sum_{t_j\in(0,q)}g_i(t_j)
[A_i(t_j^-)-A_i(t_j^+)]_+.
$$
The proposed identity gives
$$
F(q)\le q\pi_0+\frac qN\sum_{i\in H_0}\mathbb E B_i.
$$
A universal bound N^{-1}sum_i E B_i <= C sqrt(log(1/q)) would close the one-sided target. This positive-part bound is UNPROVED and may be too strong. If it fails, record a counterexample to this strengthening only, retain the exact signed identity, and test a bound after the signed charges have been assembled.

## Analytic checks already derived here, requiring independent confirmation

1. If rho_ij>=0 for every j, all p-values are nondecreasing in t, the actual rank is nonincreasing, and A_i is nondecreasing: B_i=0. In particular independent microscopic-rank null dust creates no artificial budget.
2. All-null antipodal clones with arbitrary masses a,1-a: along a + null path t<=q<1/2, the opposite p-values equal 1-t>q. R_i=Na until t=qa and then zero; A_i is continuous and nondecreasing, so B_i=0. The existing unbalanced-antipodal counterexample to the old entropy budget does not refute this new object.
3. One true-zero + clone block of mass a, with arbitrarily many positive-mean - clone blocks: force the whole true block to p=0 and call the resulting rank r*(t). It is nondecreasing and belongs to [a,1]; group self-consistency gives A_i=min{t/(q r*),1}. Each downward A jump costs at most Delta log r*, yielding B_i<=log(1/a), hence the total true-block weighted budget <=a log(1/a)<=1/e. This is a useful finite analytic screening family, not a general theorem.

## First executable falsification experiment

Use two-dimensional loadings v_j=(cos alpha_j,sin alpha_j), two or three true-null directions, and several false blocks with means chosen to trigger at geometric rank scales. Conditional on null i, only a scalar standard-normal residual x remains:
$$
T_j(t)=\theta_j+\cos(\alpha_j-\alpha_i)\{u(t)-\theta_i\}
+\sin(\alpha_j-\alpha_i)x.
$$
For nonzero rho_ij enumerate every candidate
$$
t_{jk}=S\left(\theta_i+
\frac{u(qk/N)-\theta_j-\sin(\alpha_j-\alpha_i)x}
{\cos(\alpha_j-\alpha_i)}\right).
$$
Sort the candidates in (0,q), merge ties, evaluate ACTUAL BH ranks between them, and compute both signed and positive-part charges. Handle zero correlations as constant paths. Integrate over the one-dimensional normal residual with explicit truncation/error checks for any claimed certificate. Track q, number of scales, clone weights and negative true means. Compare actual FDR, exact signed reconstruction, and positive-part budget separately. An exploding positive budget with bounded signed sum should redirect the proof to cancellation, not invalidate the main goal.

## Teaching-chat numerical diagnostic, not certified evidence

A lightweight pure-Python calculation with NormalDist and seed 20261109 enumerated all score-threshold intervals in 96 one-factor +/- loading models: N in {2,4,8,16}, q in {0.001,0.01,0.05,0.1}, six models per pair, half zero-mean nulls, other means sampled uniformly from [0.05,2.5 u(q)], signs sampled uniformly. Direct Gaussian interval integration of FDP and signed reconstruction agreed to maximum absolute error 1.88e-16. Maximum FDR/q was 0.7441 (these did not inflate above q); maximum N^{-1}sum B_i was 0.2441. All-null 8-clone splits 1/7,2/6,7/1 had FDR approximately q and B=0. This checks algebra and implementation on simple singular examples only. It is not a search over general PSD models, not proof of the budget, and not a recorded native verification.

Suggested disposition: verify the finite identity first; then run the two-dimensional budget screen. Record explicitly whether the positive strengthening survives, fails, or is deferred, and why.
