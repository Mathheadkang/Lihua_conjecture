Candidate route discovered while checking the finite-dimension moment-compactness limitation. No theorem is asserted. Fix q and sigma>0. For an ordinary finite model T_i=theta_i+sqrt(1-sigma^2) v_i.G+sigma Z, sample m original indices independently with replacement, preserving their means, null tags and original residual scores; apply ordinary m-test BH to this random submodel (duplicates are genuine exact copies). Conditional on G, the sampled residual-score/tag pairs are iid from the original empirical population. DKW or VC bounds control the empirical total and true-score tail CDFs uniformly over thresholds by delta, independently of N, directions, means and G. The bad event is independent of common Z. The approved common-loading proof's macroscopic rank trimming plus shift in Z should transfer the largest crossing in both directions under a small common shift; its random-cutoff band estimate should control signed full FDP, with its uniform microscopic-rank bound paying small ranks. Prove or refute an absolute FDR approximation bound E_indices |or signed difference between sampled FDR and original FDR| tending to zero with m, uniformly over all finite models at fixed q,sigma. Do not infer pathwise root continuity, monotonicity of mixed FDP, or a rate uniform as q,sigma vanish. The expectation over index samples need not be an expectation of absolute FDR error unless proved; a uniform bound on absolute difference of expectations suffices for supremum comparison.

If this compression is correct, finite-dimensional laws of marked exchangeable PSD Gram arrays may yield a cross-dimension compactification of quadratic-penalized extrema: mark each sampled index by its deterministic mean and tag; residual Gram entries are in [-1,1], diagonal 1. Weak limits preserve PSD and joint exchangeability. Mean moment bounds give tightness. Each finite sample defines an actual jointly Gaussian test vector conditional on its marked covariance; mixtures cannot exceed the finite-model supremum. Uniform sampling approximation is the needed continuity gate for a limiting full-BH functional; finite-N continuity alone does not establish it.

Potential representation source, metadata verified from primary arXiv: Dmitry Panchenko, On the Dovbysh-Sudakov representation result, arXiv:0905.1524, Electronic Communications in Probability 15 (2010),330-338, https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.1524 . The representation allows a Hilbert unit BALL and diagonal correction 1-||v||^2 (private Gaussian noise); one must not silently replace it by the approved unit-sphere common-loading population model. Marked representation, deterministic component selection, Gaussian sampling, derivative recovery, and q-scaled stationarity remain unpaid. The representation is a possible later tool only; first prove/refute the concrete uniform random-submodel compression statement using the already approved common-loading rank arguments.
