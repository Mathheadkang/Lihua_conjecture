Boundary search r1 s1. Cluster centres v_h need not be loadings. After embedding loadings in R^{d+m} (same law of T), v_i=cos(phi) w_i+sin(phi) f_i with fresh orthonormal f_i and 2-2cos(phi)=(kappa_2/u)^2 satisfies ||w_i-v_i||=kappa_2/u and ||v_i-v_j||^2=2-2cos^2(phi) w_i.w_j>(kappa_2/u)^2 even for w_i=w_j (when kappa_2/u<sqrt2). So every null is a separated singleton cluster; for m>q^{-C_2} all are rare and the cap says FDR<=A_2 q(1+u) for every unit-diagonal PSD Gaussian model. Conversely the cap's left side is at most the FDR, so the main one-sided bound implies the cap. Planning consequence: the separation hypothesis adds no structure; a proof of the cap is a proof of the main target for large m (including the global-null Simes problem under arbitrary correlation).
