Concrete candidate proof of idea_40b4875877b9; all new estimates require independent checking. Sample m original indices iid uniformly with replacement from any fixed common-loading ordinary finite model; couple full and submodel scores with the same residual G and common Z. Let H,V and H_m,V_m be total/true tail mass functions on normalized BH ranks. Conditional on G, DKW for residual scores and true-tagged residual scores (false tags encoded as -infinity) gives a Z-independent event of probability at least 1-4 exp(-2m delta^2), on which sup_{r,z} of each CDF difference is <=delta. Atom ties allowed; no uniform CDF convergence under moving means is being asserted.

Fix a in (0,1/4), 0<delta<a/2. Set L=q/phi(S^{-1}(qa/2)) and h=L delta/sigma. If a qualifying largest rank r>=a belongs to either model at z, the other model qualifies at r-delta after shifting common Z to z+h: u(q(r-delta))-u(qr)<=L delta. This must be checked for empirical CDF step/rank grid consistency; the approved largest-population crossing formulation may be used. Thus on the good event (R-R_m)_+ 1{R>=a}<=delta+R_m(z+h)-R_m(z), and conversely. Since each actual rank is nondecreasing in z and in [0,1], its Gaussian-averaged increment is <=h/sqrt(2pi). Candidate conclusion E_Z[|R-R_m|1{max(R,R_m)>=a}]<=2delta+2h/sqrt(2pi).

At their own positive largest crossings H(R)=R and H_m(R_m)=R_m exactly. Because V is a submeasure of H, same-z CDF closeness gives |V(R)-V_m(R_m)|<=|R-R_m|+2delta (using H at both ranks); hence when both ranks >=a, |D-D_m|<=2(|R-R_m|+delta)/a. For one rank below a and the other <=2a, use the approved uniform microscopic-FDP bound A(2a). If one is below a and the other >2a, rank difference exceeds a, controlled by the preceding rank estimate and Markov. Including the DKW failure event yields the candidate fully coupled bound
E_indices,G,Z |D-D_m| <= 2 A(2a)+8 delta/a+6 h/(a sqrt(2pi))+4 exp(-2m delta^2).
Here A(t)=E_Z min(1,q integral_(0,qt] dF_Z(s)/s), exactly the approved microscopic bound; A(t)->0 for fixed q,sigma. First fix a and let delta=m^(-1/4), m->infinity; then a->0. Constants may be loosened. This would prove uniform dimension-free absolute FDR compression at each fixed q,sigma, with duplicates and full mixed labels, without differentiating, choosing an independent rank, or assuming mixed FDP is monotone. A common deterministic covariance model is preserved conditional on sampled indices. No rate uniform in vanishing sigma/q, attained all-d extremum, or main order bound is yet asserted. Verify the rank-transfer/grid step first; a failure there would obstruct the argument.
