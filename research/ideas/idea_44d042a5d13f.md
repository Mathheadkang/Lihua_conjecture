Suggested by this chat; saved at the researcher's request. UNPROVED approach with explicit obstructions. It elaborates the Hermite-splitting experiment in [the fixed-level proof program](idea_10f14a1fa91d.md).

## High-degree suppression

For a standard normal survival probability $p=\bar\Phi(u)$, the squared coefficients in the Hermite expansion of $1_{\{G\ge u\}}-p$ are
$$
c_r(u)=\frac{\phi(u)^2\mathrm{He}_{r-1}(u)^2}{r!},\qquad r\ge1.
$$
For a jointly Gaussian correlation matrix $\Sigma$, count variances use the matrices $\Sigma^{\circ r}$. Large powers suppress entries whose absolute correlations are bounded away from one; exact or near copies and antipodal pairs persist. This suggests a threshold-dependent split between low-degree coherent structure and high-degree weak dependence, with near-copy groups retaining their true masses and multiplicities.

The indicator has an infinite Hermite tail, not coefficients confined to a narrow band near $u^2$. Any cutoff-dependent constants must be tracked; they cannot be declared universal.

## Positivity obstruction

A finite low-chaos truncation is generally signed. It is not automatically a positive normalized log-convex density to which Lehec's Gaussian regularization estimate applies.

Replacing the truncation by a positive OU-smoothed indicator preserves positivity but has semilogconvexity parameter of order $s^2/(1-s^2)$ at correlation parameter $s$. Taking $1-s^2$ of order $u^{-2}$ makes that parameter of order $u^2$, so the threshold-dependent loss must be included. This smoothing observation is a calculation to check formally, not a dimension-free bound.

## Exact exponential-mixture semigroup identity

For deterministic unit vectors $v_i$, masses $\omega_i$ summing to one, and $A>0$, let
$$
f_A(z)=\sum_i\omega_i\exp(A v_i\cdot z-A^2/2).
$$
For the standard OU semigroup
$P_t f(z)=\mathbb E f(e^{-t}z+\sqrt{1-e^{-2t}}G)$, direct Gaussian integration gives
$$
P_t f_A=f_{Ae^{-t}}.
$$
Thus a supremum over a finite interval of exponential-mixture tilt parameters can be represented by an OU maximal function of a special input. This is consistent with the earlier [Ehrhard and OU entrance-path observation](idea_459ca4f01a58.md); it does not by itself improve its bounds.

General OU maximal weak-$(1,1)$ theory has dimension-dependent constants: [primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.04823). [Lehec](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.04083) gives a dimension-free fixed-time estimate. Neither supplies a dimension-free maximal theorem for these mixtures. Also the original exponential kernel has a Mills-factor loss in sparse crossing regimes, as recorded in [the Gibbs-form observation](idea_c36aadbe5f06.md).

## Remaining obligation

A successful split must give a positive or otherwise controllable low-chaos object, a high-chaos bound uniform over its cutoff and all threshold scales, and a bounded near-copy contraction charge. Dense signed frames, small common loadings and arbitrary near-singular limits are required tests. No such estimate was proved in this chat.
