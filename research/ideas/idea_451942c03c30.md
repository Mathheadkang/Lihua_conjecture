Concrete global variational setup to prove/check in the next main plan, using the now approved continuity lemma; not a finite-support or main-bound claim. Fix a finite residual ambient dimension d>=1, q,sigma in the approved ranges, and the closed tagged space E_d with unit directions in R^d. On P2(E_d) maximize J_eta(pi)=F_pi-eta integral theta² d pi, eta>0. This regularizer depends only on the parameter law and is unchanged by exact uniform replication; unlike a logdet or a penalty in N it is clone invariant. A single true zero-mean atom has F=q and moment0, so sup J_eta>=q. Any maximizing sequence with J_eta>=q/2 has integral theta²<=1/eta. The second-moment sublevel is tight, and first moments are uniformly integrable; because directions lie in the compact finite-dimensional unit sphere and tags in a closed two-sheet mean domain, a subsequence converges in W1 with a P2 limit. The approved FDR continuity and lower semicontinuity of the quadratic moment make J_eta upper semicontinuous along this sequence, so a completed-law maximizer pi_eta exists. Prove the measure compactness steps, do not merely assert an optimizer. It may be non-atomic, singular and of infinite support: none of the finite PD clone formulas applies automatically.
Let W_sigma,d(q) be the canonical finite-model supremum with these fixed common/residual spaces. Approved canonical approximation gives equality with the completed P1 population supremum. Since finite canonical laws have finite second moment, comparing J_eta to each such law shows sup J_eta -> W_sigma,d(q) as eta->0. Also F_pi_eta -> W_sigma,d(q), and eta integral theta² d pi_eta=F_pi_eta-sup J_eta ->0. The rough bound eta M2<=1 implies eta integral |theta| <=sqrt(eta), uniformly in d; this can pay ordinary mean-transport penalty errors. For any admissible bounded-mean local deformation with W1 cost o(epsilon) and affected means in a fixed bounded region, the quadratic moment change is also o(epsilon), so its genuine recovered first-order FDR ascent is forbidden at pi_eta. In particular the isolated diffuse-fold prototype, if proved and if a maximizer belongs to that scoped family, yields its signed jump balance without invoking an unverified finite optimizer or a general derivative interchange. If using finite recovery under J_eta, choose finite approximants controlling second moments too; W1 convergence alone does NOT control M2. The compactly supported prototype admits such bounded-support approximants directly.
This may replace a separate Ekeland import for the variational foundation: optimize over completed laws at fixed d,sigma and derive a bound uniform in d,sigma, then pass eta->0 and remove sigma only after such a bound is actually proved. Every fixed original finite Gaussian model embeds in a finite d and is recovered as sigma->0 by its continuous Gaussian marginals; see idea_52f18a81d1a9. Remaining essential gates are first-variation/recovery at an arbitrary completed maximizer, mean-label boundaries (especially true mean0), and the q-scaled signed flux/trace payment. No uniform FDR bound, general deformation recovery, finite-dimensional support, or attained original worst-case model is asserted.
