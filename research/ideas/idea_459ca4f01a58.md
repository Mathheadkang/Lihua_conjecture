Two structural facts not used by the failed plans, recorded because neither closes the argument yet. (1) With Z = AG, log Lambda(AG) = sup_w log L_w(AG) is a supremum of log-sum-exp functions of affine maps of G, hence convex in G; by the Ehrhard inequality s -> Phi^{-1}(gamma(log Lambda <= s)) is concave. Equivalently log Lambda = sup_p [ (p.Z)_+^2/2 - KL(p||omega) ]. Concavity only gives lower bounds on the tail between anchor levels; the target needs Phi^{-1}(gamma(log Lambda<=s)) >= sqrt(2s) - O(1/sqrt s), and no anchor at high levels is available. (2) Since P_t L_a = L_{a e^{-t}} and the OU semigroup is reversible, Q_w = L_w dgamma satisfies Q_w = Q_a P_{log(a/w)}; so Q_w is the time-s marginal (s = -log w) of an OU process X with entrance law from infinity along v_I, I ~ omega/|omega|, and the log-scale occupation integral of idea_45d886a6a566 equals E int 1{Y_s in [x,x+1]} ds for the information density Y_s = log(dmu_s/dgamma)(X_s). Ito gives dY = -(w^2|m|^2 + 2 w D_w) ds + sqrt2 w m.dB, but taking expectations returns exactly the single-time identities of plan_gaussian_frozen_ray_order_bound_coarea_fisher_overlap; only a genuinely pathwise argument (stopping times, local times) along this path could add information.
