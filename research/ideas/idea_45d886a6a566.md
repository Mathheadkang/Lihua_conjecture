With $Q_w=L_w\,d\mathbb P$ and $F_w=\log L_w$, is $\int_0^\infty w^{-1}Q_w(F_w\in[x,x+1])\,dw\leq C/x$ for $x\geq2$, uniformly in $n$, $\Sigma$ and subprobability weights? By plan_gaussian_frozen_ray_order_bound_coarea_fisher_overlap (steps 1-6 proved) this bound gives the $S$-remainder $S\leq C\sqrt{\log c}$. It holds in the single-test, one-factor, independent and orthogonal-mixture families. It would fail only if the tilted log-likelihood stayed in a band for many dyadic scales of $w$, with a flat profile and a spread posterior.
