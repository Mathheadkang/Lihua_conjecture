Draft derivation to independently check, not an approved theorem or counterexample to the main conjecture. This may strengthen the representation-dependent iid warning in idea_786328bafc4b.

Let 0<q<1/2, m=ceil(exp(2/q)/q), X_i iid N(0,1), T_i=(X_i-Xbar)/sqrt(1-1/m), all one-sided null means zero. Sigma=(m I-11^T)/(m-1) is unit diagonal PSD rank m-1 with kernel 1. For ANY independent-idiosyncratic Gaussian factor representation Sigma=A A^T+D, D diagonal nonnegative (any finite factor dimension), 0=1^T Sigma 1=||A^T1||^2+sum_i D_ii forces D=0. Thus conditioning on its complete factor vector reveals every T_i and p_i=barPhi(T_i); conditional CDF is 1{p_i<=t}, so the envelope S_i=sup_{0<t<=q}F_i(t|Z)/t=1{p_i<=q}/p_i is representation invariant.

Set Y_i=min(S_i,m). Each p_i is exactly uniform. E Y_i=mu=1+log(qm)>=1+2/q and E Y_i^2=2m-1/q. Y_i is nondecreasing in T_i. Every pair T_i,T_j has negative correlation, so Cov(Y_i,Y_j)<=0 (bivariate Gaussian monotone covariance inequality; must justify). Hence Var(m^-1 sum Y_i)<=2. Chebyshev gives E min(1,(q/m)sum S_i)>=P((q/m)sum Y_i>=1)>=1-2q^2/(1+q)^2 ->1. This holds for every factor representation, even after optimizing over representations. It obstructs this capped full-factor conditional-envelope certificate on arbitrary PSD matrices.

Meanwhile actual global-null BH FDR should be q(1+o(1)), via finite-m coupling to independent X. Take delta=q^2, M=2 sqrt(log m), event |Xbar|<=delta/4 and max|X_i|<=M. Its complement <=16/(m delta^2)+2m barPhi(M)=o(q^2). On the event max|T_i-X_i|<=delta for sufficiently small q. For every BH grid t=qk/m, z_t=barPhi^-1(t) lies between z_q and M; barPhi(z_t-delta)/t <=exp(delta(M+1))=1+o(1), using Mills hazard for z_t-delta>=1. Thus any rejection for T at q implies an independent-X BH rejection at q exp(delta(M+1)); independent Simes gives FDR_T<=q(1+o(1))+o(q^2). Reverse coupling similarly suggests equality but upper bound is enough. Check all inequalities/ranges and covariance monotonicity; no claim of independent verification yet. The main conjecture is NOT refuted. Scope is full-factor conditioning with independent diagonal residuals, not arbitrary conditioning certificates or optimized methods outside this class.
