New route under examination, not a proved bound. The supremum in the main target is over every finite N, so it also bounds rationally weighted finite Gaussian families implemented by exact coordinate replication. For rational weights w_i=n_i/N summing to one, use the largest qualifying cumulative-weight crossing and weighted true-null FDP; every copy has the same Gaussian statistic and label. A crossing inside an equal-p block implies the end of that block also qualifies, so ordinary non-strict largest-rank BH on clones should equal this weighted rule exactly, including ties and singular covariances. Prove this rather than treating weighted BH as a new target.

Possible new information: near the global supremum one may add/reweight an atom with any mean and unit latent direction, with a lawful true-null/alternative label, then compare the actual FDR. Such first variations in model composition go beyond fixed-N mean/covariance KKT and might control the nonlinear inverse-Mills level velocity. A useful concrete test is to derive the full signed-face derivative for adding a tiny-weight test. In the strong-positive alternative limit, within a background cell with selected weight r and FDP f, the interior dilution derivative is -f/r; boundary displacement depends on 1-r. A never-reject atom merely rescales the level and yields only a monotonicity-type extremal constraint. Do not claim either simple perturbation proves the desired envelope.

Mandatory obligations: exact rational cloning, actual largest cumulative-rank face ledger with all signed FDP jumps and denominator derivatives, real near-extremizer/penalty control uniform in dimension, approximation of any continuously mixed added atom, correct labels including null means below zero, and an independent estimate for the nonlinear residual. This is distinct from replacing BH by blockwise optima or a convex count proxy. If no concrete estimate emerges, preserve it as a draft idea and do not write a circular plan.
