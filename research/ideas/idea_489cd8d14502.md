Pathwise, since L_0=|\omega|\le1, the positive variation of the clipped profile gives $\min\{c,\Lambda\}-1\le\int_0^\infty(\partial_wL_w)_+\mathbf 1\{1<L_w<c\}\,dw$, with equality when $w\mapsto L_w$ is unimodal. The Cameron-Martin shift at the same scale $w$ turns the expectation into $\sum_i\omega_i\int_0^\infty\mathbb E[(D_w)_+\mathbf 1\{0<\log L_w<\log c\}](Z^{i,w})\,dw$, where $D_w(x)=\mathbb E_{p^{(w)}(x)}x_J-w$; on the planted line, $D_w=\sum_jp_jZ_j-w(1-\alpha_i)$. This is exact for a single test, where it gives $\mathbb E Z_+\sqrt{2\log c}$, and for the $\sqrt q\,u+\sqrt{1-q}\,e_j$ family, where the profile is unimodal. Unlike the window and tracking route, it has no random window position. The open point is to control the extra positive variation coming from multimodal profiles $w\mapsto L_w$. Suggested after the planted-line plan failed at its spread-posterior step.
