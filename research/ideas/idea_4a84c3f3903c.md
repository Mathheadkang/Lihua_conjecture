Root current exploration, not yet specially fresh-audited and not native verified: fix the other p-values and put r=R(p_i=p_j=0) under ordinary largest-rank BH, with t_k=qk/N. The background count at t_r is exactly r-2. For integer h>=1 with r+h<=N, the number of background p-values in (t_r,t_{r+h}] is at most h-1, since otherwise r+h would also qualify. Consequently if r<N the adjacent interval (t_r,t_{r+1}] contains no background observation, with these exact endpoints. This is a deterministic maximal-rank constraint and should be reproved in the next native main plan. When residual coordinates are independent conditional on a shared Gaussian vector at positive residual variance, the empty probability of any FIXED grid gap is the product of (1-gapmass_l), bounded by exp(-sum gapmass_l). The random rank r depends on the background coordinates and must not be treated as an independent fixed gap; r=N needs separate treatment. This may support the three-face Gaussian weighted packing proposed in idea_ec681eb191fb, but neither a uniform noise-log beta matrix budget nor either main FDR target has been proved. No original statement or prior idea is changed.
