Concrete proposed proof refinement, to be independently checked in execution. Let ell be the unit residual of v_i orthogonal to span(v_j:j!=i), oriented so sigma=v_i dot ell>0 (original PD). Write G=ell S+H, H in ell-perp, independent standard Gaussians. All unchanged T_j are fixed by H. Each clone is strictly increasing affine in S for sufficiently small epsilon: slope (sigma+epsilon w_b dot ell)/sqrt(1+epsilon^2 norm(w_b)^2)>0. Its crossing of u_r is s_{b,r}^epsilon(H)=[sqrt(1+epsilon^2 norm(w_b)^2)(u_r-theta_i-epsilon c_b)-v_i dot H-epsilon w_b dot H]/(sigma+epsilon w_b dot ell); the minimum clone crosses at max_b s_{b,r}^epsilon. At zero s_r=(u_r-theta_i-v_i dot H)/sigma and crossing derivative equals -min_b(c_b+w_b dot(H+ell s_r))/sigma. Thus the original FDP finite step function in T_i, at fixed H, gives a sum of conditional Gaussian CDF increments weighted by its signed jumps; bound each root displacement/epsilon by C(1+norm(H)) for dominated convergence. On the face put z_r=u_r-theta_i and Y=H+ell s_r-v_i z_r. Then Y is perpendicular to v_i, and H=P_{ell-perp}(v_i z_r+Y). This projection has absolute (d-1)-Jacobian sigma; norm(H)^2+s_r^2=norm(Y)^2+z_r^2. Consequently phi(s_r) gamma_{ell-perp}(dH)/sigma = phi(z_r) gamma_{v_i-perp}(dY), exactly recovering the stated face density with no missing Jacobian. This argument conditions on unchanged scores before varying the threshold, so no unproved removal of moving-face intersections is needed. Exact-copy invariance and exponentially small partial-group events remain separate required steps. Constants may depend on sigma, finite grid and model; no uniform bound claimed.
