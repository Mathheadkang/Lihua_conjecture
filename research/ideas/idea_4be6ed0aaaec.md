Scope issue to check in the new global-potential proof. On an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space, an isonormal Gaussian process does not have a samplewise bounded/continuous linear functional on the whole strong unit ball. Thus a statement for EVERY lawful particle cannot be justified by silently choosing one pathwise-continuous Gaussian field or one full-probability event for all v. The private kernels are jointly measurable versions; for each deterministic v the usual Gaussian identities hold almost surely, and for each fixed directing law Fubini supplies its law-a.e. event. The proposed uniform integrated 1/h record bound, followed by E|X(v-v')|<=||v-v'||, can instead establish continuity of the averaged potential and its theta derivative. Construct limits on a countable dense parameter set, extend the averaged functions continuously, and show for each fixed particle that this extension agrees with its expectation by dominated/L1 convergence. This permits global inequalities at all particles without demanding samplewise continuity of X(v). All perturbations remain selected before innovations. This is a proof obligation, not a counterexample to the target.
