## Exact scalar calibration

Let $G$ be standard normal, let $\phi$ and $\bar\Phi$ be its density and survival function, put $h=\phi/\bar\Phi$, $\psi(x)=\phi(x)-x\bar\Phi(x)$, and define
$$
R_*=\sup_{x\ge0}(G-x)_+/\psi(x).
$$
For $a\ge2$, minimizing $x+a\psi(x)$ over $x\ge0$ gives the unique minimizer $x_a=\bar\Phi^{-1}(1/a)$ and minimum $h(x_a)$. Therefore
$$
\Pr(R_*>a)=\bar\Phi\!\left(h(\bar\Phi^{-1}(1/a))\right).
$$
The Mills expansion $h(x)=x+x^{-1}+O(x^{-3})$ implies
$\bar\Phi(h(x))/\bar\Phi(x)\to e^{-1}$ as $x\to\infty$. Hence
$$
\Pr(R_*>a)\sim\frac1{ea},\qquad \mathbb ER_*=\infty.
$$
The equality uses strict crossing; the scalar Gaussian law makes its threshold boundary null. A finite uniform expectation bound for the hinge supremum is therefore impossible, and concentration around that nonexistent expectation cannot prove its tightness. For every fixed $x$, however, $\mathbb E[(G-x)_+/\psi(x)]=1$; exchanging expectation and supremum is the error.

## Why ordinary Gaussian correlation does not solve all scales

For a centered jointly Gaussian vector with unit variances, each set
$$
C_x(a)=\left\{z:N^{-1}\sum_i(|z_i|-x)_+\le2a\psi(x)\right\}
$$
is symmetric and convex. Its marginal failure probability is at most $1/a$ by Markov, because $\mathbb E(|G|-x)_+=2\psi(x)$ for $x\ge0$.

The Gaussian correlation inequality yields
$\Pr(\bigcap_{j=1}^m C_{x_j}(a))\ge\prod_{j=1}^m\Pr(C_{x_j}(a))$.
Combining only these two statements gives $(1-1/a)^m$, which tends to zero with the number of scales. A collective or scale-adaptive estimate is required.

## Related tool limitations

Lehec's [regularization theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.04083) controls a fixed smoothed density; it does not grant a free supremum over smoothing times or tilt parameters. General OU maximal weak-$(1,1)$ results, such as [Casarino-Ciatti-Sjogren](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.04823), have dimension-dependent constants. Mulgund's [Gaussian-maximum comparison](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.14087) assumes $R-\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}/m\succeq0$ and a common threshold, and explicitly rejects an arbitrary unequal-threshold extension. These are literature scope checks, not imported approved dependencies.

The viable target remains a uniform positive acceptance probability or weak-tail control as in [the fixed-level program](idea_10f14a1fa91d.md); none of these obstructions rules that out.
