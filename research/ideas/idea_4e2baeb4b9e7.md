Round2 slot2 extremal test: cand_gaussian_bh_one_sided_universal_order_upper_r2_s2_spherical_null_grid_rare_gate is an explicit finite signed Gaussian model. Its M=L^2 zero-mean null loadings are equal-area height-azimuth representatives of S^2, L=2ceil(50q^(-20)), and its A=L alternatives are identical independent e4 loadings of positive mean 1/U. Thus null span is three and full covariance rank four; direction count diverges and alternative fraction tends to zero. Equal-area cell diameter gives cap discrepancy at most delta=q^10. For the original full largest BH crossing with no alternative rejection, t=barPhi^{-1}(qR/N)>=U and f_M(r,t)=R/M>=(barPhi(t)/q). If r<=r0=sqrt(U^2+2log U), the empirical cap upper bound is log(U)/(2U^2)+delta/2<1/(2U), whereas hazard integration gives barPhi(t)/q>=4 exp(-1/4)/(5U)>1/(2U), for U>=4. Hence an actual null-only crossing forces chi_3>r0. Any rejected alternative requires G4>=U-1/U, an event of probability below 3q. Exact radial integration plus Mills yields actual original FDR<=4q(U+1/U)+3q<=5qU. Thus NOT fails uniformly for this concrete finite family; no continuum model was substituted. The method does not handle arbitrary angular null populations, growing null rank, or many correlated rare-alternative gates: their combined selected contribution cannot be charged by this single-gate union bound. These unresolved payment issues are proof diagnostics rather than counterexamples.
