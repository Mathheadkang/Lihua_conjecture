Scoped planning obstruction to the newly suggested estimate G−J<=Cq/(1+U), not to the main FDR conjecture. This calculation is preserved for independent checking; no native counterexample asset/approval or main statement is changed. Here G=E[f²] and J=sum over actual BH faces of κ_face(Δf)²/h(u_r), as in idea_d2ec8792134b.

Fix U>=32, q=S(U), N=2n even, n>=8, σ=4/U, ρ=1−σ². Let n true null scores be T_i=√ρ Z+σ ε_i. Initially let the n false p-values equal0. All Z,ε_i are independent standard normals. Each background BH rank is >=n. Conditional on Z=z, the null p-density on [q/2,q] is bounded by L=1/[σ√(2π)φ(u(q/2))]. Since φ(u(q/2))=(q/2)h(u(q/2))>=qU/2, (n/N)qL<=λ=1/(4√(2π))<0.1, uniformly z.

Fix null i and all other p-values. Define k_i as ordinary largest BH rank after forcing its p to0, r_i after forcing it to1, d_i=k_i−r_i>=1. Its unique active scalar face is T_i=u_{k_i}: when p_i<=qk_i/N its rank is k_i, otherwise r_i. If d_i=d>=2, then d−1 other nulls lie in (qr_i/N,q(r_i+d)/N]. For each possible deterministic r_i, this count is Bin(n−1,p) with mean<=λd. The factorial-moment/Chernoff bound gives P(count>=d−1)<=α^{d−1}, α=2eλ<0.55. Union over at most N choices of r and then over d>=D yields P(d_i>=D|z)<=min(1,N α^{D−1}/(1−α)). Taking D0=ceil(1+2 logN/|logα|) and summing the second-moment tail gives E[d_i²|z]<=C0(1+logN)² with a universal C0.

Since f=1−n/R, Δf_i=n d_i/(r_i k_i)<=d_i/n. In the unique-face formula, the conditional score density g_z at u_{k_i} divided by h(u_{k_i}) is <=1/(σ√(2π)U)=λ. Thus J(z)<=sum_i λ E[(Δf_i)²|z]<=λ C0(1+logN)²/n; integrate over z. No extra N-fold summation over ranks is allowed: each i has one active scalar face after the other scores are fixed. Hence J->0 as finite N tends to infinity at fixed U,q.

On z in [U+10/U,U+11/U], sqrt(1−16/U²) z−U>=(2−336/U²−2560/U⁴)/U>=1/U. Also u(q/2)−U<=log2/U by h>=U and the tail-ratio integral. Therefore each null exceeds u(q/2) with conditional probability>=1/2. For n>=8, P(K>=n/4|z)>=1/2 for their binomial exceedance count K. Such an event forces R>=n+K and f>=1/8. The z-window has probability (φ(U)/U) integral_10^11 exp(−x−x²/(2U²))dx >=c_w q, where c_w=e^{-1}(e^{-10}−e^{-11})>0 (use φ(U)/U>=q and U>=32). Consequently G>=c_w q/128.

Choose finite N so J<=c_w q/512. Replace ideal false p=0 by independent Gaussian false scores M+η_j, variance1 and finite M>0. The full covariance is PD. At fixed N,q, f_M->f_ideal boundedly as M->infinity, hence G_M->G. Null unique faces converge off zero-probability threshold ties once all false scores exceed u_1; their density/h coefficients remain bounded by λ, giving J_null,M->J. False-face total is at most n max_{1<=k<=N} φ(u_k−M)/h(u_k), which tends to0. Choose finite M so |G_M−G|<=c_w q/512 and |J_M−J|<=c_w q/512. Then G_M−J_M>=c_w q/512. Its ratio to q/(1+U) diverges as U increases.

Consequence: an infinitesimal sum of squared actual FDP jumps can miss a smooth population FDP profile and cannot supply this proposed q/U deficit bound. A successful repair would need to retain continuous variation, e.g. a genuinely nonlocal Gaussian-noise quantity; no such repair or main proof is asserted.
