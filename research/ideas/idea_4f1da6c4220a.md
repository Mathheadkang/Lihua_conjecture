Suggested by the teaching chat while the researcher asked what methods might prove the finite universal constant goal. This is an unproved approach, not a proof, a new required result, or a change to the target. Read-only review of the failed plan plan_one_sided_order_gaussian_interpolation_sdp_20261106 shows that its H(a,eta) is ALREADY Gaussian averaged. Do not describe 'average first' as a new missing operation. The exact formula in that failed plan is F_eta'(a)=tr(Sigma H(a,eta))/(1+eta^2); its stronger sufficient gate bounds integral beta(H(a,eta)) da, where beta is a diagonal PSD completion charge. The scalar replica lemma is independently verified only at deterministic levels.

Proposed concrete first task for the proving chat: derive a finite identity grouping the mixed BH boundary faces by the same actual rank transitions and admitted sets, before taking absolute values or applying scalar density estimates. For a monotone enlargement of the rejection set from k>=1 to ell, with old false count V and newly admitted false count DeltaV, the bookkeeping identity is DeltaFDP=DeltaV/ell - V*(1/k-1/ell). Keep the new-false-rejection and old-rejection dilution terms paired. The k=0 case needs its own exact term. Explore discrete summation by parts of complete rank chains or mixed-coordinate rectangles, retaining every largest-qualifying-rank event. Apply the actual covariance contraction and Gaussian face integration to these grouped expressions; do not optimize against arbitrary correlation witnesses unless necessary. In particular alternative-alternative mixed increments need not vanish.

This is a specific proposed algebraic reorganization, not an established cancellation bound. The substantive missing theorem is a dimension-uniform Gaussian-weighted estimate of the resulting grouped signed contributions. Merely bounding the integrated actual contraction restates the endpoint inflation problem; the route earns progress only if the regrouping produces a new estimable quantity. Pure deterministic telescoping/total variation can still incur log N through alternating null and nonnull additions. Neither a pointwise O(N) completion nor a deterministic-level replica bound evaluated at a data-selected level is justified. Face cancellations here differ from cancellation of expected pivotal increments, whose negative total can only save order q.

Early decision tests: reconstruct the known N<=2 mixed kernels; include N>=3 configurations with nonzero alternative-alternative faces; exact duplicate configurations must agree with weighted atomic BH; include padding and long rank-cascade examples. Look for cancellation after Gaussian integration, not just a small raw coefficient on simple examples. If the regrouped estimate still pays for labels/faces or gives log N, record the obstruction rather than asserting finite C. No extra restrictions on dimension, theta, negative one-sided nulls, Sigma, singularity, ties, or q-dependence are allowed. Signed duplication is now independently confirmed, so a successful full one-sided order proof would supply the two-sided order as well. This teaching handoff does not drive the loop or certify any unverified part of the failed plan.
