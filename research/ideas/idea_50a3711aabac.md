Primary literature verified live: Patrick Beißner and Jan Werner, Optimal allocations with α-MaxMin utilities, Choquet expected utilities, and prospect theory, Theoretical Economics 18(3) (2023), 993–1022, DOI https://doi.org/10.3982/TE5060 ; official full text https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.3982/TE5060 . Section 2 represents directional derivatives using two convex compact sets and gives necessary maximum conditions through their inclusion, including a linear-constraint condition (7). Section 5 explicitly leaves infinite-state extensions for future research and points to general Banach-space quasidifferential work. Thus the difference-of-support-functions stationarity principle is known; do not claim it as a novel discovery or apply this finite-dimensional paper directly to L-infinity mean velocities. The proposed BH application still must prove posterior-gradient uniform integrability, countably additive L1 weak compactness, the lawful zero-mean tangent-cone limit, joint level normalization and the signed total-flux identity. This is a scoped literature lead, not an imported or approved project dependency.
