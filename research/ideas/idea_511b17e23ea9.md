## Proposed proof step
Any attempt to control the one-sided Gaussian BH FDR through the tested AH-main hugging functional must preserve the actual BH activation conditions, or first prove a replacement bound that retains them. The current search report says that the auxiliary integral diverges after these conditions are dropped, already for a single centered Gaussian true null; for that admissible N=1 model, ordinary BH rejects exactly when p<=q and hence its actual FDR equals q. Therefore the reported surrogate divergence is an obstruction to this proposed proof step, not a counterexample to gaussian_bh_one_sided_universal_order_upper.
