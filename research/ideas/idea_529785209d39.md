Possible simplification of the later signed-complementarity lemma, not yet verified. Instead of assuming measurable exact selectors from the active suffix set A(r,X), fix 0<zeta<=1 and allow rational t in [r,1) with B_X(t)<=M_X(r)+zeta. Such choices exist by continuity even when the minimizer is t=r (approximate from above). Enumerate rationals; first feasible and approximate maximizing choices are measurable because this is a countable family. For w+ and w- separately form C_zeta^+/- as the weak closed convex hulls of the integrated posterior densities together with the Mills coordinate m(u(qt)), weighted by w+/- phi(M)/sigma. The energy/active-quantile UI proof still holds uniformly in zeta<=1: on |M|<=B and bounded centre energy, Q(t)=u(qt)-sigma B_X(t) stays in a deterministic compact interval. Thus these sets are weakly compact, nonempty and nested as zeta decreases.
For a fixed bounded test (b,lambda), their support functions equal E integral (w+/-)phi(M)/sigma times sup over the zeta-near-active rational choices of {integral b p_t dmu+lambda*m(u(qt))}. Justify interchange by the explicit countable approximate maximizers. As zeta decreases to zero, every relevant near-minimizer stays in a compact t-interval away from 1 for each fixed X,r, and continuity of posterior velocities and m gives the actual max over A(r,X). Uniform boundedness by ||b||inf+|lambda|m(U) gives dominated convergence. Define C^+/- as intersection_n C_(1/n)^+/-. For nested nonempty weakly compact convex sets, support of the intersection is the decreasing limit of supports: choose maximizing points and a convergent subnet/subsequence, whose limit is in every later set. Hence the complete derivative is exactly bulk+support(C+)-support(C-), without constructing an exact active selector. All selector mean densities integrate to one at each X,r; their total signed mass invariants persist under weak limits. This preserves the total-flux identity and the cone-separation complementarity argument. Spell out joint Borel measurability of B,Q,posterior integrals and the conditional continuity; use standard Borel Gaussian innovation space. This is an original proof organization of standard compactness/selection facts, and still requires full execution and independent checking.
