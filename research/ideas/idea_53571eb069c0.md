## Purpose and provenance

This is the general-N continuation of the two-test source/residual check in idea_8303a1aba8de. The key new step is to integrate the Gaussian source in the own-null velocity directly, summing all physical background faces. It gives an exact exposure-orthogonal face velocity for every pair, with no omitted background term. The derivation is agent-produced and not independently verified in the native loop. It is a concrete kernel identity, not a final global rank budget.

The fractional-frame surface result has now been promoted natively as a draft dependency; its original proof idea remains preserved. This record neither assumes that draft as an approved black box nor edits its asset.

## Setup: exact physical covariance homotopy

Fix the full original model N, theta and Sigma, with all original true-null labels I0={i:theta_i<=0}. Put
$$
\Gamma_s=(1-s)I+s\Sigma,\quad
v_i(s)=\Gamma_s^{1/2}e_i,\quad
X_i=v_i\cdot G,\quad T_i=\theta_i+X_i,
$$
for G standard in R^N and 0<s<1. Let
$$
K_s=\tfrac12(\Sigma-I)\Gamma_s^{-1}=K_s^T,\qquad
W_i=v_i'\cdot G=K_sv_i\cdot G.
$$
For each pair set
$$
\rho_{ij}=v_i\cdot v_j,\qquad
\kappa_{ij}=Cov(W_i,X_j)=v_i\cdot K_s v_j
=\tfrac12\rho_{ij}'.
$$
In particular kappa_ii=0, W_i is independent of its own X_i, and kappa_ij=kappa_ji. All covariances and means remain the original ones at the endpoint; singular Sigma is allowed after the finite positive-definite calculation.

Let
$$
h_i(T)=\frac{1_{\{i\in\mathcal R_q\}}}{R_q\vee1}
$$
for each true-null i, retaining the actual largest nonstrict BH rank of all N physical tests. Then FDR=sum_{i in I0}E h_i. Partial derivatives below mean finite-interface distributions, justified first by mollification or direct finite-cell Gaussian integration.

## Cofactor own-null freezing and the real source

For the canonical constant-weight horizontal all-score Parseval cover from idea_8303a1aba8de, cofactor averaging gives for the i-th true-null contribution:
$$
\frac d{ds}E h_i
=\sum_{j\ne i}E[(W_j-\rho_{ij}W_i)\partial_jh_i]
+E[h_iX_iW_i]. \tag{OS}
$$
The own physical score velocity cancels from the first term. The second term is the Gaussian source; it is not discarded and has no trace term because physical weights are constant and Cov(W_i,X_i)=0.

This identity can also be checked directly without relying on a DPP black box: subtract the rank-one Gaussian flow freezing X_i and apply Gaussian integration by parts. Its purpose here is to retain the source in the same signed pairing as the cofactor residual.

## Exact all-physical-face source matching

Apply Gaussian Stein to W_i in E[W_i X_i h_i]. Because Cov(W_i,X_i)=0, the derivative of the factor X_i contributes zero. Every other physical coordinate remains:
$$
E[h_iX_iW_i]
=\sum_{j\ne i}\kappa_{ij}E[X_i\partial_jh_i]. \tag{SM}
$$
No conditional-independence assumption or inverse covariance is used. In particular the sum includes every alternative and every other null that can alter the actual full rank.

Combining (OS) and (SM) gives
$$
\boxed{\quad
FDR'(s)=
\sum_{i\in I_0}\sum_{j\ne i}
E[Z_{ij}\partial_jh_i],
\qquad
Z_{ij}=W_j-\rho_{ij}W_i+\kappa_{ij}X_i.
\quad} \tag{OF}
$$
The new velocity is orthogonal to its exposure:
$$
Cov(Z_{ij},X_j)
=0-\rho_{ij}\kappa_{ij}+\kappa_{ij}\rho_{ij}=0,
$$
whereas its correlation with the tested true-null score is
$$
Cov(Z_{ij},X_i)=\kappa_{ij}+0+\kappa_{ij}
=2\kappa_{ij}=\rho_{ij}'.
$$
Thus Z_ij is a centered Gaussian velocity independent of X_j, exactly the hypothesis required by the marked scalar kernels. This is true for all N; the earlier two-test warning about possible missing background faces is resolved by the full sum in (SM), not by deleting those faces.

## Actual face coefficient, both thresholds and arbitrary means

For each physical j, force its p-value to zero and to one while keeping all other physical p-values. Let b=r_j^+ and a=r_j^- be the corresponding largest ranks, with b>=a. When j crosses the physical threshold T_j=u(qb/N), the i-th actual null-share increment is exactly
$$
\Delta h_i=
\frac{1_{\{T_i\ge u(qb/N)\}}}{b}
-\frac{1_{\{T_i\ge u(qa/N)\}}}{a\vee1},
$$
where the second term is zero if a=0. The background mask specifying (a,b) retains every largest-rank compatibility condition; no arbitrary self-consistent selector is substituted.

In (OF), the exposure is X_j at the standardized level y=u(qb/N)-theta_j, with arbitrary original theta_j. Each tail of Delta h_i involves the actual true-null threshold X_i>=u(qr/N)-theta_i>=u(qr/N), since theta_i<=0. Thus negative null means are preserved and arbitrary positive or negative means of j are not restricted.

## Applying the centered determinant-scaled scalar kernel correctly

Let sigma_ij=sqrt(1-rho_ij^2) and nu_ij=sqrt(Var Z_ij). Since Z_ij is independent of X_j, idea_1ed1245e80c7 applies after normalization. For u>=1 and every actual-rank mark M in [0,1],
$$
\sigma_{ij}\phi(y)
E[|Z_{ij}-h(u)\rho_{ij}'|
1_{\{X_i+\theta_i\ge u\}}M\mid X_j=y]
\le C\,\nu_{ij}\barPhi(u). \tag{CFK}
$$
The exact drift h(u)rho_ij' is retained. Applying this to the two terms of Delta h_i uses their own ranks a,b and their own quantiles, rather than one common center. The coefficient rho_ij' is the actual covariance derivative; it is not an independently optimized Gram contraction.

There can be an additional Gaussian component of Z_ij orthogonal to (X_i,X_j). Explicitly,
$$
Z_{ij}=\frac{\rho_{ij}'}{\sigma_{ij}}
\frac{X_i-\rho_{ij}X_j}{\sigma_{ij}}+R_{ij},
$$
with R_ij orthogonal to both physical pair scores. It is included in nu_ij and in (CFK), not silently removed. Singular-pair endpoint divisions require limits; the finite homotopy has positive-definite Gamma_s.

## What remains

The exact source matching supplies a genuine all-N exposure-orthogonal marked face kernel. It does not yet sum the centered terms over all actual physical ranks and pairs, nor pay the signed Mills drift. Summing (CFK) after dropping masks can still lose dimension or a logarithmic endpoint factor. Averaging all determinant cofactors too early can erase the determinant gain, while taking absolute values too early can erase source cancellation.

A continuation must retain the original signed actual-face differences and the global DPP mass/cofactor structure while allocating (CFK) and the explicit drift. This record does not rename that remaining allocation as an established theorem and does not create a pending main plan or change any native stage.
