In independent one-factor blocks, Lei's good-event range bound (Lemma 4.7/(269)) breaks when other blocks supply a frozen background B(u) independent of the null's factor. With sharpened (Bernstein) crossing conditions, the block-pivotal part should reduce to Lemma 4.5/4.6 for the block's own measure; the open part is the background-pivotal regime where B is within relative eta+eps_k of the BH line at the tilt-matched level. Needed: (1) a version of Lemma 4.5 with the line p/q replaced by the deficit D=p/q-B (only p/q-D nonincreasing is known), and (2) an anti-concentration bound, uniform over other blocks (loadings up to one, arbitrary means, any number of blocks), on the tilt-weighted probability that the background hugs the line; or a coupling to a Z_g-independent rank in that regime. See the Failure section of plan_two_sided_exogenous_pivotal_blocks_20261105.
