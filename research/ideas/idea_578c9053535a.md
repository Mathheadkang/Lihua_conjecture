Primary source checked directly (scoped source-based citation check, not an imported/approved theorem): Alexander Shaposhnikov, Some remarks on Talagrand's convolution conjecture, arXiv:2609.11290v1, 10 September 2026, https://arxiv.org/html/2609.11290v1 . Exact relevant locations: Lemma 2.3, Corollary 2.4, Theorem 2.5 in Section 2. Title/author/version/date and those original passages were opened; no bibliography-wide or retraction audit claimed.

The source proves conditional exponential/second-moment identities for a bounded progressively measurable Brownian drift u with pathwise integral from 0 to 1 equal to zero. If M=int u.dB and A=int|u|^2 dt, then E[exp(lambda M-lambda^2 A/2)|B1]=1, so E[M|B1]=0 and E[M^2|B1]=E[A|B1]. It uses a stopped density martingale and an adapted return on a fixed remaining interval. Its Theorem 2.5 bounds a FIXED-TIME heat-smoothed density tail by 1/[eta sqrt(2(1-s)log eta)]. This is not a maximal-in-time theorem and does not settle the frozen-ray conjecture.

Proposed BH use, not proved: take the terminal weight to be the FULL original FDP h(B1), with original labels, actual largest rank and covariance representation. Conditional-on-B1 identities then preserve that whole weight; they do not discard background faces or identify a signed current with an unknown endpoint FDR difference. For f=h/Eh, the tilted-law entropy is at most log(1/Eh); in the only nontrivial case Eh>=q this is <=log(1/q). A fixed-time adapted return could therefore retain dimension-free energy while cancelling signed terminal-weight terms.

FIRST UNPROVED GATE: construct an explicit adapted zero-displacement control and exact identity connecting the all-score BH source/face current to a weighted martingale charge with the necessary q factor. The source does not supply that BH-specific construction. A generic entropy estimate alone, or assigning q to an uncomputed endpoint flux, is insufficient. Any candidate must pass iid-null (including growing N), physical replication, the frustrated small-model checks, and the approved positive-path counterexample before becoming a proof plan. If the construction only restates the old masked signed aggregation gap, do not promote it.
