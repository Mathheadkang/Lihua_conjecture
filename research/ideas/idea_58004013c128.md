Local analytic obstruction, not a counterexample to either conjecture. Exact proof: research-local/gaussian_bh_shared_threshold_envelope_obstruction_20261104.md. With N independent Gaussian boundary nulls of total mass 1/2, empirical null subcdf G_N(t)=(2N)^(-1)sum1{U_i<=t}, relax the full BH rule to any common t<=q satisfying G_N(t)<=t/q<=G_N(t)+1/2. The supremum M of qG_N(t)/t has liminf_N EM >= (q/2)E1(q/2) = (q/2)log(1/q)-O(q), by evaluating at the smallest uniform p-value on q/(2N)<U_(1)<q/2. Actual valid mixed independent Gaussian BH, with N independent mean-one alternatives, has exact FDR q/2 by the full-rank leave-one-out identity. The relaxed lower bound persists under strictly positive noisy-clone variance via strict-threshold approximation and Fatou. Fixed q precedes N limit; admissible diagonal N(q),eta(q) is explicit by existence. Root independently checked the order-statistic integral, dominated convergence, contact constraints and smoothing argument. No manuscript/asset approval or unrestricted bound. Thus even a shared threshold plus exact necessary null feasibility and alternative total mass loses the sharp scale; retain the actual Gaussian alternative cdf and largest contact.
