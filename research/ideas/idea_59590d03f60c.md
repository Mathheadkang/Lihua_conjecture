New proposed elementary architecture for the exact iid equal-weight target. This is a DRAFT derivation, not a verified proof; it refines the critical collective gap and reuses the clipped-envelope intuition of [the late-threshold approach](idea_978f04065c8e.md), [the planted-SNR program](idea_dc6cf14ab94d.md), and [the tail calibration](idea_76d8bdfb8584.md). It is distinct from a fixed-time regularization application and does not use Eldan-Lee or Lehec as a black box.

## Critical truncation
For K>=2 put u=sqrt(2 log K). For iid standard Z_i define
B(w)=K^{-1} sum_i exp(w Z_i-w^2/2) 1{Z_i<=u}, B*=sup_{w>=0} B(w),
X_i=K^{-1} exp(Z_i^2/2) 1{Z_i>u}, R=sum_i X_i.
Then Lambda<=B*+R pointwise, and each summand f_i(w)=K^{-1} exp(wZ_i-w^2/2)1{Z_i<=u} lies in [0,1] for every w>=0. K=1 is the separately exact scalar case.

## First quantitative gate: uniform mean of B*
For X_w=(Z-w)exp(wZ-w^2/2)1{Z<=u}, E X_w=-phi(u-w)<=0. Thus
E(B'(w))_+ <= sqrt(E X_w^2/K).
On 0<=w<=u/2, E X_w^2 <= (1+w^2)exp(w^2), so the integral of this upper bound is at most
(u/2+u^2/8)exp(-u^2/8), uniformly bounded over u>=sqrt(2log2).
On w>=u/2, substitute Z=u-s to get the EXACT formula
E X_w^2/K=(2pi)^(-1/2) exp(-(w-u)^2)
 *int_0^infty (u-w-s)^2 exp(-(2w-u)s-s^2/2) ds.
Because 2w-u>=0, the last integral is at most C((u-w)^2+1). Its square-root integral in w is bounded by a universal constant. Tonelli and pathwise total positive variation then give E B*<=E B(0)+int E(B')_+ <= C_B universally. All constants and measurability/limits must be written out. This directly controls the previously unpaid COLLECTIVE subcritical/critical contribution; it is not a single-leader asymptotic.

## Sharp truncated mean
Since min(c,Lambda)<=B*+min(c,R) and min(c,sum X_i)<=sum min(c,X_i),
E min(c,R)<= [sqrt(log(Kc))-sqrt(log K)]/sqrt(pi)
 +c K S(sqrt(2log(Kc))).
The bracket is <=sqrt(log c), and the remaining term is universally bounded by Mills' inequality. Therefore the proposed argument gives the exact requested coefficient
E min(c,Lambda)<=sqrt(log c/pi)+C,
not merely an order bound. This identity uses X_i=0 when Z_i<=u, so it must not silently restore the removed scalar interval.

## Maximal tail: bulk variance and rare-count mixture
For B* let B*_{-i}=sup_w sum_{j!=i} f_j(w). Pointwise 0<=B*-B*_{-i}<=1 and sum_i(B*-B*_{-i})<=B*: choose an actual common maximizing w, or use approximate maximizers and a limit. Tensorized variance/Efron-Stein in the deletion form then gives Var B*<=sum_i E(B*-B*_{-i})^2<=E B*<=C_B. Thus P(B*>=alpha/2)<=C/alpha^2 for large universal alpha; smaller alpha can be absorbed into C_tail. Supply an elementary tensorized variance proof inline, or verify a precise existing source before relying on it.

Let M=#{i:Z_i>u}, p=S(u), lambda=Kp<=C. Conditional on M=m the positive marks X_i are iid copies of exp(Z^2/2)/K given Z>u. For m<=sqrt(alpha), union bound at alpha/(2m) (adjust alpha>=16) and Mills give
P(R>=alpha/2|M=m)<= C m^2/[Kp alpha sqrt(log alpha)].
This follows because alpha/(2m)>=sqrt(alpha)/2 and log(K alpha/(2m))>=c log alpha for universal sufficiently large alpha. Averaging yields E M^2/(Kp)=1+(K-1)p<=1+C. On M>sqrt(alpha), binomial fourth moments are uniformly bounded when lambda<=C, giving probability <=C/alpha^2. Hence P(R>=alpha/2)<=C/[alpha sqrt(log alpha)] and the same for Lambda. Explicitly check all thresholds, zero-count case, and universal finite-alpha adjustment; this is currently a proposed calculation.

## Scope and disposition
If independently validated, this supplies BOTH exact assertions of gaussian_frozen_ray_independent_equal_weights with no dependence on K, c, alpha and with no SNR truncation. It uses iid at the derivative variance, deletion variance, and conditional rare-count steps. Nor does it reduce arbitrary finite Gaussian BH to (T), or remove the private-noise loss in (S). It adds no completion goal.
