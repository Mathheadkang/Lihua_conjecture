The first search round's two failed candidates are duplicate scalar zero-mean true-null checks: each passes H1--H7 and fails NOT, with the reported derivative -phi(1){1-m(1)}. Their actual finite-copy and scalar population calculations give a narrow obstruction to using that scalar endpoint/fractional-rank mechanism as a counterexample, not a proof for general models. A possible proof route is to identify exactly which order/comparison fact makes the scalar finite-copy largest crossing converge, then prove a replacement for the common global largest crossing with exact background atoms. Only after that bridge is established can the proposed local contact minimization produce the signed face derivative. The bridge must retain the zero-offset atom, non-strict BH inequalities, endpoint rank N, root births/switches/ties and fractional FDP; rank-by-rank minimization alone is insufficient. The N=2 mixed-label fractional-FDP calculation remains unfinished and the separate open test case retained by the root is not verified. This is a draft approach, with no general comparison or variation proof claimed.
