Unverified plan-stage observation (12 November 2026). (1) Strength: K_flat(q)^2 4^{K_flat(q)} q <= (log(1/q))^2 q^{3/4}/(4 log 4)^2, so by the approved clone-block Schur-square variation bound M(q) <= 1/8 for small q. Since V_-(f) >= f(0)-f(1) and F_q(Sigma o Sigma) <= q by lib_chi2024_positive_gaussian_simes (nonnegative entries), compression would give F_q(Sigma) <= q + 1/4 for every Sigma at one small q, and gaussian_simes_fixed_level_amplification then gives the uniform linear Simes bound. So a proof of compression must contain a proof of the main all-null conjecture plus oscillation control; it is not a reduction step that can be planned with a local mechanism. (2) Mechanisms checked and failing: (a) closure/lower semicontinuity: V_- is lsc under pointwise convergence of profiles, which would help only if Sigma were a limit of models with at most K blocks, but for fixed N that set is closed, so Sigma with more than K distinct directions is not such a limit. (b) Mixture convexity V_-(E g_xi) <= E V_-(g_xi): would need N(0,R_t^Sigma) as a mixture of few-block Gaussian laws along the whole path; a mixture of centered Gaussian laws is not Gaussian unless trivial, and conditioning on part of the tensor realization produces nonzero means, non-unit variances and t-dependent conditioning, outside M(q). (c) Angular clustering at scale kappa/u (as in the partial entropy bound) or subsampling with replacement: the number of clusters is unbounded (sphere entropy), well-separated clusters are only approximately independent in the tail, and merging clusters of angle >> 1/u changes the profile at order one; subsampling to K_flat coordinates changes the Simes thresholds qk/N. Every remaining route bounds f' by a sum over coordinate pairs or over threshold crossings, which grows with N (idea_3829e8b6542a).
