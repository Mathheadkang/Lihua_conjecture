A genuinely different proposed route after the unrestricted trace budgets and local/fibre payments failed. Analyze near-worst models of the actual FDR rather than require a stronger auxiliary inequality at every Gaussian model. This is a proposed adaptation, not a theorem attributed to the sources.

Verified analytic source: Felix Voigtlaender, A general version of Price's theorem, official arXiv:1710.03576v2 (1 June 2020), https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.03576v2; Theorem1 on p.3, proof in Section4. Publisher DOI10.1007/s10959-020-01017-w identifies J.Theor.Probab34(2021)1474-1485. It justifies smooth covariance dependence for bounded Gaussian functionals and distributional derivatives in the positive-definite cone. It gives no extremal BH bound and no automatic singular extension. Gaussian-density differentiation can be reproved inline. The Roquain-Villers exact-calculation/LFC source DOI10.1214/10-AOS847 was checked at its published abstract: it uses independence/equicorrelated models and a hypothesis mixture, so its least-favourable conclusions cannot be borrowed for arbitrary covariance.

Concrete regularized object to verify: for fixed finite N and q, optimize over all frozen null-label sets I0, closed mean orthants theta_i<=0 for true labels and theta_i>=0 otherwise, and positive-definite unit-diagonal Sigma, the objective L=F_{I0}(q;theta,Sigma)-epsilon log(1+||theta||^2)+(eta/N)log det Sigma. The log mean penalty and covariance barrier suggest an attained finite/PD optimizer while permitting approximation of every original finite model as epsilon,eta go to zero. Any alternative label at mean zero could be relabeled true, strictly increasing F at finite PD parameters without changing penalties; this suggests the joint optimizer has genuine positive alternative means. Reprove this reduction rigorously, including label changes, negative interior null means, divergent means and singular covariance. Do not assert an unpenalized global optimizer exists.

At a verified optimizer define g=gradient_theta F and the symmetric Frechet covariance gradient C=(1/2)Hessian_theta F. A diagonal multiplier Lambda should satisfy C+(eta/N)Sigma^{-1}=Lambda and tr(Lambda)-tr(C Sigma)=eta. The mean equations give theta dot g<=2epsilon. For indices J whose means are interior to their allowed orthants, second-order mean optimality gives Hessian F restricted to J <=2epsilon I. With Q=(FDP/F)gamma_N in latent coordinates and w=Sigma^{-1/2}z for z supported on J, Gaussian score identities then suggest E_Q(w dot G)^2 <=||w||^2+(2epsilon/F)||z||^2 <=(1+2epsilon N/F)||w||^2. This is only a projected approximate moment bound; it does NOT give global Cov_Q<=I or a Poincare constant. It could supply a legitimate restricted condition that generic entropy-stability sources previously lacked. All these suggested structural claims require full proof.

Possible substantive gate: compute exact q-derivatives of the optimized objective from actual maximal-rank BH faces, then use the first/second-order mean and covariance constraints to cancel problematic signed contributions before estimating them. An adequate explicit target would be an optimized-envelope inequality E(q)-q E'(q)<=C q/[1+U(q)] plus controlled regularization errors, with universal C. Its integration from one fixed q0 would imply E(q)<=C' q[1+U(q)], since integral ds/[s(1+U(s))] is O(U(q)). This is an unproved test, not an assumed envelope theorem. A different valid use could restrict a mixed-trace estimate to genuinely high-FDR stationary models. No claim is made that stationarity alone closes either gate. Retain exact penalties, fixed labels while differentiating, all finite quantifiers, covariance boundary terms, and every original main target; no new mandatory auxiliary root is proposed.
