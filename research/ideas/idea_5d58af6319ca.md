Proposed strengthening of idea_2cbf52aaf6bc, not yet proved. TV continuity alone does not make full-atom small mean translations cheap: their TV distance is the full atom weight even when displacement tends to zero. Rare clones instead have W1 cost O(epsilon²), so the natural near-maximal domain is a complete Wasserstein-1 space. Fix a common independent Gaussian loading sigma>0 and residual unit directions in a separable Hilbert space. Parameter cost |theta-theta'|+||v-v'||+1(label differs); require finite first moment. Completeness subtlety: canonical false means theta>0 are not closed at zero. For a closed tagged extension allow true-labelled theta<=0 and false-labelled theta>=0, including false-tagged zero only in the completion. These extra finite models have FDP no larger than the same data model with zero tags restored to true; conversely false-zero tags may be approximated by strictly positive false means. Thus any claimed equality of suprema should be proved using domination and continuity, never silently redefine the original null set. Unit-sphere directions with the tagged closed mean sets form a complete separable parameter space, hence its P1 Wasserstein space is complete and finite support laws are dense. Candidate continuity argument: optimally couple pi_n and pi. Under the same residual Gaussian field, expected coupled absolute score difference is <=E|delta theta|+sqrt(1-sigma²)E|X(delta v)| <=C W1; label disagreement also vanishes. For a fixed rank r>0, conditioning on residual field and integrating common Z gives expected indicator disagreement <=C_sigma E|delta score|. A finite rank grid on [a,1], monotonicity of H, and the unconditional unit-variance Gaussian marginal density bound control expected sup_r|H_n-H| and |V_n-V| as mesh and W1 tend to zero (quantile derivative bounded on [a,1]). Extract subsequences and use the common-Z rank sandwich from idea_2cbf52aaf6bc at z±eta, plus monotone-rank continuity a.e z. Cutoff numerator atoms have zero mass a.e z by Fubini: for any fixed parameter score strictly increases while cutoff u(qR(z)) decreases. The conditional-BY microscopic-rank estimate from idea_9e99723c91e9 is uniform over parameter laws, including non-atomic laws, and should finish full FDR convergence. Measurability of largest root can be handled by descending iteration r0=1, r_{k+1}=H(r_k); H is right-continuous monotone so limit is its largest fixed point/qualifier. If all this is proved, bounded continuous FDR admits an Ekeland near-maximizer at fixed sigma; rare-clone W1 cost O(epsilon²) would pay no first-order penalty. Remaining serious gate: the Ekeland law may be non-atomic or singular, while current rare-clone formula is finite PD and acts on one positive atom. Need a valid first-variation extension/localization or finite near-maximal transfer, not an assumed finite optimizer. Also require q-scaled zero-null boundary flux, and a bound uniform in sigma before removing regularization. Do not use the existing rare formula directly at an arbitrary completion point.
