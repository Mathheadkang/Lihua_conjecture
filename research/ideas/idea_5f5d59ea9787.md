Candidate extension derived during source analysis, not yet independently verified and not a theorem stated in the source. Model T_i=theta_i+a_i^T Z+sqrt(1-||a_i||^2)epsilon_i, Z standard Gaussian in arbitrary d, epsilon_i independent and independent of Z; one-sided true null theta_i<=0. Suppose nonzero true-null loadings occupy H oriented rays a_i=r_i v_h, 0<r_i<=1; alternative loadings unrestricted. For q<1/2, u=barPhi^{-1}(q), conjectured finite-q upper bound FDR <= q[H+pi0(1/2+u/sqrt(2pi))]. Proof route: conditional LOO identity given full Z; group null FDP by direction Y_h=v_h^T Z; on Y_h>u bound each group's FDP by 1, costing q per ray; on Y_h<=u source's exact one-dimensional tail-ratio integral applies per null, with coefficient (1-r_i)/sqrt(1-r_i^2)<=1. No independence among Y_h required. Composite nulls handled by pointwise conditional CDF domination only, not global BH monotonicity. Handle r_i=1 separately (no rejection on Y_h<u) and r_i=0 directly. If valid, H=o(sqrt(log(1/q))) preserves one-sided constant 1/sqrt(pi), even growing latent dimension; original sharp lower construction embeds. Need separate asset, fresh counterexample search and proof review, and novelty comparison. Does not solve unrestricted growing direction complexity or sharp two-sided factor 1/2.
