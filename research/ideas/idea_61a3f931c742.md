Draft family-specific obstruction, not a proof of the unrestricted conjecture. It concerns two opposed signal streams, including arbitrary q-dependent loading angles, and a growing rather than fixed number of deterministic signal means.

Let Z,W and all null residuals be independent standard normals. Null coordinates have mean zero and value rZ+sqrt(1-r^2)E_i. Signal streams have unit-variance factors Y_+=rho Z+sqrt(1-rho^2)W and Y_-=-rho Z+sqrt(1-rho^2)W, with arbitrary rho=rho(q) in [0,1]. Give both streams the same mean ladder mu_j=(1+r)b_j. At each ladder point require each stream's upper-tail coordinate fraction to be at least 2p(b_j)/q, where p(c)=2PhiBar(c).

A concrete finite construction is r=q^2, u=p^{-1}(q), R=2u, J=ceil(R), b_0=p^{-1}(qr/4), b_j=b_0+j/r, N=ceil(exp(q^{-10})). Set w_j=2[p(b_j)-p(b_{j+1})]/q for j<J and w_J=2p(b_J)/q. Each stream uses ceil(Nw_j) copies at mu_j; all remaining coordinates are null. The signal fractions sum to r up to rounding O(J/N), and all means, counts and covariance entries are deterministic. The covariance is a two-factor Gram matrix plus independent null residual diagonal, with unit diagonal and exact joint Gaussianity.

The identity max(Y_+,Y_-)=rho|Z|+sqrt(1-rho^2)W>=-|W| is decisive. On |W|<=R, choose j minimally such that rb_j>=|W|. The stream with the larger factor has every ladder point l>=j above Gaussian cutoff b_j, so qF_N(b_j)/p(b_j)>=2 for the GLOBAL empirical CDF. The ordinary BH cutoff is therefore at most b_j, and rb_j<=|W|+2 for small q. The same nonnull-only cap holds after replacing any null p-value by zero. Taking an earlier crossing is beneficial for this bound; separate stream FDRs are never added.

On the factor box |Z|,|W|<=R, uniformly for u<=c<=b_j and a=rc, Mills bounds give
$$
h_{r,z}(c)/p(c)\leq\{1+o(1)\}e^{-a^2/2}\cosh(az)
\leq2e^{-a^2/2+a|z|}.
$$
Here a<=R+2 and |z|<=R; the exponent error is O(r^2R^2)=o(1), and the Mills prefactor is 1+O(u^{-2}+r). Thus the conditional leave-one-null BH identity bounds the integrated FDP on this box by 2q E K_2(Z,W), where
$$
K_2(z,w)=\sup_{0<=a<=|w|+2}\exp(-a^2/2+a|z|).
$$
This expectation is finite because W is independent of the null factor Z. Writing x=|Z| and y=|W|, K_2<=e^{2x}K_0 with K_0=e^{x^2/2} for x<=y and K_0=e^{-y^2/2+xy} for x>y. Integration against (2/pi)e^{-(x^2+y^2)/2} gives the explicit finite bound
$$
E K_2\leq4e^4\Phi(2)^2+\sqrt{2/\pi}\{e^2\Phi(2)-1/2\}.
$$
The factor-box complement has probability at most 4PhiBar(2u)=q^{4+o(1)}=o(q). Hence the exact finite Gaussian arrays have FDR=O(q), uniformly in every schedule rho(q), and their q sqrt(log(1/q)) normalization tends to zero.

This does not cover unequal mean ladders, independently varying nonnull residuals, or general multiple loading directions. Its value is to expose the global-crossing obstruction in the smallest attempted doubling construction: two opposite null-factor couplings do not supply two additive local gains when their nonnull ladders jointly cap the single global BH threshold.
