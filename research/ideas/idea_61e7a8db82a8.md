Same q sqrt(log(1/q)) shape for a null-only functional, but different functionals: the frozen-ray supremum of likelihood-ratio averages versus the conditional cluster intensity K_0(Y). Taking Y = the null vector and singleton clusters compares K_0 to Lambda only up to a factor of order u, so the frozen-ray bound does not give the carrier bound.
