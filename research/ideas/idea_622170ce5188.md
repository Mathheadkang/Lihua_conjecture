Round 3 gives two distinct obstructions: the zero-sum regular-simplex candidates report P(E_q) <= 2 e q for q <= 1/(2e), while two arbitrarily dependent signed Gaussian carriers give at most four distinct p-value formulas and hence P(E_q) <= 4q. The elementary general step is that, with at most L distinct standard-uniform formulas among the coordinates, a BH crossing forces one formula to be <= q, giving P(E_q) <= Lq regardless of dependence. Thus a bounded number of carriers cannot yield the required unbounded rejection-to-level ratio, even with arbitrarily many repetitions. A possible proof route must replace this count-dependent union bound by a Gaussian-geometric or dependence-comparison argument controlling the union over BH crossing ranks with a universal constant when the number of carriers, covariance rank, and dimension all vary with q. The simplex bounds and prior independent-block bounds do not justify such a comparison. This records the missing proof step, not a proved comparison or proof of the conjecture; simplex estimates were supplied as candidate failure lines in the round-3 summary pack.
