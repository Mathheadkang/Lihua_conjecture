For the signed-duplication reduction, separate each pair into its smaller member p_i/2 and its larger member at least 1/2. At q<1/2 no larger member can qualify at any BH threshold. Compare the original inequality p_(k)<=qk/N with the duplicated inequality (p_(k)/2)<=qk/(2N) for k<=N, then verify the largest qualifying rank and threshold rejection set, including all ties and zero rejections. Once this deterministic equality is proved, original zero-mean nulls map to zero-mean signed nulls, giving false-rejection domination. This is an approach suggested by first-round search obstructions, not a proved result or a bound for either main FDR target.
