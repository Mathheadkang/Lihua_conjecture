Candidate proof step motivated by the round5 unresolved all-pairwise-difference family. Not yet independently verified or promoted. Suppose centered unit-variance Gaussian coordinates admit a RANDOM subset S, chosen from deterministic parameter geometry independently of the Gaussian observations, such that each index belongs to S with probability at least p>0 and every fixed S has nonnegative internal correlations. Let E_q be the full Simes event; on it let A be the ACTUAL BH rejection set and r=|A|>0. Conditional on the Gaussian vector, E_S |A intersect S|>=p r and |A intersect S|<=r. Hence P_S{|A intersect S|>=p r/2}>=p/(2-p)>=p/2. On this event the S submodel qualifies at level q'=2q/p: K=|A intersect S| selected p-values are all <=q r/N<=q' K/|S|, since |S|<=N. Thus (p/2)P(E_q)<=E_S P(E_{q'}^S)<=q', using the known positive-Gaussian Simes inequality. For q'>=1 use the trivial probability bound; the final proposed estimate is P(E_q)<=4q/p^2. Check ties, empty S, q-range and the positive-correlation source before making this an approved lemma.

Concrete geometry: if every unit loading v_i in R^d has at most s nonzero coordinates, sample independent Rademacher signs epsilon_j and keep i precisely when epsilon_j (v_i)_j>=0 for every nonzero coordinate. Each index is kept with probability 2^{-support_size}>=2^{-s}; any two kept loadings become coordinatewise nonnegative after the same sign flip, so their dot product is nonnegative. This proposes the uniform-in-N,d bound 4^{s+1} q for every fixed sparsity s, with growing rank and arbitrarily many distinct directions. For all ordered differences T_ij=(Z_i-Z_j)/sqrt2, take the random directed cut {i positive sign,j negative sign}; p=1/4 suggests 64q. This could close the round5 NOT check that existing finite-partition arguments did not settle.

The constant still depends on s (or the fractional cover probability). Arbitrary dense-loading Gaussian models need not have a dimension-free p; sign graphs with tiny correlations can be arbitrarily complex. This is NOT a proof of the unrestricted linear conjecture or mixed FDR bound. Its novelty is unverified; it may be an instance of existing fractional-cover multiple-testing arguments. Primary positive-Gaussian Simes source already checked: Chi–Ramdas–Wang2024, section3.3 Theorem8(I), author PDF https://sas.uwaterloo.ca/~wang/papers/2024Chi-Ramdas-Wang-BJ.pdf . The theorem must be imported/approved or an existing approved equivalent identified before use as a proof dependency.
