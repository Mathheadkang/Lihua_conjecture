Round3 slot1 boundary-degenerate multivariate diagnostic for gaussian_bh_one_sided_universal_order_upper. Scope: exact small-loading null-tail limits and a fixed-d isotropic angular-envelope asymptotic, with a local rare-alternative crossing diagnostic. These are not finite-model counterexamples, do not assume a general frozen-ray maximal inequality, and do not establish the unrestricted target. No candidate is written because no complete finite Gaussian sequence with a decided NOT check has emerged.

1. Multivariate null limit. Let d>=2 be fixed, G in R^d standard Gaussian, v in S^{d-1}, and T_v=r v.G+sqrt(1-r^2)epsilon_v with independent private residuals. Write S for the normal survival function. For fixed g, a>0, and bounded h, the exact conditional null ratio at c=a/r+h is

S((c-r v.g)/sqrt(1-r^2))/S(c) -> exp(a v.g-a^2/2).

Indeed (c-r v.g)/sqrt(1-r^2)=c+r(a/2-v.g)+O(r^2), so the difference of squared cutoffs tends to a^2-2a v.g and Mills ratios tend to one. The convergence is uniform in v on the compact sphere, and in a in compact subsets of (0,infinity), for bounded g,h. Hence a null angular probability measure nu has limiting normalized conditional curve L_nu(a,g)=exp(-a^2/2) integral exp(a v.g)nu(dv). The supremum remains outside the angular integral; replacing it by exp(||g||^2/2) discards angular dilution. A finite directional mixture satisfies the same local limit with its own empirical nu, but this statement gives no uniform empirical BH approximation.

2. Fixed-d isotropic angular dilution, calculated rather than assumed. Let nu be uniform on S^{d-1}, R=||g||, k=(d-1)/2, and define I_d(t)=integral exp(t v_1)nu(dv). Its exact one-dimensional integral has density C_d(1-u^2)^((d-3)/2) on [-1,1]. Endpoint Laplace substitution gives I_d(t)~A_d exp(t)t^(-k), where A_d=Gamma(d/2)2^((d-3)/2)/sqrt(pi). Thus L_d(a,R)=exp(-a^2/2)I_d(aR). Completing the square localizes its maximizing a near R; differentiating the Laplace expansion gives a_R=R-k/R+O_d(R^(-3)). In particular

M_d(R):=sup_{a>=0}L_d(a,R)~A_d exp(R^2/2)R^(-(d-1)) as R->infinity, for each fixed d.

The chi_d radial density is f_d(R)=B_d R^(d-1)exp(-R^2/2), B_d=2^(1-d/2)/Gamma(d/2), so M_d(R)f_d(R)->A_d B_d=1/sqrt(2pi). This explicitly cancels the misleading high-dimensional radial prefactor. Also M_d is nondecreasing in R (each L_d is). For fixed lambda>0 and q->0, the large root R_q of lambda q M_d(R_q)=1 satisfies R_q~sqrt(2log(1/q)). Therefore

E min(1,lambda q M_d(||G||)) ~ (lambda/sqrt(pi))q sqrt(log(1/q)).

To check this last step: the interior integral is lambda q integral_0^R_q M_d(R)f_d(R)dR~lambda q R_q/sqrt(2pi), while the chi tail is asymptotic to lambda q/(sqrt(2pi)R_q), of smaller order. Constants in this fixed-d asymptotic do not assert uniformity in growing d. This is a limiting null-envelope calculation; it is not a claim that actual finite BH FDR attains this value.

3. Naive multi-direction Lei rare alternatives encounter earlier crossings. For finitely many alternative loading directions -w, take a mean profile mu=(1+r)a/r with exact survival W_r(a,w)=g(a,w)S(a/r)/q on a local interval, where g is fixed, positive and continuous there, and assume W_r is a valid decreasing survival profile. At score c=x/r+h, the exact conditional alternative survival samples beta=r(c+w.g)/(1+r). Its normalized local contribution is g(beta,w)S(beta/r)/(q S(c)). For fixed x>0 in the profile interval,

r log[S(beta/r)/S(c)] -> x(x-w.g).

Thus it vanishes exponentially when x<w.g and explodes when x>w.g; at x=w.g, S(beta/r)/S(c)->exp(xh). With several positive-weight directions, any such exploding contribution creates a qualifying population BH threshold already at that score. The original largest rank corresponds to the lowest qualifying score, so one cannot select a later radial-maximizing score while ignoring this earlier directional crossing. This is a local rate diagnostic, not a global first-crossing theorem: support endpoints, q-dependent or exponentially r-dependent g, null-only earlier crossings, and finite type/finite sample approximations all require separate control.

Outstanding obligations: simultaneous q,r,d,N limits; arbitrary nonuniform angular measures; a valid finite rare-alternative survival construction with no earlier largest-rank crossing; conditional independence of all finite coordinates under multivariate conditioning (a standard Borel encoding can make the conditioning variable real); and uniform integrability/finite-model empirical approximation. The isotropic fixed-d calculation supplies no dimension-uniform maximal gate and no main proof. It explains why simply replacing a scalar small-loading Lei null by isotropic higher-dimensional null loadings does not produce the desired unbounded normalized envelope through radial probability alone.
