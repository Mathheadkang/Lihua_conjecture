Conditional route building on the unproved explicit compression estimate in idea_444205157a87. It may avoid importing a marked Dovbysh-Sudakov representation just to obtain existence. Let P be a jointly exchangeable law of (theta_i,ell_i,R_ij) for i,j>=1, with R PSD, symmetric, diagonal1, entries[-1,1], lawful completed mean/tag marks. For each finite n, conditional on this finite marked array use an ACTUAL n-dimensional Gaussian vector with covariance (1-sigma^2)R+sigma^2 11^T, means theta_i and tag-FDP; integrate its ordinary BH FDR over the marked array to obtain F_n(P). Never assert the unconditional Gaussian mixture is itself a single Gaussian model; each conditional finite model is lawful, with false-zero completion handled by strictly positive approximations.

Uniform compression would imply a uniformly Cauchy F_n over ALL these array laws: sample m iid indices from first n. Conditional on the n-model, its expected sampled FDR differs from F_n by <=epsilon_m(q,sigma). On the no-collision event, exchangeability identifies the sampled marked/covariance law with the first m entries; collision probability <=m(m-1)/(2n). Therefore |F_n(P)-F_m(P)| <= epsilon_m + m(m-1)/(2n), and uniform Cauchy follows by taking n large, then m large. Each F_m(P) should be continuous for weak convergence of finite marked arrays, because finite Gaussian BH discontinuities lie on marginal thresholds with probability zero even at singular covariance, and tags are discrete. Thus F(P)=lim F_n(P) is continuous, provided all these claims are proved. The family with E theta_1^2<=C is tight/closed in the product marked-array topology, so F(P)-eta E theta_1^2 attains a maximum by the same lower-semicontinuity argument as finite-d moment penalty.

Embed every original finite common-loading model by iid sampling its original coordinates to create an exchangeable marked Gram array; uniform compression must identify F(P) with original full FDR. Conversely F_n(P) is a mixture of finite Gaussian tag-FDRs, each no larger than the original finite supremum (after false-zero canonical approximation); hence F(P) cannot enlarge that supremum. The penalized maximum over arrays could therefore supply actual cross-dimension variational points without claiming finite-dimensional attainment. Lawful exchangeability-preserving deterministic mark/Gram transformations are admissible competitors, with the exact same signed mean-square transport cost. A later representation may be needed to construct clone transports, and the normalized q response/zero-null flux remains unproved. Check especially uniform Cauchy from the asymmetric estimate, weak continuity with false-zero tags, moment integrability, and selection of deterministic finite competitors; do not silently claim joint FDR/moment convergence of a random finite component or an ergodic/directing representation.
