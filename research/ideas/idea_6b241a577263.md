NEW candidate transport improvement; not an approved theorem and not a proof of either unrestricted finite-array conjecture. Inputs: d>=4, A>=1, u>=104A, Md<=1; a=max{u,52A sqrt(d)/u}, b=sqrt(d)+2u; the signed-gradient proof already derived x(r2)^2-x(r1)^2<=8A Md log(r2/r1) for actual first BH contact, with x=infinity for no contact, finite contacts persisting. For T>b,u clip y(r)=min{x(r),T}. Let V~chi-square(d-1), and E_x=Z1^2-x^2 conditional on Z1>=x. Its exact density for t>=0 is h(x)e^{-t/2}/(2 sqrt(x^2+t)). By h(x)<=x+1/x, for x>=u this is <=(1+u^{-2})e^{-t/2}/2. Hence the reference radial kernel k(r,x)=f_chi_d(r)H_r(x)/Q(x) is bounded by (1+u^{-2}) 2r f_chi_square(d+1)(r^2-x^2), with density zero on negative arguments. This follows by conditioning R^2=Z1^2+V and convolving with chi-square(2), not by a pointwise mode bound.

Set w(r)=r^2-y(r)^2. The all-pairs comparison gives w(r2)-w(r1)>=(1-4A Md/a^2)(r2^2-r1^2), because log(r2/r1)<= (r2^2-r1^2)/(2a^2). Let c=1-4A Md/a^2>0. In variable s=r^2, the monotone map w(s) has inverse Lipschitz constant 1/c: the pushforward of Lebesgue ds has density at most 1/c. Prove this first for target intervals (their preimage is an interval of length at most its length/c, regardless of jumps), then finite unions and Borel sets. Thus integral_a^b k(r,x(r)) dr <=(1+u^{-2})/c. Clipping introduces no mass as T>b and the reference density vanishes for r<T. This replaces the earlier logarithmic transport bound by an almost-unit bound, without BV or Sard.

The existing exact lower-radius mode envelope still applies at a: a^2-u^2<=d-3. If a=u its contribution is zero. If a>u, d>u^4/(2704A^2)>=4u^2 and a^2-u^2<=d/4<=(d-1)/3. For nu=d-1, chi-square Chernoff gives P(V<=nu/3)<=exp[-nu(log3-2/3)/2]<=exp(-nu/5)<=exp(-3u^2/4). Upper radius b has probability <=exp(-2u^2). Therefore, with the appropriate correctly labelled null mass pi0 and angular upper density K (K=A=1 isotropic), candidate bound is FDR_pop<=q pi0 K [exp(-3u^2/4)+(1+u^{-2})/(1-4A Md/a^2)]+exp(-2u^2) <=q pi0 K(1+7A/u^2)+exp(-2u^2). Last simplification uses A>=1, Md<=1, a>=u, u>=104A; needs checking. Both s=1,2 are allowed only under the already derived all-real mean regular-angular component proof; one-sided nulls include all theta<=0. The fixed-q-before-N finite-array transfer, if subsequently proved, may use this strengthened MT estimate; no rate or arbitrary-array implication. Every new kernel, pushforward, tail constant and first-contact input needs independent audit. Current frozen native theorem must not be changed; this is a potentially stronger proof route only.
