Round four produced no candidates. The inherited coordinatewise envelope replaces the common w by separate coordinate maximizers and therefore loses the constraint that contributions to L_w must occur at one shared scale. Its existing entropy and square-root-summability estimates exclude only the board's controlled-weight families; when these controls grow, they do not establish the unrestricted bounds. Proposed proof step: retain the common w in a scale decomposition and prove a dimension- and weight-uniform cost for combining contributions from arbitrarily late scales. Any such step must explicitly account for correlations and overlapping scale events. Small common loading, separated maximizing scales, and nested high-rank or hierarchical structures remain unresolved in the supplied report; no family-wide exclusion follows from the absence of a checked violation. This is an unproved route, not a candidate, an established inequality, or a transfer to either Gaussian BH bound. The order, sharp truncated-moment, and maximal-tail assertions remain separate obligations.
