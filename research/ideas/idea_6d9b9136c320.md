A proof-like obstruction from the first native search round suggests an actual conditional-bridge mechanism, within an explicitly restricted scope. The full derivation is retained in cand_gaussian_bh_entropy_coherence_trace_budget_r1_s3_parallel_composite_nulls. It has not undergone independent native proof verification and is not an approved dependency.

Scope: every hypothesis is a true null, all loading vectors equal a unit vector v, and the original finite means theta_i are arbitrary nonpositive numbers. With Y=v dot G and means sorted decreasingly, the actual largest BH rank is R(Y)=max({0} union {r:Y>=u_r-theta_(r)}). Set A=min_r(u_r-theta_(r))>=U. Then f=1{Y>=A}, F=normal_survival(A)<=q. On Q support define eta(Y)=1-H/h(u_R) in [0,1]. Its heat posterior pi_t=E_Q[eta(Y_1)|F_t] gives a_t=v(1-pi_t)/H and 1-x>=E_Q eta. The exact product-Ito identity is M=(F/H) E_Q[eta(Y_1) (integral_0^1 beta_t dt-Y_1)], where beta_t=h((A-Y_t)/sqrt(1-t))/sqrt(1-t) is the scalar halfspace drift.

Conditioned on Y_1=g>=A, the path is an ordinary Brownian bridge Y_t=tg+sqrt(t(1-t))Z. Monotonicity and h(z)<=max(z,0)+h(0) give E_Q[integral beta_t dt | Y_1=g] <= A+pi/(2sqrt(2pi))+2sqrt(2/pi)<A+3. Hence M_+ <= (3F/H)E eta <=(3q/H)(1-x), including arbitrary negative means and F approaching zero; eta=0 gives x=1 and M=0 exactly.

Possible next proof step: try to retain this terminal-deficit-weighted conditional drift-bias identity when actual false-null-triggered rank transitions occur. The scalar halfspace form of f and the fixed direction v are essential to the calculation above. A general tilted law is not a halfspace law, and no uniform conditional bias bound, covariance assumption or cross-direction payment has been established for arbitrary Gaussian BH. The finite restricted calculation alone proves neither the new trace-budget lemma nor the main order theorem.
