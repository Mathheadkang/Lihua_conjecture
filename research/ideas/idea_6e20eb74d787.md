Scoped boundary calibration supplied by round1 boundary worker, not a proof of the deformation theorem. Model q=.1,p=.5,sigma=.5,v0=+1,v1=-1, rho=-.5. Choose c*=.5,r*=.75, a=.05/phi(S^{-1}(.075))≈.353218542714673, vartheta(c)=a+1-a c and b≈.262922199581120. At Y=b and X0=u(.75), the actual non-strict largest rank is .75 and FDP2/3. At Y=u(.5)-vartheta(1)≈.644853626951472 and X0<u(1), the actual largest rank is .5 with only false rejections, FDP0, although the auxiliary forced-true rank is1. Thus any proof must use the true-eligibility indicator before treating the auxiliary rank as actual. Numerical diagnostics (not certificates): F≈.0513978683, phi(b)Delta≈.00119336979. For smooth plateau bump chi,K=10, normalized increments at epsilon1e-5 are about +.001291899 for delta=-1 and -.001291889 for delta=+1; at epsilon2.5e-6 they are +.001242893 and -.001242891, consistent with a square-root remainder. The full derivative/finite recovery was not rigorously checked by this worker, so these numbers do not establish NOT fails. No candidate file was written and no main-counterexample claim is made.
