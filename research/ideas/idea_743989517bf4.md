More generally, if every null loading lies in a two-dimensional Gaussian subspace and every null mean is nonpositive, V>0 implies ||G_2||>=U, regardless of the number of null directions, clone multiplicities, alternatives, and full covariance rank. The explicit candidate cand_gaussian_bh_one_sided_universal_order_upper_r1_s2_null_span_two_rare_alternative fails NOT for this reason.
