New explicit candidate with a short direct proof, awaiting an independent cold check; not an approved manuscript result. Let N>=2, 0<q<1/2, Q be the standard-normal survival function, z_j=Q^{-1}(q*j/N), j=1,...,N, and z_0=+infinity. Take Z standard normal, one centered true null T_0=Z, and N−1 positive-mean alternatives T_j=z_j+z_{j+1}−Z, j=1,...,N−1. The unit-variance covariance is ss^T with s_0=1, s_j=−1, so it is a valid one-common-factor Gaussian array with zero residuals. Candidate exact largest-rank calculation: if z_k<Z<z_{k−1}, the null qualifies at rank k and precisely k−1 alternatives suffice, whereas every rank r>k fails since the (r−1)-st alternative mean is z_{r−1}+z_r and Z>z_{r−1}. Thus R=k and FDP=1/k. If Z<z_N, the null p-value exceeds q so FDP=0 regardless of any alternative-only rejections. Endpoint ties have probability zero. Hence FDR=(q/N)*sum_{k=1}^N 1/k exactly. This needs verification of all larger ranks, including alternative-only possibilities, rather than only checking k. Potential corollary, conditional on checking the approved entropy notation: the single oriented true-null group has pi0=1/N, loading norm 1, Gamma0=0, and E0=(log N)/N. Consequently FDR/(q E0)=H_N/log N tends to 1, showing the entropy coefficient 1 cannot uniformly be decreased when the remaining additive term is only O(q*pi0) and Gamma0=0. This is a candidate sharpness extension of the approved entropy bound, not a proof of the original unrestricted sharp upper conjecture. No novelty priority is asserted without literature checking.


Independent cold check completed: the all-N construction and full largest-rank proof were independently reconstructed by onefactor_mixed_label_bound_check. For every r>k, alternative r−1 lies below threshold z_r and the sorted alternative means imply at most r−2 alternatives qualify; adding the null still leaves fewer than r rejections. Thus no larger rank qualifies. The checker also read the approved entropy statement and its three definitions through native show, confirming pi0=1/N, zero null-loading remainder, and E0=log N/N. Therefore the exact FDR formula and the entropy-coefficient conclusion above are analytically confirmed, with the stated O(q*pi0) remainder restriction. At Z=z_k for k<N the actual rank can be k+1; these finitely many ties have Gaussian probability zero and do not affect the expectation. This is still a draft idea, not an approved asset or manuscript theorem; native extraction, proof verification and approval remain necessary before manuscript adoption.
