Continuation after teaching-window idea idea_eaffb9d8734f. Same-order averaged transport cost is not itself fatal at an exact maximizer of J_eta=F-eta integral theta^2: a bounded deterministic mean transport has a moment penalty derivative bounded by 2 sqrt(eta) times its speed once eta integral theta^2<=1. First establish existence and convergence of these completed-law maximizers at fixed q,sigma and finite ambient dimension, using the approved full W1 FDR continuity and moment compactness. The maximizer need not be finite or nonsingular. Finite endpoint competitors must approximate both FDR and second moments, not just W1. This does not yet imply the main Mills stationarity or a q-scaled flux bound.

A candidate way to derive the correct subsequent transport response, avoiding omission of moving folds: condition on residual Gaussian x, let Q_x(r) be the upper score quantile, B_x(r)=(S^{-1}(qr)-Q_x(r))/sigma, and M_x(r)=inf_{s in [r,1]} B_x(s). Subject to carefully proved quantile conventions/attainment, P_Z(R_x(Z)>=r)=S(M_x(r)). If f_x(r)=true-tail(Q_x(r))/r is absolutely continuous on [a,1], the macroscopic signed FDR is f_x(a)S(M_x(a))+integral_a^1 f_x'(r)S(M_x(r))dr. For a smooth deterministic transport family, a right derivative of M is the minimum barrier velocity over all active minimizers (Danskin-type envelope), rather than evaluation at an arbitrarily selected old fold. Differentiating the whole Stieltjes expression must retain changes of f and all moving-boundary contributions. Prove regularity, ties, endpoint and microscopic-rank passage separately; these are candidate calculations, not established formulas for arbitrary laws. A new supporting goal should first supply the exact quadratic-moment variational framework; transport calculus remains an explicit unresolved next gate.
