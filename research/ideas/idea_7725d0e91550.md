A deterministic macroscopic comparison supplies the mixed-label step without assuming FDP monotonicity. Fix residual scores, sampled indices and common z. Let r,s be the full and sampled actual normalized BH ranks, and b_r=u_q(r)-sigma z, b_s=u_q(s)-sigma z. Assume r,s>=a>0, simultaneous uniform errors |H-H_m|<=gamma and |V-V_m|<=gamma, and exact self-consistency H(b_r)=r, H_m(b_s)=s. The true-tag set is contained in the total set, and threshold sets are nested. Consequently |V(b_r)-V(b_s)|<=|H(b_r)-H(b_s)|<=|r-s|+gamma, so |V(b_r)-V_m(b_s)|<=|r-s|+2gamma. Since V_m(b_s)<=s, splitting the two ratios gives |D_N-D_m|=|V(b_r)/r-V_m(b_s)/s|<=(2|r-s|+2gamma)/a. This exact algebraic step obstructs a fixed mixed-label error on the event of macroscopic, close ranks and small tail errors. It is conditional on positive-rank self-consistency and does not establish trimmed-rank transfer, the empirical maximal bound, or microscopic/one-microscopic-rank control. The three-prefix failure reported in round 3 motivates this step; the growing orthogonal all-null failure supplies no general mixed-label proof.
