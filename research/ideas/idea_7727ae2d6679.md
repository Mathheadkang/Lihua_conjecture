Proposed intermediate theorem and a demarcation of what would remain; from the cross-disciplinary brainstorm of 10 November 2026. UNPROVED. The class is defined on the covariance alone, with no bound on rank, factor dimension, or the number and geometry of null directions. It is the general-loading companion of the conjecture (T) in idea_9f8ffb204424.

## Class and target (S)

Class $\mathcal C(s_0)$, $0<s_0\le1$: finite one-sided Gaussian models that admit $T=\theta+AG+D\varepsilon$ with $G\sim N(0,I_d)$, independent standard $\varepsilon$, and $D$ diagonal with $D_{ii}\ge s_0$ for every true null $i$ (no condition on non-null rows, on $d$ or on $A$). Every unit-diagonal $\Sigma$ with $\lambda_{\min}(\Sigma)\ge s_0^2$ belongs to it (take $D=s_0I$); approximate factor models with idiosyncratic variance bounded below are the statistical reading.

Target (S): there are $C(s_0)<\infty$ and $q_0(s_0)>0$ such that $\mathrm{FDR}_{\rm BH}\le C(s_0)\,q\sqrt{\log(1/q)}$ for all models in $\mathcal C(s_0)$ and all $q\le q_0(s_0)$, uniformly in $N$, means, $d$ and $A$. Null-side arguments should give $C(s_0)$ of order $1/s_0$ and cannot give less.

Relation to existing results: the approved directional-entropy theorem needs entropy $O(\sqrt{\log(1/q)})$ for the order; the draft radial-strip refinement reaches polynomially many directions through weights $(b_h-U)/(s\sqrt{2\pi})$; the slow-dimension theorem restricts $d$. (S) removes every complexity restriction at the price of the noise floor. The private-smoothed population objects of the variational program have this form at fixed smoothing; (S) asks for a bound uniform in the directing law, which stationarity identities at maximizers have not supplied.

## Why it should be true

Given $G=g$ a null has conditional tail ratio $\rho_{i,x}(g)=\overline\Phi((x-\theta_i-\langle a_i,g\rangle)/s_i)/\overline\Phi(x)$, at most its value at $\theta_i=0$. As a function of $g$ it is the density of $\mathrm{Law}(G\mid T_i\ge x)$, a mixture over the overshoot $\tau\ge x$ of the point-conditioned laws $N(\tau a_i,I-a_ia_i^{\top})$, whose densities are the Mehler kernels $s_i^{-1}\exp\{m^2/2-(m-r_i\tau)^2/(2s_i^2)\}$, $m=\langle a_i,g\rangle/r_i$, $r_i=\|a_i\|$. These are semi-log-convex in $g$ with constant $r_i^2/s_i^2\le1/s_0^2$, and so are their averages over nulls and the supremum over levels. For small $r_i$ they are the exponential kernels of (T). For general $r_i$:

- one direction: Lei's integrated tail-ratio bound, uniform in $r$;
- many independent directions of masses $\pi_h$: a direction rises above the bulk only for $m_h\ge\sqrt{2\log(s/\pi_h)}$ and stays below the cap $1/q$ for $m_h\le\sqrt{2\log(s/(q\pi_h))}$, a window of length at most $U$ with integrand $1/(s\sqrt{2\pi})$: a total of at most about $U/(s\sqrt{2\pi})$ whatever the number of directions. Short Monte Carlo at $q=10^{-2}$ with $K=1$ to $1000$ independent blocks (free level, exact kernels): $1.56\to1.55$ for $r=0.3$, $1.15\to1.80$ for $r=0.8$, $1.07\to2.31$ for $r=0.95$ (standard errors up to $0.13$). The value is bounded in the number of blocks and grows as $s$ decreases.

## Steps

(S1) Mehler form of (T): for nulls with $s_i\ge s_0$, $\mathbb E\min\{c,\sup_{x\ge U}\tfrac1{N_0}\sum_{i\in I_0}\rho_{i,x}(G)\}\le C(1+\sqrt{\log c})/s_0$, uniformly in the loading vectors. Gate: prove it first for independent blocks and for trees.

(S2) Supremum outside the sum for finite $N$. Given $G$ the coordinates are independent and exactly $\mathbb E[\mathrm{FDP}\mid G]=\tfrac qN\sum_{i\in I_0}\mathbb E[\rho_{i,x_i^*}(G)\mid G]$, with $x_i^*$ the level when $i$ is forced rejected. The approved conditional leave-one-out bound puts the supremum inside the sum, which for independent directions costs a factor of order $\sqrt{\log N}/s_0$. One has $\tilde x\le x_i^*\le x^*$ with $\tilde x$ the level after one phantom rejection, so all leave-one-out levels lie in one random window; the gate is that this window is short compared with the kernel width $s_i/r_i$ except on an event of small weighted probability (ranks at most $k_0$ cost at most $q\pi_0k_0$ by Markov). The approved uniform submodel compression and common-shift rank sandwiches treat a fixed common loading; the same statement uniformly in the loading geometry is what is needed.

(S3) Two-sided tests: directly, with the sign-symmetrized kernel (half weight on each of the two antipodal Mehler kernels), again a semi-log-convex average. The signed-duplication route does not apply here because duplication destroys the noise floor.

## What would remain after (S)

Every finite model is a weak limit of members of $\mathcal C(s)$ as $s\downarrow0$ (jitter $\Sigma\mapsto(1-s^2)\Sigma+s^2I$ at fixed $N$ and means; the FDP is bounded and its discontinuities have probability zero in the limit, as in idea_52f18a81d1a9). Since $\mathcal C(s)$ decreases in $s$, the phase target is EQUIVALENT to (S) with $\sup_{s_0}C(s_0)<\infty$. Benign degenerate models outside every $\mathcal C(s_0)$, such as the zero-sum simplex of idea_45ea077b792d, are covered by the limit.

So after (S) the whole remaining problem is the removal of the factor $1/s_0$. That factor is real for null-side arguments: with small private noise and many independent blocks a free level can follow the leading block (the Monte Carlo above; at $s=0$ the loss is the full logarithm of idea_d353cd54790b), and in every finite model the Chernoff step of idea_9f8ffb204424 costs the hazard $h(x^*)\ge U$. Removing it must use that the non-nulls are unit-variance Gaussians and cannot make the level follow the leading block. This is the pooled shell and hugging problem of idea_9e08f52bc7c4 and the remainder $B$ of idea_0d2e69e0ba3b, now restricted to nulls with small idiosyncratic noise in at least $q^{-C}$ nearly independent directions; exact null clones are harmless, since they merge into weighted atoms with a leave-cluster-out identity.

## Failure criterion

A family inside $\mathcal C(s_0)$ with $\mathrm{FDR}/(q\sqrt{\log(1/q)})$ unbounded (which would also refute the main target), or a failure of (S1) at bounded $s_0$; in the second case free-level arguments are insufficient even with private noise, and the first structure where they fail must be recorded.
