Round 2 reports O(epsilon^2) mixed fractional FDP corrections in the independent N=2 models with means (4,-4), selected false-null coordinate, and (0,1), selected zero-mean true-null coordinate. These checked cases suggest a proof step, not a general result: compare the actual common largest population crossing with its local face-shift description, and bound the expectation of the residual fractional FDP and background-atom clipping before summing signed face contributions. A possible route is to separate ordinary single-contact neighborhoods from simultaneous contacts and root switches, then quantify both the probability and FDP size of the exceptional regions under fixed positive-definite original covariance. Small positive ranks require separate denominator control; an O(epsilon) probability bound alone does not give an O(epsilon^2) expectation. General N and correlated mixed-label models remain unresolved. No candidate is supplied and no general estimate is asserted.
