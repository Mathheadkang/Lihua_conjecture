Tentative obstruction test for the pending fixed-label-block completion strategy, not an established result. A nearly tangential conditional population BH crossing might make null/null and null/alternative covariance-score contributions individually large with opposite signs, while their physical directional derivative cancels. The constrained support function retains the null/null term but replaces the cross term by a whitened nuclear norm; this may destroy the cancellation. A finite Gaussian common-factor family with tunable alternative means/weights could test this. Crucial qualifications: the certificate takes the nuclear norm AFTER integrating the cross-score matrix, so sign changes along the path may restore cancellation; a population tangency alone proves nothing without a finite-array largest-BH transfer and score control. First complete the prescribed witness tests; use this only as an additional falsification direction if those pass. No such family or divergent score has yet been derived.
