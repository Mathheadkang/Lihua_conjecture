Live web search after network recovery verified the author-hosted PDF of Pierre Bernhard and Alain Rapaport, On a theorem of Danskin with an application to a theorem of Von Neumann-Sion, Nonlinear Analysis 24(8), 1163–1181 (1995), https://www-sop.inria.fr/members/Pierre.Bernhard/publications/berrap95.pdf . Exact checked locations: §2.1 hypotheses D1 on pp.1165–1166 and Theorem D1 on p.1166; §2.2 hypotheses D2 and Theorem D2 on pp.1167–1168. The former treats compact maximization with right directional derivatives and upper-semicontinuity; the latter replaces compactness by uniform differentiability conditions and uses maximizing sequences. This is a known analogue for retaining all active minima (apply to a negative objective), not a BH result. It does not supply the required conditional-rank integral uniform integrability, Gaussian averaging, or global particle-potential compactness/separation. Do not cite as an approved dependency: paper not yet imported/extracted/verified in this project. Their calculus may instead prove the one-dimensional compact envelope argument directly, checking escape toward rank one.
