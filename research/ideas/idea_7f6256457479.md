NEW UNPROVED route after the ten preserved failed allocations. It uses an explicit entropy/coherence split rather than a free threshold, exact BH gradient, unweighted perimeter or independent Gram optimization. Every identity must be rederived in native execution; the proposed trace tradeoff is not proved and may be false.

Represent the original finite Gaussian model by T_i=theta_i+v_i dot G, G standard Gaussian, ||v_i||=1, vi dot vj=Sigmaij. Use the original labels theta_i<=0, largest BH rank R, true count V and f=FDP=V/(R vee1). Write F=E_gamma f. F=0 is trivial; otherwise Q has density f/F relative to gamma, and

$$
D(Q\|\gamma)\le\log(1/F).
$$

Let U=S^{-1}(q)>=1, h(u)=phi(u)/S(u), hU=h(U), u_r=S^{-1}(qr/N). Define a nonconservative, Mills-weighted signed-selection field (not an asserted scalar gradient)

$$
B(G)=\sum_{i\in I_0}\frac{v_i\mathbf1\{i\text{ rejected}\}}{R h(u_R)},
$$

zero when R=0. Then ||B||<=f/hU and

$$
J:=E_\gamma[G\mathbin\cdot B]\ge[U/hU]F.
$$

The last inequality uses true-null means and the increasing ratio u/h(u). Preserve all original covariance coefficients in divB. A face from old rank r to new rank k has exact vector jump w_k sum_(newtrue)vi +(w_k-w_r)sum_(oldtrue)vi, w_k=1/[k h(u_k)]; its normal contraction includes own entry, other-new-null covariance marks and negative old-null dilution. Coincident faces and singular covariances need direct grouping or uniform regularization, never double counting.

For Brownian latent G_t, let H_t=P_(1-t)f(G_t), v_t=grad log H_t, and let a_t=P_(1-t)B(G_t)/H_t. Under the terminal FDP tilt Q, a_t=E_Q[b(G_1)|F_t] with b=B/f on f>0, is a vector martingale; write da_t=K_t dW_t under Q. The exact identities to establish are

$$
E_Q\int_0^1\|v_t\|^2dt=2D(Q\|\gamma),\qquad
J=F E_Q\int_0^1[a_t\mathbin\cdot v_t+\operatorname{tr}K_t]dt.
$$

Let A=E_Q int||a_t||^2dt, x=hU^2 A in[0,1], and M=F E_Q int tr K_t dt, keeping its SIGN. The exact nonnegative resource split is

$$
1-x=E_Q[1-hU^2\|b\|^2]+hU^2 E_Q\int_0^1t\|K_t\|_{HS}^2dt.
$$

For selected true-null average vbar0, the terminal term equals

$$
1-hU^2\|b\|^2=
1-(hU/h(u_R))^2+(hU/h(u_R))^2V^{-2}\sum_{i,j\in\mathcal R\cap I_0}(1-\Sigma_{ij}).
$$

Thus the resource is actual rank slack, selected-null Gram dispersion, and posterior Jacobian energy. KEEP ALL THREE: replacing it by Jacobian energy alone is not justified, including in independent all-null models.

A concrete stronger sufficient certificate to try/refute is

$$
M_+\le Cq\sqrt{1-x}
$$

for a finite universal C on a universal small-q interval. This is unproved. It implies, by entropy Cauchy-Schwarz and the displayed lower bound,

$$
U^2\le2\log(1/F)+C^2q^2hU^2/F^2.
$$

Set z=F/(q hU), and use exp(-U^2/2)=sqrt(2pi)q hU. Then 2log[z/sqrt(2pi)]<=C^2/z^2, so z<=max(sqrt(2pi e),C), giving the desired order because hU<=U+1/U. This algebra is an implication only, not a proof of the certificate. The new executable task is to derive actual finite BH posterior/Jacobian cell formulas and establish or falsify this trace-versus-total-deficit inequality without sqrt(d), logN or inverse-eigenvalue losses.
