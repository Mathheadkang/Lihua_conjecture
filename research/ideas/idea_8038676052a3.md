Proposed genuinely different nonsharp route, NOT an established inequality. The freshly scoped-audited margin calculation in idea_12ea1e9582c2 yields an endpoint for common null-mean interpolation, without any factor/entropy restriction. Keep the ORIGINAL true-null set I0={theta_i<=0} fixed, keep every coordinate in BH, and put T(t)=T-t*1_I0, 0<=t<=1. Alternatives and covariance do not move. At t=1 every counted true-null mean is <=-1, so the margin calculation gives FDR(1)<=3 exp(-3/2) q for q<=S(1). This does not assume coordinatewise mean monotonicity.
For positive-definite Sigma first derive, rather than assume, the exact signed boundary derivative. For i in I0, background y=T_-i(t), define r_i(y)=R(p_i=0), s_i(y)=R(p_i=1), t_k=qk/N, u_k=S^-1(t_k). Let v_k(y) count OTHER original true nulls among background p<=t_k, v_0=0. The active coordinate switch is at T_i(t)=u_{r_i}, with full FDP jump A_i(y)=(v_{r_i}+1)/r_i-v_{s_i}/(s_i vee 1). Verify r_i>=s_i+1 and the single-active-face formula including cascades, ties and zero rank. Then d FDR(t)/dt=-sum_{i in I0} E_y[f_{T_i(t)|T_-i(t)}(u_{r_i}|y) A_i(y)].
The NEW, stronger certificate to attempt is the integrated positive null-entry face flow
B_shift=integral_0^1 sum_{i in I0} E_y[f_cond(u_{r_i}|y) (A_i(y))_+] dt <= A q(1+U)
with UNIVERSAL finite A, U=S^-1(q)>=1, independent of N, all means, Sigma and its least eigenvalue. If proved, FDR(0)<=FDR(1)+B_shift directly yields the current order and a finite-model PSD limit follows. The certificate is NOT proved and may be false; any auxiliary candidate needs fresh audit and does not itself refute the main target. Derivative identities alone are not progress on this certificate.
Unlike covariance interpolation, this path preserves the full original covariance and has no unrelated Gram maximization. Unlike regression BV/NT it shifts true-null means together in one deterministic direction, with coefficient 1 rather than negative-edge speeds; all false-null statistics are frozen on each realized deletion path. For each realization the BH rejection sets are nested in t and each coordinate/grid threshold is crossed at most once. Exploit this common nested deletion path to retain exact r-to-s cascades and charge positive true entries before taking expectations. Attempt an explicit finite-cell/rank allocation using A_i>0, fixed ordering of true statistics, and original Gaussian marginals; arbitrary mean monotonicity, a qU pointwise derivative bound, or unconditional independence is not available. The pointwise derivative bound is in fact implausible: independent all-null models concentrate a q-sized change in a t-window of width about 1/sqrt(log N). Only the integrated bound is requested.
A serious plan would first prove the margin endpoint and exact flow identity inline, then attempt a summable GLOBAL Gaussian rank-flow charge with all original means, including strongly negative backgrounds, retained. Naive separate marginal face sums or deterministic harmonic total variation leave N/log N losses and are insufficient. Stop at the first unproved integrated-flow lemma with exact expression if no certificate exists. A planner should reject this proposal rather than merely rename a tautological FDR difference. Its positive boundary flow is an explicit stronger object that can be attacked or refuted independently.

## Candidate partial allocation for the nested path

The following root-derived allocation is a proof candidate, not yet independently verified or native-approved. Traverse the common-mean path backwards, so rejection sets only grow. At a jump from rank s to rank r>s, let v_s be the previous true count and m the number of newly entering original true nulls. For s>0 the signed FDP jump is A=m/r-v_s(r-s)/(r s); for s=0 it is A=m/r. Thus A_+<=m/r. When m>0, assign each of the m entering true nulls an equal share A_+/m, at most 1/r; when m=0 the positive jump is zero. Each original true null enters at most once over the whole path, including cascades. At its entry time t, monotonicity of its own p-value in t and BH self-consistency give p_j(0)<=p_j(t)<=qr/N<=q, so its assigned share is bounded by q/[N p_j(0)] times 1{p_j(0)<=q}. Consequently the complete expected assigned flow to entrants with theta_j<=-1 is at most 3 exp(-3/2) q by the already scoped-audited marginal calculation, with all coordinates retained. What remains is the expected pro-rata flow assigned to -1<theta_j<=0 entrants, retaining the exact A_+ rather than replacing all shares by 1/r. This is an explicit new weak-null-flow certificate to attempt or refute. The elementary allocation by itself does not control those remaining entrants: applying the same marginal bound at theta=0 restores a harmonic/dimension loss. A proof must still exploit joint Gaussian structure and the full cascade compensation.

## Read-only scout reconstruction and exact cell reduction

The scout scout_common_null_mean_flow independently reconstructed the allocation and gave its exact weak-entrant weight. Put d=r-s and let w be the number of newly entering true nulls whose original means lie in (-1,0]. For s,m>0 the weak share of the positive FDP jump is (w/r)[1-v_s d/(s m)]_+. For s=0 it is w/r; if m=0 it is zero. This retains the dilution term from the full cascade. Dropping the bracket returns an insufficient marginal/BY charge. This reconstruction is not native approval.

The same scout proposed integrating each triggering face before estimating it. Here is the root's explicit candidate derivation, to be checked independently. For positive-definite Sigma, partition the background statistic space at the fixed BH grid u_k, so r,s and the old/new true counts are constant on each cell b. Write z=T(0). At a trigger i in I0 the equation T_i(t)=u_r becomes t=z_i-u_r. In the face integral use z_i=u_r+t, z_j=y_j+t for j in I0 excluding i, and z_a=y_a for a outside I0. The transformation from (t,y) to z has determinant one. Hence integrating the original joint face density over 0<=t<=1 and cell b gives the probability, under the ORIGINAL joint Gaussian z, of the event E_{i,b}: z_i in [u_r,u_r+1], every other true coordinate satisfies the cell's fixed band inequalities for z_j-z_i+u_r, and every alternative satisfies the original fixed band inequalities for z_a. In particular a true-coordinate comparison y_j>=u_k becomes z_j-z_i>=u_k-u_r, while an alternative comparison remains z_a>=u_k. All full rank/cascade restrictions of b are retained. These are Gaussian polyhedral events, not independent marginal bands. Boundary endpoints have zero probability under positive definiteness. Consequently the proposed weak flow is exactly the finite sum of P(E_{i,b}) times the displayed weak-share coefficient for that cell. It introduces no reciprocal conditional variance, inverse eigenvalue, independent Gram maximization, or new limiting model.

No summable Gaussian probability certificate for this finite cell sum was found. The scout explicitly recommended deferring a native main-target execution unless a concrete allocation is identified, rather than relabelling the new flow inequality as proved or as an executable certificate. No lawful counterexample to this stronger auxiliary flow bound or to the main target was found. Nestedness and marginal integration alone leave the unresolved logarithmic/dimension loss. The entire proposal remains an unapproved research direction.

## Later all-coordinate-shift variant and metric-Stein trial

Root proposed a stronger geometric simplification, independently reconstructed by the native planning agent: shift ALL coordinates as T(t)=T(0)-t*1_N, while counting only the ORIGINAL set I0={theta_i<=0} at every time. This intermediate counted FDP is not asserted to equal the usual FDR for the shifted model, whose true-null labels may change. At t=0 it is the target FDR. At t=1 all counted coordinates have means <=-1, and the marginal calibration bound applies to their partial FDP irrespective of the other means or labels. Thus the same O(q) endpoint is valid. All original true nulls still enter at most once on the reverse nested path, and the strong-entry calibration and exact pro-rata weak allocation are unchanged. False coordinates may now trigger cascades and must be retained as triggers.

Every coordinate ordering is fixed on this path. With original ordered scores z_(1)>...>z_(N) and a_k=z_(k)-u_k, ordinary BH has rank R(t)=max({0} union {k:a_k>=t}). Each new rank r on the reverse path is an upper record of these margins and triggers at t=a_r, with trigger at original GLOBAL score rank r. Under positive definiteness ties among the relevant scores and margins have zero probability. Its predecessor s is the largest j<r with a_j>a_r (or zero); all j>r have a_j<a_r. The jump is V(r)/r-V(s)/(s vee 1), where V(k) counts original true labels in the first k ORIGINAL scores; weak and strong prefix counts remain fixed. A false trigger can bring weak true entrants at earlier ranks that did not individually qualify. Adjacent-rank or single-null charges therefore cannot discard the full record/cascade event.

Conditional on X_i=T_i-theta_i=x, every relative original score is theta_j-theta_i+W_j-(1-Sigma_ij)x. Since 1-Sigma_ij>=0, the trigger's GLOBAL descending score rank is nonincreasing in x. Moreover 1-Sigma_ij=||v_i-v_j||^2/2 in a unit-vector Gram representation. In this all-coordinate variant all permutation and record inequalities are invariant under simultaneous translation z->z+c*1_N; only the cutoff band z_(r) in [u_r,u_r+1] breaks that invariance. This suggests an explicit finite-cell trial rather than an asserted bound: construct the allocation vector on permutation/record cells, perform prefix summation by parts, and exploit Gaussian Stein with sum_j partial_j g_i=0 for translation-invariant weights. Formally, for smooth such weights, sum_j Sigma_ij E partial_j g_i = -sum_j(1-Sigma_ij) E partial_j g_i. For the actual piecewise weights all permutation, record, cutoff and mean boundary terms must be derived and paired explicitly. The actual nonnegative distance weights may not be replaced by an independent Gram supremum, and no sign or cancellation of the resulting face coefficients is assumed. A successful certificate would bound the combined endpoint, cutoff, mean and metric-face charge by a universal C q(1+U). Failure to do that at the first unsummable face is the explicit stopping condition. No such certificate has been established. All of this remains a candidate route requiring full derivation and fresh verification; the native planner is evaluating whether the trial is executable.
