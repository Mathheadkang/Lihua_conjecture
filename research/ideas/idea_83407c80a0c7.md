Observed while refuting gaussian_simes_clone_block_schur_variation_bound (search round 1, boundary_degenerate). Let Y_t ~ N(0, R_t) with R_t the block path of Gamma_eps=(1-eps)Gamma+eps I_K, which is positive definite for every t in [0,1] (Schur product of positive definite matrices). The Simes rejection set A is the union over nonempty block sets S of the orthants {Y_b >= l_S for b in S}, l_S = invPhibar(q w(S)) >= invPhibar(q) > 0; let L be the set of at most 2^K-1 levels. Since R_t is linear in t with fixed unit diagonal, Plackett gives f'(t) = sum_{b<c} (Gamma_bc^2-Gamma_bc) E[d_b d_c 1_A(Y_t)]. For fixed other coordinates the (y_b,y_c) section of A is a staircase upper set, whose indicator is a +-1 combination of quadrant indicators with distinct corners in L x L (corners with an infinite coordinate do not depend on rho). Integrating out the other coordinates gives |E d_b d_c 1_A| <= sum_{(x,y) in L^2} phi_2(x,y; rho_bc(t)). The correlation rho_bc(t)=(1-t)g+t g^2 is monotone with derivative g^2-g, so the t-integral is at most int phi_2(x,y;rho) d rho over the swept range, which is at most Phibar(max(x,y)) <= q. Hence V(f_eps) <= C(K,2)(2^K-1)^2 q. Letting eps -> 0, f_eps -> f pointwise (the boundary of A lies in finitely many hyperplanes y_b = level, null for unit-variance marginals) and variation is lower semicontinuous, so V(f) <= C(K,2)(2^K-1)^2 q < K^2 4^K q / 2. This is a candidate proof of the whole statement; the step to check is the +-1 corner decomposition of the two-dimensional section and the use of Plackett's identity for the indicator (via smoothing).
