Candidate family for the new construction specification, based on project observations idea_cc4ddef48cdd and idea_e60eb39b5946. This is a seed, not an approved construction; rederive all properties from the specification and verify independently.

For q in (0,1/2), let u=barPhi^-1(q),v=barPhi^-1(2q/3),w=barPhi^-1(q/3), B=w+1/4,delta=(v-u)/2,C=B+2delta,mu=B+v,M=B+delta-u. Put tA=M-delta/2,tB=M+delta/2. Use Z~N(0,1), TA=(Z,Z-tA,mu-Z), TB=(Z,Z-tB,mu-Z). The same covariance is ss^T, s=(1,1,-1); mean vectors are (0,-tA,mu),(0,-tB,mu), with -tB<-tA<0<mu. Only coordinate2 changes; coordinate1 is fixed0.

For arbitrary t in(M-delta,M+delta), actual BH FDP is0 belowv,1/2 on[v,B],1 on(B,t+u),2/3 on[t+u,C],1 aboveC. Exact FDR(t)=q/3+barPhi(B)/2-barPhi(t+u)/3+barPhi(C)/3. The proposed positive difference is [barPhi(B+delta/2)-barPhi(B+3delta/2)]/3. All parameters are explicit Gaussian quantiles, and false rejection requires Z>=u, giving FDR<=q. Check the complete largest-rank cases, orderv<B<t+u<C<t+v, endpoints, all q and truth labels afresh. Do not cite these calculations as already approved inputs.
