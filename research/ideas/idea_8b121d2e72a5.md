Phase I cold-context proposal. Only the supplied original problem was used. No native context, existing proof ideas, auxiliary assets, or history were read. This is a conjectural transport-and-smoothing architecture; the central operator inequality below is unproved.

Mechanism and specifically Gaussian ingredient. Translate each rare true-null rejection cell back to the hyperplane where that null's centered Gaussian projection equals zero. The Cameron-Martin density is then an explicit exponentially decaying kernel. Average the sum of these translated densities with the Gaussian Mehler operator at spatial scale 1/sqrt(log(1/q)). The hoped-for mechanism is that PSD Gram geometry prevents many heavily weighted translated BH cells from concentrating in the same small Gaussian neighborhood. Large families of almost parallel nulls should force a large BH rank after translation; nearly orthogonal families gain the reciprocal-threshold factor from smoothing each thin exponential kernel. This is a local geometric packing claim rather than a generic marginal-p-value argument.

Exact BH reduction. Represent Sigma as the Gram matrix of unit vectors a_i in R^d and let Z be standard Gaussian in R^d. Let R_i(z) be the ordinary nonstrict BH rank after forcibly setting only p_i to zero, and let A_ir={z:R_i(z)=r}. For a rejected i, BH stability gives R=R_i, hence the FDR is exactly the sum over true i and r of (1/r) times the Gaussian measure of A_ir intersected with the relevant true-null tail. Verify this deterministic stability identity first, including ties and r=1.

One-sided transport. Put t_r=S^{-1}(qr/N) and c_ir=t_r-theta_i>0 for true theta_i<=0, taking q<1/2. For each true i and r define
h_ir(y)=(1/r) 1{y+c_ir a_i in A_ir} 1{<a_i,y>>=0} exp(-c_ir<a_i,y>-c_ir^2/2).
Let H_1(y)=sum_i sum_r h_ir(y). The exact Gaussian translation formula gives integral H_1 d gamma_d = FDR.

Two-sided transport, separately. Put t_r=S^{-1}(qr/(2N)). For true theta_i=0 split the tail into the disjoint plus and minus faces. For epsilon in {+1,-1}, set b=epsilon a_i, c=t_r, and use the same formula (1/r) 1{y+c b in A_ir} 1{<b,y>>=0} exp(-c<b,y>-c^2/2). Sum over both faces to obtain H_2. Their overlap has Gaussian measure zero. Again integral H_2 d gamma_d = FDR. Alternatives retain arbitrary original means and the original two-sided p-values inside A_ir.

FIRST UNPROVED GATE (Mehler-smoothed translated-cell congestion). Set L=log(1/q), tau=1/L, and
P_tau H(x)=E[H(exp(-tau)x+sqrt(1-exp(-2tau))G)]
for an independent standard Gaussian G in R^d. For each s separately, do universal C_s and q_s>0 exist such that every finite original model satisfies
sup over x in R^d of P_tau H_s(x) <= C_s q sqrt(L)
whenever q<=q_s?
This is a pointwise operator inequality for EXPLICIT translated BH cells. It is substantially stronger than the desired integral bound and may be false. It is not asserted as known. The reason for using full shifts c_ir rather than shifts only to the q-threshold is that even N=1 otherwise leaves an unsmoothed far-tail plateau. The reason for the specific smoothing scale is that a kernel exp(-c u) 1{u>=0} averaged against a Gaussian of width L^(-1/2) can supply a factor of order min(1,sqrt(L)/c), eliminating spurious sqrt(log N) from large thresholds.

Concrete quick falsification/support tests. For N=1 evaluate the one-dimensional convolution analytically and optimize x; this checks normalization, the proposed q sqrt(L) scale, and extreme negative true-null means. Then test independent all-null vectors at x=0 and optimize x numerically: the r=1 summands are a sharp check that smoothing actually removes sqrt(log N). Next test rank-one sign patterns with adversarial alternative means and many duplicate null vectors, where A_ir can be enumerated by scalar breakpoints and the Mehler convolution is one-dimensional quadrature. Finally test small N with nearly parallel and opposite vectors, optimize both their Gram matrix and x, and compare exact quadrature/importance-sampling estimates of sup P_tau H/(q sqrt(L)). A growing optimized ratio refutes the gate even if ordinary FDR remains small. Repeat two-sided tests with both faces and do not transfer a one-sided result automatically.

How the final constants stay universal. Gaussian invariance gives integral P_tau H_s d gamma_d=integral H_s d gamma_d=FDR, so the proposed pointwise gate immediately gives the desired estimate. No dimensional normalization, number of directions, null fraction, epsilon-net size, or covariance inverse occurs. Singular covariance is handled directly by its finite-rank Gram representation; tau is an analytic smoothing parameter fixed explicitly by q, not a model regularization whose constant may diverge. The real unresolved step is the local translated-cell packing inequality, where the BH rank and PSD Gram geometry must do the work; a Gaussian convolution estimate applied separately to every r would lose a rank-count factor and is insufficient.
