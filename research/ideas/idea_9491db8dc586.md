All means are true, scores share G0, and the finite covariance is singular but lawful. W1(pi_n,pi)=10/(4n). For the limiting population H(r;g)=min(1,max(0,(g-u(qr))/10)), so qualifying ranks are g>=b(r):=u(qr)+10r. The function b tends to infinity at zero and is strictly convex on(0,1] because q<1/2; its derivative is 10-q/phi(u(qr)), so it has an interior minimum r* with phi(u(q r*))=q/10. At g=b(r*) the root is a double contact, and above it two roots may occur; the actual largest root must be used.
