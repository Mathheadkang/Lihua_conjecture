A different candidate successor to the refuted full-dimensional Mehler gate. It is unproved and is not a repair of that deprecated statement. The distinction is to leave every unmarked Gaussian background at its ORIGINAL conditional variance, and to use a FIXED smoothing width only in the marked true-null direction.

For the original finite one-sided Gaussian model, choose unit Gram vectors a_i in R^d, theta arbitrary, true set I0={i:theta_i<=0}. Let R_i(z) be the full original nonstrict largest BH rank after setting only p_i to zero. Put u_r=S^{-1}(qr/N), c_ir=u_r-theta_i, and define the per-tag translated contribution
$$
H_{i,q}(y)=\sum_{r=1}^N r^{-1}
1\{R_i(y+c_{ir}a_i)=r,\ a_i.y\ge0\}
e^{-c_{ir}a_i.y-c_{ir}^2/2}.
$$
This is the SAME local mass translation, not the deprecated aggregate bound. Deterministic BH stability and Gaussian change of variables still need proof inline: sum_i int H_i d gamma equals actual FDR, preserving original labels and physical tests.

For xi in a_i-perp, define a one-dimensional Mehler average at fixed time t0=1:
$$
M_i H_{i,q}(z,\xi)
=E_g H_{i,q}\{\xi+(e^{-1}z+\sqrt{1-e^{-2}}g)a_i\},\quad g\sim N(0,1).
$$
Let gamma_perp_i be the ORIGINAL standard Gaussian law on a_i-perp (Dirac in d=1). The new proposed gate is
$$
B_q=\sum_{i\in I0}\int
\sup_{z\in\mathbb R} M_iH_{i,q}(z,\xi)
\,d\gamma_{\perp i}(\xi)
\le C q\sqrt{\log(1/q)}
$$
uniformly over all finite N,d,means,PSD unit-diagonal Gram geometries and sufficiently small universal q. No supremum over xi and no shrinkage or recentering of its law is permitted. If true, one-dimensional Mehler invariance and Tonelli make this a sufficient bound for the exact original FDR. It adds no assumption on the model. The gate is stronger than the integral goal and may still be false.

Why it differs from the approved refutation. In the full-dimensional convolution, all unmarked private nulls have variance v<1; the explicit ladder exploits their suppressed tails and the removal of the original null-population feedback. Here only the a_i coordinate is smoothed for tag i; an unmarked direction a_j retains variance 1-(1-v0)<a_i,a_j>^2, not v0. In the vanishing-common-loading ladder, true-null pair correlations eta^2 are tiny, so this variance change is of order eta^4. Do NOT infer a bound from this observation: analyze the actual original-background conditional law and all later BH ranks.

Initial exact calibration: when all original scores are independent, R_i is fixed upon conditioning on xi and unaffected by shifts in a_i; a single exponential half-line integral gives a contribution <=2q/[N sqrt(1-e^{-2})] for q<=S(1), and summation is O(q). For N=1 the same calculation works even for negative true means. Physical replication must also be checked: on genuine rejection tails R'=K R and each copied tag contributes 1/K, so the intended full functional should be invariant in the same latent embedding. These are draft derivations, not independently approved results.

First executable screening: calculate B_q exactly for scalar mixed-sign/mixed-label arrays, and for the approved vanishing-loading ladder at its asymptotic scales; also test the approved tangent-reserve positive-path-charge family. If any gives B_q/[q sqrt(log1/q)] unbounded, abandon this gate without changing its statement or weakening the main goal. If those screens pass, the first new proof obligation is a Gaussian averaged-over-background bound for the cross-translated actual rank masks; neither coordinatewise Mills bounds nor the approved fractional-frame kernel alone supplies it. No main theorem is claimed.
