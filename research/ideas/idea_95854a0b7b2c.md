Round 1 supplies one reusable proof step, not a proof of the target: for the exact opposite-direction alternative ladder, the full forced-rank masks force every higher-rank contribution onto the singleton y=0. A positive-variance Mehler input assigns this set probability zero. After discarding it, k null copies at rank k contribute k/k=1 and leave K_u(y)=1{y>=0} exp(-uy-u^2/2), u=u_k>=1. For sigma^2=1-exp(-2/L), L=log(1/q), a normal-density supremum bound gives sup_mu E K_u(mu+sigma G) <= phi(u)/(u sigma) <= 2S(u)/sigma. With S(u_k)=qk/N and sigma^2>=(1-exp(-2))/L this is at most 2q sqrt(L)/sqrt(1-exp(-2)), uniformly in N,k,q. The broader approach is first to identify any cells supported only on equality hyperplanes from the exact forced-rank masks, then estimate the remaining positive-measure weighted cells. The extension is unproved: perturbed ladders can create positive-width cells, and separately summing one-kernel bounds over all ranks/nulls can introduce an uncontrolled multiplicity factor.
