Candidate fully proved-in-plan route for the measurable-lifting gap (still require fresh execution/referee verification), avoiding an undeclared measurable-selection black box. For a Borel probability lambda on Polish X, define outer content c(A)=inf{lambda(B):A subset B, B Borel}. Every A has a minimal Borel envelope B of measure c(A). If A_n increases, envelopes B_n are increasing modulo null sets: B_k\B_n is Borel, disjoint from A_k, and hence null by minimality for k<=n. Thus c(union A_n)=lim c(A_n). Now let A be a continuous image F(N^N) with cylinder images of diameter <=2^-n at depth n. Such a representation exists for a projection of a closed Polish relation: construct a continuous surjection N^N onto that relation via a countable tree of nonempty nested closed cells of diameters <=2^-n in a complete metric, then project. At each level prune to finitely many children per retained node. By continuity from below of c, choose the finite level so the outer content of its union of cylinder images drops by at most epsilon*2^-n from the preceding retained level. The finite unions of CLOSURES of these images are closed and decreasing; each has lambda at least c(A)-epsilon. Their intersection K has the same lower bound by measure continuity from above. It is totally bounded and closed in complete X, hence compact. A finite-branching/Konig argument gives each x in K a retained branch beta; shrinking image diameter forces x=F(beta), so K subset A. Let epsilon tend to zero: every analytic projection has equal inner and outer lambda measure and is measurable in the lambda completion. Supply the tree construction explicitly, including repeated children when a finite cover is encoded by N, so no compactness is silently assumed for the strong Hilbert ball.
Apply to a closed relation R={(Q,mu):Psi(mu)=Q} between Polish spaces, where Psi is the continuous iid marked-Gram law map. Fix the distribution lambda of component laws, supported on the analytic projection of R. Recursively choose the first ball of radius <=2^-n whose closed intersection with all earlier chosen balls still meets the fiber R_Q. Each hit set is the projection of a closed relation, hence lambda-completion measurable by the preceding pruning argument. Closed nested fiber cells are nonempty and have diameter tending to zero in a complete metric on Prob(E). The chosen ball centres converge to a completion-measurable mu(Q) in that fiber. This produces a genuine random directing measure after completion, not a claimed everywhere-Borel section. Use the approved unmarked representation plus Borel mark decoding only to show almost every component fiber is nonempty; no jointly measurable generator was granted by that source. Then prove the continuous map Psi and apply affinity/moment integrability for deterministic maximum-component selection. This should make clause 6 executable without another specialized-source dependency.
