## Proposed step

For the independent equal-weight frozen-ray target, separate the far-threshold regime from the unresolved critical collective regime. Set Y_i=exp((Z_i^+)^2/2), so Lambda <= K^{-1} sum_i Y_i. At a tail threshold alpha, clip at T=K alpha and set X_i=min(Y_i,T). Independence is preserved. The scalar Gaussian tail gives P(Y_i>t) <= C/[t sqrt(log t)] for t>=2. Integrating this tail suggests the uniform estimates E X_i <= C_0(1+sqrt(log T)) and E X_i^2 <= C_1 T/sqrt(log T), with absolute constants. On the event max_i Y_i<=T, the scalar envelope equals the average of the X_i. A union bound for an exceedance of T plus Chebyshev for that average would therefore yield P(Lambda>=alpha) <= C/[alpha sqrt(log(K alpha))] whenever alpha>=2 C_0(1+sqrt(log(K alpha))). Establish the two scalar integral estimates and all constants explicitly before using this as a proved lemma.

## Scope and evidence

This is a proposed proof decomposition, not a proof of either target assertion. The round-2 candidate report quantifies only K=n>=3 and c=alpha=exp(n^2): moment excess at most 2 and normalized tail at most 5. Those supplied bounds reject that specific growing sequence. They do not establish the displayed proposed estimates or a statement for all late-onset parameter sequences. Earlier scalar/two-coordinate envelope checks do not already supply this clipped-independent concentration step.

## Remaining obligation

The condition above omits large K at moderate alpha, where the scalar-envelope mean grows and simultaneous contributions at a common w matter. Critical collective SNR remains unresolved. A tail bound with an unspecified constant also cannot recover the required sharp truncated-moment coefficient. Late single-leader asymptotics are screening heuristics unless upgraded to uniform finite-parameter bounds. No general theorem or Gaussian BH reduction follows from the unsuccessful search.
