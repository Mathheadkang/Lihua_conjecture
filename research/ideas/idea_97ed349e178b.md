## Scoped step

In the tested rank-one model with m identical true nulls (theta,v)=(0,1) and k identical alternatives (mu,-1), m,k positive integers and mu>2U, write w=m/(m+k), A=barPhi^{-1}(qw), T=mu-U, L=max(A,T), E=[U,T], J=(L,infinity), b=1/h(U) and c=1/h(A)<b. The exact largest-rank BH rule gives f=w 1_E+1_J and B=wb 1_E+c 1_J. For t<1, the tilted conditional terminal measure nu_{t,z} has density proportional to f(y) phi_{1-t}(y-z). If pi=nu_{t,z}(E), then a=c+(b-c)pi and

$$
\partial_z\pi=\frac{\pi(1-\pi)}{1-t}\{\mathbb E_{\nu_{t,z}}[Y\mid E]-\mathbb E_{\nu_{t,z}}[Y\mid J]\}<0.
$$

The order of the disjoint supports and b>c therefore gives K=partial_z a<0. This is an explicitly checked two-component scalar step, not a statement about arbitrary FDP posteriors. The tested N=2 model with alternative mean U+A+1 is the m=k=1 subfamily. In these models the candidate calculations also establish absolute trace integrability and the endpoint identity, so M<0 and M_+=0 rather than merely a formal derivative sign.

## Possible use

When decomposing a future scalar proof, remove exactly such ordered two-component pieces from the positive-trace budget after proving their integrability; arbitrary multiplicities in the displayed blocks and q-dependent mu>2U are permitted. No decomposition for a general model is supplied. Reversed mark order, overlapping or multiple terminal components, unequal within-block means, positive trace, and noncollinear directions remain outside this step. Nothing here proves the entropy-linear term, the unrestricted budget, or the full-model endpoint identity.
