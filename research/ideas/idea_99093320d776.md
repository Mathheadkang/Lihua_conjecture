Round 2 search-summary proof step, prompted by the failed critical-two-atom and opposite-null-atom switch candidates. Conditional on the residual scores and sampled indices, the actual largest normalized BH ranks rho_N(z) and rho_m(z) are nondecreasing functions into [0,1], and the common Z remains independent standard Gaussian. For every bounded nondecreasing f: R -> [0,1] and h >= 0, its Stieltjes measure has total mass at most one; Tonelli gives E[f(Z+h)-f(Z)] = integral P(Z < t <= Z+h) df(t) <= h/sqrt(2*pi). Endpoint conventions do not affect the Gaussian expectation. The same bound holds for E[f(Z)-f(Z-h)]. Thus, after proving the required two-way trimmed-rank transfer on the simultaneous empirical-tail good event, rank jumps can be integrated with a bound independent of their number, locations, or sizes. The good event depends on residual scores and indices rather than Z, so this conditional argument remains applicable. This establishes a scalar smoothing step only: deriving the trimmed comparison, treating small ranks, identifying exact finite-grid crossings, proving total-count self-consistency, and comparing full mixed FDPs still require separate arguments. Do not assume the FDP is monotone. Source: current round-2 pack failure summaries, without independent verification of candidate bodies.
