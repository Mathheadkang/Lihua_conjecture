New proposed extension of growing-clone contact analysis, NOT proved and not a main bound. Fix a chosen TRUE null i with theta_i<=0 (including0). Uniformly copy every original coordinate K times. Only i's copies have deterministic mean offsets -epsilon w_b^(K), with w_b^(K)=[K/(K-b+1/2)]^{1/kappa}, b=1,...,K, kappa>0 fixed; directions remain exactly v_i. Thus every finite expanded vector is jointly Gaussian with unit marginals, and all chosen copies remain true nulls without any upward mean compensation. Other groups are exact. The deterministic offset empirical law tends to Pareto W>=1 with P(W>w)=w^{-kappa}. Condition on the original Gaussian vector. On an active block with k=r-1 unchanged selected coordinates, chosen fraction nu has contact T_i=u(k+nu)+epsilon(1-nu)^(-1/kappa), with u(x)=S^{-1}(qx/N). Write A_r=1/[r h(u_r)] and alpha=kappa/(kappa+1), beta=1/(kappa+1). The contact derivative is -A(k+nu)+(epsilon/kappa)(1-nu)^(-1-1/kappa); since A decreases with x for q<1/2, this contact is strictly convex and has one minimum near nu=1. Heuristically delta_c=1-nu_c~[epsilon/(kappa A_r)]^alpha and birth threshold equals u_r+C_kappa epsilon^alpha A_r^beta+o(epsilon^alpha), C_kappa=(kappa+1)kappa^{-alpha}. Tentative actual-FDR variation: epsilon^{-alpha}(lim_{K->infty}F_{K,epsilon}-F) -> -C_kappa sum_r A_r^beta mu_{i,r}(total). Prove every limit, do not use this as a theorem. Fractional FDP and clipping errors may be lower order: the high-branch missing mass behaves as (epsilon/(T_i-u_r))^kappa; its integral is O(epsilon^{2alpha}) for kappa>1, O(epsilon log(1/epsilon)) for kappa=1, and O(epsilon^kappa) for 0<kappa<1, all o(epsilon^alpha). Gaussian joint density bounds should charge unchanged-atom clipping by this same missing mass. Unlike private Gaussian cloning, tiny population ranks are empty below the finite maximum T_i-epsilon, avoiding a Gaussian empirical-extreme step. Essential obligations: deterministic quantile-array convergence at the COMMON largest crossing; active-block and global-rank comparison; signed FDP jumps and clipping; fractional errors with Gaussian tails; full boundary cases and finite original PD scope. This route removes the unlawful positive compensation at a zero true-null mean, but supplies powers of A_r, not yet the desired Mills-weight cost or near-extremizer comparison. Even a proof would not establish the unrestricted main conjecture.
