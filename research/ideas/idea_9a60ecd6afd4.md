New candidate route beyond mean/level complementarity, not an established theorem. At fixed positive sigma,s,a, consider the lawful PRE-NOISE mixture pi_epsilon=(1-epsilon)pi+epsilon*nu, with arbitrary lawful finite-M2 directing nu on the same (or an enlarged separable) Hilbert ball. Let H_nu,V_nu denote its conditional private-smoothed tails. Fixed-rank implicit derivatives should be Qdot=(H_nu(Q)-H(Q))/h(Q) and fdot=[V_nu(Q)-V(Q)-(h0(Q)/h(Q))(H_nu(Q)-H(Q))]/r, hence |fdot|<=2/a on r>=a. The complete signed suffix-envelope formula should still hold, with this Qdot and optional constant level velocity; proving its integral/expectation interchange is a NEW obligation, not covered by bounded-mean transport.
Key actual-record identity: B'(t)=[1/h(Q(t))-m(u_q(t))/t]/sigma. For almost every r, if an active t>r then it is an interior minimum so B'(t)=0, giving 1/h(Q(t))=m(u_q(t))/t<=m(U)/a. If the active point is t=r, then on the record set M'(r)=B'(r)>=0 a.e., giving 1/h(Q(r))=m(u_q(r))/r+sigma*M'(r). M is locally absolutely continuous, nondecreasing, and S(M(r)) extends absolutely continuously to r=1 with integral phi(M)M'<=1. Since |w|=|(chi f)'|<=||chi'||inf+1/a and integral|w|<=1+log(1/a), the rank integrand's absolute integral is bounded by m(U)[1+log(1/a)]/(a*sigma*sqrt(2pi)) + ||chi'||inf+1/a, uniformly in the Gaussian background and directing law, after retaining the actual active-record structure. This avoids an uncontrolled 1/h estimate in low-density mixture gaps.
For finite difference quotients, conditional total/true tails differ by at most epsilon. Horizontal rank trimming plus a common-Z shift of order epsilon (from u_q(r-epsilon)-u_q(r), r>=a/2) brackets the perturbed rank by the baseline ranks at shifted Z and ranks r±epsilon. Thus the rank-survival difference quotients are controlled by bounded Gaussian-shift terms and horizontal difference quotients of the ABSOLUTELY CONTINUOUS function S(M(r)). Establish their uniform integrability in r, use pointwise all-minima envelope limits plus Vitali convergence, and then the background-uniform bound to average X. This is a possible proof of a full signed mass-variation derivative, not a justification from value continuity alone. Moment derivative is M2(nu)-M2(pi); finite endpoint recovery can then use the approved result after fixing epsilon.
A stronger dual construction may be feasible. Tail-gradient potentials use S((Q(t)-theta-tau_s X(v))/c_s(v))/h(Q(t)), not normalized posterior densities. Arbitrary near-active rational t can have uncontrolled 1/h. Instead allow the EXACT boundary choice t=r only on B(r)=M(r), plus rational t>r with B(t)<=M(r)+1/j and |B'(t)|<=1/j. These hybrid choices are nonempty a.e. and approximate every active minimum. The preceding identity bounds their integrated 1/h weights uniformly per X, including the record M' contribution. Consequently the positive/negative tail potentials are uniformly bounded over ALL particle parameters x and uniformly Lipschitz (indeed C1 in theta with bounded/equicontinuous derivative) at fixed s: private Gaussian tails have bounded derivatives and E|X(v-v')|<=||v-v'||. A countable dense parameter set and these uniform continuity bounds give compact potential sets in pointwise/locally uniform topology; do not assume the strong Hilbert ball compact.
At a true normalized global maximum, apply the complete mass/level derivative to every finite-M2 nu and legal lambda. After centering potentials by their pi integrals and subtracting eta*qU*(theta^2-M2), support-function separation against finitely supported probability measures should match each positive potential with a negative potential so that a combined particle potential P(theta,v,ell)=bulk_mass+Gplus-Gminus-eta*qU*theta^2 is <=kappa for EVERY lawful particle, with equality pi-a.e.; retain the correctly matched level coordinate. Here bulk_mass(x)=E[chi(R)/R*(ell(x)-h0(Q(R))/h(Q(R)))*S((Q(R)-theta-tau_s X(v))/c_s(v))]. Its theta derivative is exactly the earlier mean bulk density, and derivatives of the matched tail potentials give posterior mean gradients. Closed convex potential sets with uniform C1 bounds could therefore yield coherent averaged mean complementarity and an additional global Euler-Lagrange inequality.
At theta=0 the true/false particle potentials differ by the explicit nonnegative tag payoff E[chi(R) S((Q(R)-tau_s X(v))/c_s(v))/R]. The global inequality for a newly added false particle of small positive mean may be useful for bounding true-zero normal flux; it is not yet a uniform inverse-Mills estimate. All selectors are dual descriptions after averaging, never noise-dependent competitors. The product-space separation, mean derivative coherence, global potential equality and any q-scaled consequence require proofs. This route uses global particle-mass optimality that mean transport alone does not supply; do not label it a completed main-bound argument.
