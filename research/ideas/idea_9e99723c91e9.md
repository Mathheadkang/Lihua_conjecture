Candidate elementary lemma to independently check before adoption. Fix sigma in (0,1), s=sqrt(1-sigma²). Allow arbitrary finite N, means, and arbitrary PSD dependence of residual unit Gaussians X_i, independent of Z~N(0,1), with T_i=theta_i+s X_i+sigma Z. True labels mean theta_i<=0. Conditional on Z=z every true p-value is stochastically bounded by F_z(t)=S((S^{-1}(t)-sigma z)/s), regardless of residual dependence. The deterministic BY interval allocation gives conditional FDR <= N0 sum_{k=1}^N [F_z(qk/N)-F_z(q(k-1)/N)]/k <= (N0/N) B_sigma,q(z), where B=q integral_(0,q] dF_z(t)/t = F_z(q)+q integral_0^q F_z(t)/t² dt. IMPORTANT: the first inequality needs justification for nonidentical true means; use each actual conditional F_i first and integration-by-parts monotonicity to replace by F_z (do not assume increments of dominated CDFs are dominated). Equivalently B=q integral_U^infinity phi((u-sigma z)/s)/(s S(u))du. For each fixed z and sigma>0, this is finite and tends to zero with q: choose any 1<a<1/(1-sigma²), then F_z(t)<=C(z,sigma,a)t^a for sufficiently small t. Conditional FDR<=min(1,B); dominated convergence yields sup over all N, means, residual covariances FDR<=E min(1,B_sigma,q(Z)) ->0 as q->0. Constants can blow up as sigma->0, so this is not the unrestricted order target and sigma cannot be sent to zero without additional work. Possible use: control microscopic rejection ranks in a complete parameter-law domain having a fixed common loading, supporting the total-variation continuity needed for a clone-invariant near-maximal principle (idea_29dfa0708abc). This latter continuity and the singular/countable-atom first variation remain unproved. Preserve distinction from independent-residual single-factor source results: here residual dependence is arbitrary; the proof only uses conditional marginals and BY allocation.
