Round 5's failed diffuse zero-mean circle candidate isolates a proof step: partition the compact direction support into cells of vanishing diameter and map each cell to one unit representative, preserving the mean and tag. The induced coupling has vanishing directional Wasserstein cost, and the second mean moment is unchanged; at zero means it stays exactly zero. With q, positive loading sigma and finite dimension fixed, apply the approved full largest-crossing FDR continuity theorem after checking its exact hypotheses. For canonical finite approximation, keep this step separate from rationalization and boundary repair: alternative-tagged zero means must move to positive means tending to zero, whereas true-null zero means may remain zero. Such boundary repair yields moment convergence, not exact zero moment preservation. This is an approximation ingredient supported by the round's obstruction, not a proof of the complete target, general penalized attainment, or finite endpoint derivative transfer.
