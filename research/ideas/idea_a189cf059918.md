Draft NEW finite-array transfer route; no assertion of original unrestricted conjectures, no native proof or approval. It could replace the prior Sard/transverse-root transfer. Fixed q,d,W,regular angular kernel (kappa<=f_theta<=K, A=K/kappa), u>=104A. Exact finite arrays T_j=theta_j+v_j.Z, unit v_j, same fixed latent d. Let F_N(r,omega,x) be full oneS/folded rejection fraction, F its smooth population limit, and G0_N the correctly labelled null subfraction. Assume uniform total and null subCDF convergence on compact z annuli and all real x; weak joint and separately labelled empirical measures imply this (finite z-net plus deterministic |v.(z-z')|<=|z-z'| brackets and continuity of limiting CDF). OneS true nulls are all theta<=0. No moment bounds on theta. Finite BH uses actual largest qualifying rank; its selected statistic cutoff x_N is the smallest finite x>=u with F_N>=sQ(x)/q and at selection F_N=sQ(x_N)/q, by exact maximum-rank grid identity including ties. No contact means infinity. A finite null cannot reject for x_N>r because unit loading and theta<=0 oneS or theta=0 twoS.

Core a=max{u,52A sqrt(d)/u}, b=sqrt(d)+2u. Existing signed-gradient feasible ODE for smooth F: c0=2A Md>0, X'=2c0/(rX), X(r1)=x1>=u, X^2=x1^2+8A Md log(r/r1). On F>0, J=log(qF/(sQ(X))) obeys J'>=c0 beta/r, beta=8A Md sqrt(d)/(rX), 0<beta<=2Md/13<1. Positivity along this exact path follows from the previous argument for every positive starting F; initial F need not be feasible. This quantitative strict drift is key.

Fix clipping T>max(b,u), T1=sqrt(T^2+8A Md log(b/a)); let m1=sQ(T1)/q>0 and gamma=16 A^2 Md^2 sqrt(d)/(b^2 T1)>0 (since c0 beta/r=16 A^2 Md^2 sqrt(d)/(r^2X)). Uniform total-CDF error epsilon_N on [a,b]xSphere x [u,T1] tends zero. For epsilon<m1/2, if actual finite cutoff x_N(r1)<T, starting population F>=sQ(x1)/q-epsilon>0 and J(r1)>=log(1-epsilon/[sQ(x1)/q])>=-2epsilon/m1. Until r2 the ODE stays X<=T1 and J'>=gamma. Thus whenever r2-r1>=delta_N=4epsilon/(m1 gamma), J(r2)>=2epsilon/m1 and F(r2,X)>=sQ(X)/q+2epsilon. The uniform CDF error yields F_N(r2,X)>=sQ(X)/q+epsilon, a strict actual qualification. Hence x_N(r2)<=X(r2). For clipped y_N=min(x_N,T), if x_N(r1)>=T the increment is nonpositive trivially. Therefore for ALL pairs with r2-r1>=delta_N, y_N(r2)^2-y_N(r1)^2<=8A Md log(r2/r1), even if individual finite-array cutoff graphs have arbitrarily many small-scale switches.

Set w_N(r)=r^2-y_N(r)^2, c=1-4A Md/a^2>0. At pairs separated by delta_N, w_N(r2)-w_N(r1)>=c(r2^2-r1^2). This is NOT exact monotonicity at shorter scales. Nevertheless, for every target interval I of length ell, its preimage under w_N in s=r^2 has diameter at most max(ell/c,2b delta_N): any two preimage radii separated by delta_N satisfy the first bound, and closer pairs satisfy the second. Thus the pushforward mu_N of ds obeys mu_N(I)<=ell/c+2b delta_N, without assuming the preimage is an interval. Partition the fixed compact w range [a^2-T^2,b^2-u^2] into bins of width h. For continuous chi-square(d+1) density f, integrate using upper bin sums and interval estimates, then let N->infinity at fixed h and h->0. It follows limsup integral_a^b 2r f(w_N(r))dr<=1/c. This is an approximate co-Lipschitz transport lemma robust to arbitrary finite-array threshold oscillations; no Sard theorem, root simplicity, first-root convergence or generic nondegeneracy is used.
