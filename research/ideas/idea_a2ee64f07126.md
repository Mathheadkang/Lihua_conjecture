Plan-stage side calculation (11 Nov 2026), unverified by a check stage. Global null, T_i=w_i^T Z with unit w_i, u=Phi-bar^{-1}(q), u_k=Phi-bar^{-1}(qk/N)>=u, N_k=#{i:T_i>=u_k}. (1) Dense crossings. f_u(z)=(1/N)sum_i exp(u w_i^T z-u^2/2) is smooth, positive, gamma-normalized and log-convex (log-sum-exp of affine maps), so lehec2015_semilogconvex_density_tail applies with beta=0. On {N_k>=k} with u_k<=u+delta0: f_u>=Phi-bar(u_k)e^{u u_k-u^2/2}/q>=e^{-delta0^2/2}/(2 sqrt(2 pi)(u+delta0)q) by the Mills lower bound. Lehec gives P(exists k with qk/N>=Phi-bar(u+delta0) and N_k>=k)<=C e^{delta0^2/2}(1+delta0/u)q for q small and delta0^2<=log(1/q). No correlation-sign assumption and no union over ranks. (2) Why this does not finish: for sparse ranks (u_k-u large) the same tilt at lambda=u_k costs about q u_k/u per unit threshold window, and one coordinate above u_k+(log k)/u_k already triggers f_lambda, so windows cost about q each and their number grows with log N. Under independence the Simes mass sits in exactly this sparse regime. (3) Nonpositive crossers: if all pairs among the crossers have correlation <=0, Slepian's orthant inequality (not yet in the library) plus a union over k-subsets gives P(N_k>=k for some k>=2)<=sum_{k>=2}(eq)^k. (4) By idea_8699fa13f1f6 the target is equivalent to sup_{N,Sigma}P(E_{q*})<1 at one fixed q*, so any dimension-free bound tending to 0 (even with log(1/q) powers) suffices. The missing step is dimension-free control of sparse crossings whose crossers include weak positive and negative correlations in high-dimensional spread configurations, where bounded positive or nonpositive class covers (idea_665151fb5a87, idea_b2631a978a30) fail because the negative-correlation graph has unbounded fractional chromatic number.
