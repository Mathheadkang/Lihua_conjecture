## Mechanism and scope

This is a new first calculation, not an approved result or a proof of the universal target. It tests a distributional lifting to a Gaussian model with nonnegative covariances, rather than an interpolation budget for the original FDP. Native library search for Gaussian BH, PRDS common noise and Hermite/order-statistic mechanisms found the positive-Gaussian Simes node but no proof section to adapt. No library conclusion is assumed.

Let the full original model have arbitrary means and unit-diagonal PSD covariance. On an independent standard normal Z, choose lambda>=1 and s=sqrt(1+lambda^2), and put Y_i=(T_i+lambda Z)/s. Then Var(Y_i)=1 and Cov(Y_i,Y_j)=(Sigma_ij+lambda^2)/(1+lambda^2)>=0. The mean theta_i/s preserves the full true-null label set, including negative means. This finite construction allows singular original covariance.

## Exact first step: retain the largest crossing

Write u(t)=barPhi^{-1}(t). Conditional on the added Z=z, the original comparison p_i<=t is exactly
$$
barPhi(Y_i)<=c_z(t),\qquad
c_z(t)=barPhi((u(t)+lambda z)/s).
$$
Since c_z is increasing, every rank comparison, multiplicity, nonstrict inequality and the largest qualifying rank are preserved when ordinary BH's thresholds q r/N are replaced by c_z(q r/N). This is an exact random-curve representation; it does not say that conditioning on Z leaves a PRDS Gaussian model.

A possible route would compare this curved-threshold procedure to an ordinary PRDS procedure at a rank-independent random level. Its required scalar inflation factor is
$$
K(z,q)=sup_{0<t<=q} c_z(t)/t.
$$
This factor is infinite for every fixed finite z and every lambda>0. As x=u(t)->infinity, the Gaussian-tail logarithm gives
$$
log(c_z(t)/t)=\frac{lambda^2}{2(1+lambda^2)}x^2
-\frac{lambda z}{1+lambda^2}x+O(log x+1),
$$
so the positive quadratic dominates.

## Second first test: rank-dependent preservation cost

To preserve the original numerical threshold t itself after lifting requires c_Z(t)<=t, equivalently
$$
Z>=(s-1)u(t)/lambda.
$$
The probability of this added-noise event tends to zero as t decreases to zero. Thus a rank-independent positive preservation probability is unavailable when N can grow without bound. Taking the actual selected rank before selecting this noise event is anticipative and needs a new proof; independence of Z from the original model alone does not justify replacing its probability by a fixed constant.

## Status and remaining constructive possibility

The Gaussian covariance lift and the exact curved rank comparisons are proved calculations. They do not refute the target or every noise-lifting route. A successful version would have to use a joint rank-and-noise allocation, keeping the full curve c_z(qr/N), rather than its infinite supremum or a uniform preservation event. Such an allocation has not been supplied, so this record is not an executable main-target plan.
