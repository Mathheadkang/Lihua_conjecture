Concrete exact representation to rederive and audit before treating as a lemma. Retain original actual BH, f=V/(R∨1), Q=f gamma/F, U,H and heat fields. Enlarge Q by a terminal label L=(I,R), sampling I uniformly from the actual selected true nulls. For l=(i,r), define w_l(G)=1{R=r,i selected true}/r and c_l=v_i/h(u_r). Then sum_l w_l=f, B=sum_l c_l w_l, and for t<1 the label posterior pi_l,t=P_{1-t}w_l(W_t)/P_{1-t}f(W_t). Hence a_t=sum_l pi_l,t c_l exactly, with every rank and label retained.

Set v_t=gradient log P_{1-t}f, and v_l,t=gradient log P_{1-t}w_l on nonzero-probability labels. After identifying the Q Brownian innovation W^Q, Ito quotient calculus should give d pi_l=pi_l(v_l−v)·dW^Q and
K_t=sum_l pi_l (c_l−a_t) tensor (v_l−v).

Finite-label entropy Ito calculus, with localization and regularization of log at zero, gives E_Q integral sum_l pi_l|v_l−v|² dt=2 I_Q(L;G), where I_Q(L;G)=H_Q(L)−E_Q log V is finite for each finite model. Let y=H² E_Q[1/h(u_R)²], so x<=y<=1. The integrated conditional mark variance is E_Q integral sum_l pi_l|c_l−a_t|² dt=(y−x)/H². Thus Cauchy–Schwarz on the EXACT signed trace supplies

|M| <= (F/H) sqrt{2 I_Q(L;G)(y−x)}.

This is a concrete information certificate, not the desired q-scaled mixed budget: the information term can grow with model complexity, and the prefactor F/H cannot be replaced by q without proof. One must retain correlations in the trace if this Cauchy certificate is too expensive. It is not a Gaussian-mixture assumption: conditional laws given L are actual BH-truncated densities, and their drift fields are derived from w_l.

Possible next investigation: coarsen the radial Mills mark while controlling the approximation by 1−x, and use the selected-index mutual-information identity from idea_96ead5c9a062 to relate label information to actual selected fractions rather than log N alone. No such uniform estimate is presently proved. The general finite-entropy Wiener drift source lehec2013_follmer_entropy_energy supports the Brownian representation once properly approved/linked or reproved; it does not on its own establish the explicit heat-gradient identification or the mixed trace estimate. This proposal preserves the exact largest-rank event and does not assume independent label components.
