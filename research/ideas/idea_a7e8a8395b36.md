Verified primary analogue: Klivans, O'Donnell, Servedio, Learning Geometric Concepts via Gaussian Surface Area (author manuscript dated 23 Dec 2008), https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~odonnell/papers/perimeter.pdf , section 4.3 printed page 9 equation (5) and Theorem 20. Equation (5), attributed there to Nazarov, bounds integral over boundary of phi_d(y)/(1+h(y)) by 1-gamma(K) for convex K containing the origin. Elementary sharper polyhedral candidate to audit: for a full-dimensional closed convex polyhedron K with distinct facets F_j, outward unit normals n_j and supporting constants b_j=n_j dot y, the sets {y+t n_j: y in relative interior F_j,t>0} are disjoint because their nearest point projections onto K are y. The Gaussian mass of each such tube is integral_Fj phi_d(y) dH^{d-1}(y) times integral_0^infinity exp(-b_j t-t²/2)dt = m(b_j) gamma_surface(F_j). Thus sum_j m(b_j) gamma_surface(F_j)<=1-gamma(K); no dimension or face count. This is a convex proxy calculation, not a BH bound. Actual largest-crossing BH/FDP cells need not form one convex body's boundary, outward tubes from different cells may overlap, and replacing signed FDP jumps by absolute per-face charges already failed historically. Any adaptation must supply a signed cancellation/controlled-overlap argument retaining actual ranks, true labels, and a q-scaled budget. Do not apply the convex inequality separately to every BH cell and sum without a new summability proof. No source node/import added because the transfer to the main target remains unresolved.
