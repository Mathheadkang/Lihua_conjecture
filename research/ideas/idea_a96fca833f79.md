Round 2 suggests a local proof obligation: before assembling the signed original-rank face-jump formula, control saddle clipping and fractional-copy contributions outside the principal displacement at the scale s/t_s. In the fixed strongly anticorrelated two-null diagnostic, upper-saddle clipping is O(s^2) and the separate low-entry branch has exponentially small mass. In the independent N=2 mixed model theta=(-1,+1), Sigma=I, q=1/4, retaining the fractional true-null numerator and both root births gives a fractional-FDP error O(s^2/t_s^2), and the checked compensated coefficient 0.03608922386092275 agrees with the proposed formula. These are fixed-model calculations, not general or uniform bounds. A general approach must prove an appropriate integrated remainder estimate while preserving the actual common largest crossing, root switches, rank cascades, and signed jumps; it cannot infer this estimate from fixed-K bounds or exchange the limits.
