Round 4's two reported failed families suggest isolating an exact reduction before attempting the general survival derivative. If both lawful directing laws have only true-tagged particles, then for every mixture and conditional background V_epsilon=H_epsilon. Consequently f_epsilon(r)=1 at every positive interior rank (and under the true-mass endpoint convention at rank one); its fixed-rank mass derivative is zero. Equivalently, the proposed numerator derivative cancels exactly since h_0=h and V_nu-V=H_nu-H. Hence the cutoff objective is exactly E[chi_a(R_epsilon)], and every nonzero first-order response must come from the actual-rank distribution. The reported all-true rightward zero-boundary insertion and antipodal true-mass transfer both fail NOT because their actual-rank response agrees with the asserted rank term. Use this cancellation as a proof decomposition step, while retaining the full all-active-minima survival analysis. This identity alone proves neither the survival differentiation/interchanges for arbitrary all-true laws nor the target for mixed true/false laws; general competing minima, endpoint domination, and finite recovery remain open obligations.
