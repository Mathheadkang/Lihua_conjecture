Scoped proof step reconstructed in round 5 from cand_gaussian_bh_mixed_coherence_trace_budget_r5_s1_three_null_directions; this records an approach, not a native-approved theorem or a proof of the unrestricted mixed budget. It complements the search consequence already recorded in idea_0e8aa4e76eba.

For each finite Gaussian BH model with k distinct oriented true-null directions w_g and F>0, let alpha_g be the actual fraction of selected true nulls in direction g and eta=H/h(u_R). Under the original FDP tilt, add a terminal auxiliary label J with probabilities p_g=eta alpha_g and p_0=1-eta, carrying marks c_g=w_g/H and c_0=0. Then E[c_J|G]=B/f exactly. This randomization absorbs the actual rank amplitude without adding rank labels and leaves the Brownian filtration and prescribed tilt unchanged. Establish bounded-mark martingale energy, innovation Brownian motion, absolute trace/drift integrability, and the endpoint identity before using trace estimates.

For pi_j(t)=E[p_j(G)|F_t] and score g_j=grad log pi_j, finite-label entropy localization gives E int sum_j pi_j ||g_j||^2 dt=2 I(J;G)<=2log(k+1). The auxiliary label is randomized conditional on G, so its terminal conditional entropy is retained. The exact covariance factorization K_t=sum_j pi_j(c_j-a_t) tensor g_j and integrated variance E int sum_j pi_j||c_j-a_t||^2 dt=(E_Q eta-x)/H^2<=(1-x)/H^2 imply |M|<=(F/H)sqrt(2log(k+1)(1-x)). A true rejection in any direction requires w_g dot G>=U, hence F<=kq. For k<=3 and H>=3 this yields |M|<=2q sqrt(1-x), uniform over arbitrary finite N,d, conservative null means, singular covariance, alternatives, multiplicities and rank cascades; for every fixed k it yields a k-dependent finite constant.

The unresolved proof obligation is a bound uniform as k grows: the displayed k sqrt(log(k+1))/H coefficient need not remain bounded. No angular compression, Gaussian-mixture representation, or complexity-free entropy estimate has been proved. In particular this step excludes bounded-k unbounded-ratio candidate sequences but neither proves the general mixed budget nor establishes that unbounded k suffices for a counterexample.
