Route 3 of the researcher-authorized parallel round of 12 November 2026. UNPROVED, high-risk draft direction; no project file previously used this theory.

**Core idea.** The approved compactification (gaussian_bh_exchangeable_array_variational_compactification) represents worst-case models by jointly exchangeable marked Gram arrays (Dovbysh-Sudakov, approved library node lib_panchenko2009_dovbysh_sudakov_mixture). In spin-glass theory, arrays whose overlaps satisfy the Ghirlanda-Guerra identities have ultrametric support almost surely: D. Panchenko, The Parisi ultrametricity conjecture, Ann. of Math. 177 (2013) 383-393, DOI 10.4007/annals.2013.177.1.8; finite-valued precursor arXiv:0810.0743. (Checked at record level only, 12 Nov 2026; not imported.)

**New step.** In spin glasses the identities come from stability under small random perturbations of the Hamiltonian plus convexity/concentration of the free energy. Seek the analogue: derive Ghirlanda-Guerra-type identities for overlaps at regularized worst-case maximizers from stability under a random family of small perturbations, using the approved first-variation results and the approved global particle-potential certificate.

**Payoff.** If extremal null-direction arrays are ultrametric, Route 1 only needs to work on trees; the tree-structured direction idea (idea_38a72ab5ee7e) and the draft two-level tree frozen-ray result target exactly that case.

**First test (cheap).** Average the approved first-variation identities over a random perturbation family and check whether anything of Ghirlanda-Guerra shape appears. If the FDR functional has no convex/concentration structure playing the role of the free energy, mark this route obstructed with that reason.
