Suggested by the teaching chat; agent-origin proposed bridge after the verified common-loading continuity theorem. This is a new approach to test, not an extension already established by the finite clone formulas. It can be considered after the current isolated-fold prototype; do not turn every work package below into a new main goal.

## Main change in order of operations

Optimize a regularized, rank-truncated functional FIRST, instead of optimizing full FDR and then discarding a small-rank term inside a difference quotient. The approved microscopic-rank bound is a statement about values, not derivatives: o_a(1)/t need not be small. This proposal avoids that unsupported division.

Fix q,sigma in (0,1/2)x(0,1). Let W have a fixed smooth density supported inside (0,1). Define the deterministic parameter-law smoothing S_h by replacing each parameter mean by theta-hW for true tags and theta+hW for alternative tags, with the same direction and tag. W here indexes a deterministic mixture of means; it is not extra observation noise. Thus all resulting testing statistics remain exactly Gaussian with unit variance in finite canonical approximations.

Define F_a(pi)=E[A/(r vee a)], where r is the actual largest crossing and A=V_pi(r). Put G_{h,a}=F_a o S_h.

For using a C1 version of the BV chain rule, a convenient alternative is A/d_a(r): take a smooth nondecreasing d_a with d_a>=max(r,a), d_a=2a for r<=a, d_a=r for r>=3a, and 0<=d_a'<=1. Such an interpolation can be constructed on [a,3a]. Then the difference from full FDP is still confined to r<3a. State which cutoff is used throughout; do not use a C1 chain-rule theorem directly on the nonsmooth max without justification.

## Package 1: a complete-space variational problem

Check from the already verified joint rank and numerator stability that each fixed-a F_a is W1-continuous. This does not follow merely from closeness to full FDR.

S_h is W1-continuous. Same-tag couplings are contractions, but across different tags use the safe bound
W1(S_h mu,S_h nu)<=(1+2h E W)W1(mu,nu).
Also W1(S_h pi,pi)<=h E W.

The verified microscopic-rank estimate gives, uniformly in pi at fixed q,sigma,
0<=F(pi)-F_a(pi)<=B_{q,sigma}(a)->0
(or B_{q,sigma}(3a) for the smooth cutoff).
It follows that sup_pi G_{h,a}(pi) recovers sup_pi F(pi) as h,a tend to zero. For the lower bound fix ONE near-maximizing finite canonical model, apply value continuity as h goes to zero, and then the uniform rank-cutoff bound. This requires no uniform h-continuity modulus or compact optimizer.

Apply Ekeland to -G_{h,a} on the complete marked P1 space, with all regularization parameters fixed, to obtain a near-maximal pi_* and
G_{h,a}(nu)<=G_{h,a}(pi_*)+eta W1(nu,pi_*) for every admissible nu.
Ekeland 1974 Theorem 1.1 supplies this metric variational principle; it supplies none of the BH derivative identities below.

## Package 2: actual deterministic, label-preserving paths

For a bounded deterministic field psi(theta,v,ell), independent of X,Z, use
theta_t=min(0,theta+t psi) for true tags,
theta_t=max(0,theta+t psi) for alternative tags.
Let pi_t be the pushforward of pi_* by this map, leaving v and ell fixed. Projection ensures a common legal t-range even if mass accumulates near zero, and
W1(pi_t,pi_*)<=t integral |psi| d pi_*.
Hence the genuine upper Dini inequality is
limsup_{t down 0}[G_{h,a}(pi_t)-G_{h,a}(pi_*)]/t
 <= eta integral |psi| d pi_*.
No ordinary derivative at the Ekeland point is presumed.

The base-law zero-mean boundary remains one-sided: true-tag theta=0 cannot move upward; alternative-tag theta=0 cannot move downward. Smoothing its image into the interior does NOT remove this base constraint. Interior two-sided variations can be tested with fields supported a positive distance from zero.

## Package 3: joint BV instead of enumerating isolated folds

Condition on the residual isonormal X. Write r_t(z)=rho_{S_h pi_t}(X,z) and A_t(z)=V_{S_h pi_t}(r_t(z);X,z). With the common z shift, rejected sets are nested. Thus both r_t and A_t are nondecreasing in z and
0<=A_t(z_2)-A_t(z_1)<=r_t(z_2)-r_t(z_1).

If |psi|<=M, common underlying parameter marks give the coordinatewise comparison
f_{t+s}(z) in [f_t(z-M|s|/sigma),f_t(z+M|s|/sigma)],
for f=r,A, at legal parameter times. Establish this using the ACTUAL largest crossing and the full rejection set, not only a chosen root.

Proposed next lemma: these cone comparisons imply local BV regularity of (t,z)->(r_t,A_t), with distributional measure bounds
|D_t r|<=(M/sigma)D_z r,
|D_t A|<=(M/sigma)D_z A.
Apply the BV chain rule to A/d_a(r). It retains smooth, jump, and possible Cantor contributions without assuming generic folds, finitely many folds, or finite factor dimension.

For a positive-rank untruncated jump, the EXACT ratio change is
A_+/r_+ - A_-/r_-
 = (A_+-A_-)/r_+ - A_-(r_+-r_-)/(r_-r_+).
This includes both new null mass and dilution of the old numerator. A continuous chain rule at a jump, or taking positive parts separately, loses the needed cancellation. For the cutoff use the corresponding exact difference with d_a.

A useful intermediate target is Var_z[A/(r vee a)]<=1+2 log(1/a), or a safely larger C[1+log(1/a)] bound for the smooth cutoff. First prove the deterministic variation bound from 0<=dA<=dr, including jumps. Its purpose is domination and finite-difference control, not payment of the final q-scale budget.

BV derivatives exist as measures and ordinary derivatives only almost everywhere. Do not silently assume differentiability at the particular Ekeland point t=0; retain one-sided finite differences, Dini bounds, or correctly integrated measure responses.

## Recovery and the remaining mathematical gap

For each FIXED t>0, only after deriving the population finite-difference formula, choose canonical finite approximations of its two endpoints using joint rank/numerator continuity. Approximation errors in the truncated objective can be chosen o(t). This avoids requesting an N-uniform radius from a finite clone derivative formula.

A truncated-objective difference is not the derivative of ordinary full FDR. The passage back to full FDR occurs at the LEVEL OF SUPREMUM VALUES as a,h tend to zero; never divide the rank-cutoff error by t.

The bridge alone does not prove the main bound. The next substantive requirement is to extract from the FULL signed response a near-maximality inequality implying a universal q sqrt(log(1/q)) bound, with no surviving h^{-1}, sigma^{-1}, log(1/a), direction-count, or dimension loss. If the response only gives such parameter-dependent bounds, report that precise remaining obstruction.

Suggested sequencing: verify continuity/Ekeland applicability and the joint-BV lemma first, then test the resulting signed stationarity inequalities on the two-direction fold and independent-null boundary example. The finite rare-clone formula remains a diagnostic special case, not an automatically valid nonatomic derivative.
