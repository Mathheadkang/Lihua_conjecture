Round 4 yields a deterministic proof step: partition the N coordinates into nonempty classes of sizes n_j and define E_{q,j} as the classwise Simes event at level q. If the full event E_q crosses at rank k, the counts r_j of class-j p-values at most qk/N sum to at least k. Some r_j/n_j is at least k/N, so that class crosses because p_{(r_j),j} <= qk/N <= q r_j/n_j. Thus E_q is contained in the union of E_{q,j}, without independence between classes. Classwise bounds P(E_{q,j}) <= C_j q imply P(E_q) <= q sum_j C_j. This explains the supplied 2q failures for adjacent-difference chains (odd/even independent classes) and signed one-factor models (two sign classes with the reported nonnegative-correlation Simes control). The combinatorial implication is established directly; these model-specific inputs are taken from the round summaries, not independently rechecked. Proposed missing proof step: replace the class-count-dependent union bound by a dimension-independent estimate, or prove a justified reduction of every centered Gaussian covariance to controlled classes with uniformly bounded total constants. No such general reduction is established here. Unbounded partition complexity remains an obstruction to this route, not evidence of a counterexample. The conjecture remains unresolved.
