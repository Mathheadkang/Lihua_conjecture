Suggested by this chat while independently checking which observations to save at the researcher's request. Draft countercheck to a proposed stronger sufficient condition, not a counterexample to the Simes asset, and not a native verified-counterexample verdict.

## The proposed shortcut

For $a\ge2$, write $\phi,\Phi,\bar\Phi$ for the standard Gaussian density, distribution and survival functions, and let $h=\phi/\bar\Phi$. For centered unit-variance Gaussian coordinates $Z_1,\ldots,Z_N$, define
$$
H_a(Z)=\max_{\varnothing\ne I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}}
\left\{\frac1{|I|}\sum_{i\in I}Z_i
-h\!\left(\bar\Phi^{-1}\!\frac{|I|}{aN}\right)\right\}.
$$
The [hinge formulation](idea_e1c816206945.md) identifies $\{H_a\le0\}$ with hinge acceptance. A tempting stronger sufficient condition is $\mathbb EH_A\le0$ for one universal finite $A$. For a convex Gaussian functional, an Ehrhard-quantile/Jensen argument would make this sufficient for a median at most zero, hence acceptance probability at least one half. That sufficiency needs its usual degenerate-law conventions checked; it does not rescue the proposed expectation inequality, which fails.

## Countercheck for every fixed finite a

Fix $0<\varepsilon<1$. Set $m_N=\lfloor\varepsilon N\rfloor$ and $n_N=N-m_N$. Let the first $m_N$ coordinates be exact copies of one standard normal $X$. Let the remaining coordinates $Y_1,\ldots,Y_{n_N}$ be mutually independent standard normals, independent of $X$. All correlations are nonnegative.

Singleton subsets among the independent coordinates imply
$$
H_a\ge D_N:=\max_{i\le n_N}Y_i-h\!\left(\bar\Phi^{-1}(1/(aN))\right).
$$
Standard Gaussian maximum and quantile asymptotics give $D_N\to0$ in $L^1$: both deterministic centerings are $\sqrt{2\log N}+o(1)$, and the centered maximum has $L^1$ fluctuations tending to zero.

The subset consisting of all clones gives
$$
H_a\ge X-c_N,\qquad
c_N=h\!\left(\bar\Phi^{-1}(m_N/(aN))\right)\longrightarrow
c=h\!\left(\bar\Phi^{-1}(\varepsilon/a)\right)<\infty.
$$
Thus $H_a\ge\max(D_N,X-c_N)$. Since the maximum is Lipschitz in both arguments, this lower bound converges in $L^1$ to $(X-c)_+$. Therefore
$$
\liminf_{N\to\infty}\mathbb EH_a
\ge\mathbb E(X-c)_+
=\phi(c)-c\bar\Phi(c)>0.
$$
It follows that no fixed finite $a\ge2$ satisfies the proposed nonpositive-mean criterion uniformly.

## Meaning

The model is already within the known nonnegative-correlation Simes class. The countercheck invalidates only the stronger expected-maximum shortcut. It leaves uniform positive acceptance probability, a suitable median bound, the hinge weak-tail conjecture and the original Gaussian Simes conjecture untouched. Preserve the Gaussian-max $L^1$ asymptotics as an explicit verification obligation if this observation is promoted.
