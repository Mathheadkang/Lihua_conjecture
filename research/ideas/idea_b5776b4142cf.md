Source-level check, 12 November 2026 (arXiv HTML text only; not imported as library nodes). Serves [the global-null Simes question](idea_b897932e6b15.md).

- Chi, Ramdas and Wang, Multiple testing under negative dependence, arXiv:2212.09706v4 (Bernoulli 31(2), 2025), Theorem 8, one-sided p-values $\Phi(-Y_k)$: (i) nonnegative correlations give size at most $\alpha$; (ii) nonpositive correlations give size at most $\alpha+2\alpha^2+6\alpha^3$ for $\alpha\le0.1$ (at most $1.26\alpha$; Lemma 7 shows nonpositive Gaussian correlations are weakly negatively dependent); (iii) any Gaussian dependence with fixed $K$: $\limsup_{\alpha\downarrow0}\Pr(S_K\le\alpha)/\alpha\le1$, not uniform in $K$. Verbatim: "It remains an open question to find a useful upper bound for P(S_K(P) <= alpha) over all Gaussian-dependent P for practical values of alpha such as 0.05 or 0.1" and "A simple conjecture is that (20) or a similar inequality holds for all Gaussian-dependent P, but a proof seems to be beyond our current techniques." They also suggest that mixed dependence, such as positive within groups and negative across groups, may admit intermediate bounds.
- Hochberg and Rom (1995) and Samuel-Cahn (1996): one-sided Simes is slightly anticonservative for negatively correlated normals; for $K=2$ at $\alpha=0.05$ the size is about $0.0501$ (as quoted by Chi, Ramdas and Wang). Block, Savits and Wang (JSPI 2008): the Simes inequality reverses for broad negatively dependent classes.
- X. Zhang, Exact Simes inequalities for two-sided Gaussian tests, arXiv:2611.02513 (Nov 2026): two-sided validity for each fixed $m$ at small enough levels uniformly in the covariance, for bounded-degree correlation graphs, and for slowly growing independent blocks; the general two-sided case is stated open.
- No paper found proving a dimension-free bound for one-sided Gaussian Simes with mixed-sign correlations. Searches: standard and extended web searches on 12 November 2026; Scholar not used.
