Alternative full one-sided proof program, distinct from full-factor conditional envelopes and per-null signed-face domination. Not a proved estimate. Keep actual heterogeneous null means, fixed I0 and arbitrary alternative means throughout.

Interpolate Sigma_t=(1-t)I+t Sigma, 0<=t<1, with unit diagonal; write F(t)=E[FDP(theta+G_t)]. At t=0 independent superuniform nulls give F(0)<=q*pi0 using the approved leave-one-out input. For each fixed finite model, the BH FDP is bounded and piecewise constant on the coordinate grid b_ik=barPhi^-1(qk/N)-theta_i. Gaussian covariance differentiation / integration by parts should give F'(t)=(1/2)sum_{i!=j} Sigma_ij E[partial_i partial_j FDP(theta+G_t)] in the distributional sense. Because the diagonal covariance derivative is zero, only mixed derivatives matter; for i!=j these are codimension-two grid-corner measures with ACTUAL mixed FDP jumps, not a surrogate at zero null means. Prove this by cellwise integration or smoothing, explicitly derive every corner coefficient (a BH rank can jump by more than one), then integrate t and pass to singular endpoint using bounded finite-model continuity away from fixed grid boundaries.

Main candidate analytic task: aggregate signed null/null, null/alternative, and alternative/alternative corner contributions using the PSD Gram structure, possibly Mehler expansions / Schur-product positivity, before taking absolute values. Need a concrete dimension/mean-uniform integrated estimate giving q*u_q/sqrt(2pi)+o(q*u_q). Do not simply restate the target as a black-box corner lemma; derive a usable kernel inequality or locate a finite-model failure. Covariance convexity, fixed-mean rank-one extremality, positivity of mixed FDP differences, monotonic F(t), and termwise ordering must NOT be assumed. The prior face plan's local kernel/nonpositivity comparisons failed; the actual boundary-null contribution can individually exceed every uniform (q/N)sqrt(log1/q) bound (idea_f733876e24f5), and even optimized independent-residual full-factor envelopes fail (idea_b4d004ae71a6). The possible merit here is preserving cross-null allocation globally.

This may still fail or yield only BY. Uniform N, q-dependent means/Sigma, nonpositive composite null means, t→1 singularity, and the correct leading constant are all essential obligations. The exact formulas, any auxiliary inequalities and endpoint arguments require independent verification before any theorem claim.
