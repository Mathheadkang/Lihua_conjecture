Unverified auxiliary counterexample candidate, derived by the search driver during read-only route assessment; NOT a counterexample to the project's Gaussian FDR order target and NOT independently verified.
Proposed gate under examination: after replacing each negative true-null coordinate separately by its centered upper bound, sum the positive jumps of h_i(x)=min{barPhi(x)/tau_i(x),1}, tau_i(x)=q R_i(x)/N, and bound their residual-averaged sum by C N(1+U), U=barPhi^{-1}(q), uniformly over all models.
Candidate: fix 0<q<1/2 and integer M>=2, N=2M. Let Z be standard normal. For M true nulls set T_i=-B+Z. For k=2,...,M+1 set the M alternative statistics T_{M+k-1}=u_k+a_k-Z, where u_k=barPhi^{-1}(qk/N), a_k=barPhi^{-1}(qk/(2N)), and choose B=3a_2. All null means are strictly negative, all alternative means positive, and the covariance is v v^T with M entries +1 and M entries -1 in v: unit diagonal, PSD, singular rank one.
For any true null i, X_i=T_i-theta_i=Z and all regression residuals vanish. Force only p_i to zero and use ordinary maximal-rank BH. On x<=a_2 all other null statistics are negative and cannot satisfy a threshold <=q. A rank k in {2,...,M+1} qualifies exactly when x<=a_k, while ranks above M+1 cannot qualify. Thus R_i(x)=max({1} union {k:x<=a_k}) on this region.
At x=a_k the one-sided limiting forced rank drops from k to k-1. Since barPhi(a_k)=qk/(2N), h_i(a_k-)=1/2 and h_i(a_k+)=k/[2(k-1)] (including value 1 for k=2). The proposed positive jump is therefore 1/[2(k-1)]. Summing these jumps gives H_M/2 per true null and at least M H_M/2 over all M nulls, where H_M=sum_{l=1}^M 1/l. The residual expectation is trivial.
If independently verified, this contradicts any universal C N(1+U) bound for this centered positive flux: at fixed q let M tend to infinity, or along q down to zero take M=ceil(exp(U^2)). It does not contradict the actual FDR goal, because the numerator barPhi(x) comes from a separate counterfactual centering for each null; the actual null rejection event uses x-B. A valid replacement route should retain the real negative mean weights or establish a different aggregate cancellation. Preserve the original approach idea and mark this as a candidate obstruction only until fresh review.

## Independent auxiliary review

After the candidate above was recorded, the root driver relayed the verdict of the fresh, derivation-independent agent `/root/audit_flux_gate_negative_nulls` (created with `fork_turns: none`). The reviewer also checked that the actual FDR is at most barPhi(B+U)<q: any false rejection requires Z>=B+U. Thus this object does not refute either current Gaussian FDR order target.
