Unverified plan-stage observation (12 November 2026). For any real f on [0,1], V_-(f) = f(0) - f(1) + V_+(f) when the total variation is finite (and V_-(f) >= f(0)-f(1) always). At a loaded maximizer f(0) = B^sigma(q_*) and f(1) <= q_*, so q_* + V_-(f) >= B^sigma(q_*). The maximizer variation lemma therefore contains B^sigma(q_*) <= 1-delta uniformly in sigma, hence B(q_*) <= 1-delta by part 6 of gaussian_simes_schur_array_compactification, which by the approved gaussian_simes_fixed_level_amplification is the uniform linear all-null Simes bound itself. Every lever supplied by maximality is a comparison of a competitor with B: the global inequality f(t) <= f(0), the global comparisons Phi(g o P*) <= Phi(P*) for Schoenberg maps g (power series with nonnegative coefficients and g(1)=1, including constants c in [0,1], i.e. dilution and common loading), and the first-order conditions, which by the Price formula read: the double integral of (g(rho)-rho) J against mu x mu is <= 0 for every such g. These bound other values above by B, so at best they force V_+ = 0 (a monotone path). They give no upper bound on B and no control of the descent B - f(1), which is exactly the dimension-uniform signed pair-sum estimate that failed for few-block compression (idea_5c178744c4ad) and the sparse budget (idea_3829e8b6542a). A contradiction route would need a self-improvement map sending a near-maximizer with value close to 1 to a lawful competitor of strictly larger value, uniformly as sigma tends to 0; block amplification only relates B at different levels and does not supply this. So maximality is not by itself an executable mechanism for this lemma.
