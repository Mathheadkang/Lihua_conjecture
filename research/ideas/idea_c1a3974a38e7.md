The round-2 sparse-left-tail failure suggests a scoped proof step for fixed-level, stochastically leftward mass contamination. If H_nu,X(z) <= H_X(z) for every z, then Q_epsilon,X(t) <= Q_X(t) and B_epsilon,X(t) >= B_X(t). For a compact rank interval with upper endpoint b < 1, choose one fixed t0 in (b,1). Continuity of the perturbed quantile at t0 bounds B_epsilon,X(t0) by a finite C_X for all sufficiently small epsilon. Since B_X(t) tends to +infinity as t approaches one, B_epsilon,X(t) > C_X near one, so no suffix minimizer for r <= b can enter that endpoint region. This yields an X-dependent common compact localization for this monotone direction before applying an envelope argument. It does not establish localization for arbitrary mass/level perturbations, an X-uniform bound, the signed expectation interchange, or the target lemma.
