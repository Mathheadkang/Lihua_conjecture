Both bound the downward variation of the full Simes size along the Schur-square path, but on different objects. The clone-block bound is for every finite model with at most K distinct directions, unloaded, with a budget K^2 4^K q that grows with K. The maximizer lemma is only at loaded population maximizers in the compact array space, along the residual-loaded path (1-sigma^2)R_t + sigma^2 11^T, which is not the Schur path of any finite matrix, with budget 1-delta-q_*. Neither covers the other: maximizers need not be few-block, and clone-block models need not be maximizers.
