Route 5 of the researcher-authorized parallel round of 12 November 2026. UNPROVED, speculative draft direction; essentially new to the project.

**Core idea.** At small q, null exceedances of BH thresholds are rare and arrive in correlated clusters; FDP behaves like a ratio of compound-Poisson counts, and inflation means that rejections concentrate on clusters where the total rejection count R is small. Model V and R by a compound-Poisson (Poisson-cluster) approximation and bound E[V/R] through the cluster-size law.

**External inputs.** Chen-Stein compound-Poisson approximation: Arratia, Goldstein and Gordon, Statist. Sci. 5 (1990) 403-434; Barbour, Novak and Xia, compound Poisson approximation for the distribution of extremes, Adv. Appl. Probab. (explicit Wasserstein bounds from finite-sequence properties only); Aldous's Poisson clumping heuristic as the guiding picture. Checked at record level only, 12 Nov 2026. Project hooks: approved conditional compound calibration and conditional sub-Poisson calibration.

**Known obstruction.** Classical normal-comparison (Berman / Li-Shao) error terms sum over all pairs and are not dimension-free. So the realistic use is (i) to predict the geometry of extremal families, and (ii) to attack the rare-cluster cap L2 of Route 1: show that clusters of mass < q^C behave like independent Poisson points at the BH level.

**Failure criterion.** If no dimension-free cluster decomposition at angular scale about 1/U can be defined, record the route as heuristic only.
