UNVERIFIED observation from the route merge of 12 Nov 2026. The draft ultrametric-covariance order bound restricts to covariances with nonnegative ultrametric (tree) correlations, so every correlation is nonnegative. Lei (2026, p. 4 of the imported transcription) states that for one-sided Gaussian p-values, nonnegative correlations between every true null and every other hypothesis make the p-values PRDS, and Benjamini and Yekutieli (2001) then give FDR control at level q. If this applies with the composite null set theta_i <= 0 (the Lei transcription treats composite nulls; check the monotonicity in negative null means), the lemma holds with constant 1 and needs no chaining on trees. Benjamini-Yekutieli 2001 is not in the library; importing its PRDS theorem (scoped) would settle this. This does not affect the null-only tree variant, where alternatives may be negatively correlated with nulls.
