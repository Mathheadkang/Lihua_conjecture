New candidate, not independently verified. For d-dimensional Gaussian factors choose b with P(||Z||>b)=q and truncate conditional FDP on the ball. Each null projection Y_i lies in [-b,b]. Crucial correction: source envelope integral at b>u_q cannot replace u_q by b in its gamma coefficient. For one-sided tests, monotonicity F_{r,y}(t) in y gives F_{r,y}(t)barPhi(y)<=E[F_{r,Y}(t)1(Y>=y)]<=t, so the added envelope integral from u_q to b is at most log(q/barPhi(b)). This suggests FDR<=q+pi0 q(1/2+u_q/sqrt(2pi)+log(q/barPhi(b))). For fixed d, b²=2L+(d-2)log L+O_d(1) and u_q²=2L-log L+O(1), L=log(1/q), giving added cost O_d(q log L)=o(q sqrt L). It may prove the sharp one-sided constant for any fixed factor dimension, a genuine broader class than one common factor. Sufficient growing d condition d log L=o(sqrt L) needs separate uniform quantile proof. Rank-one source lower construction embeds for sharpness. Two-sided radial analogue recovers only twice target constant without an average gamma loading restriction. An initially proposed simpler extension failed at r=1: truncated ratio integral is log(q/barPhi(b)), not bounded by 1/2.
