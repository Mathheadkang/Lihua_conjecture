Different rare sets: the rare-direction cap removes nulls whose own kappa_2/u angular neighbourhood has null mass < q^{C_2} (residual loadings allowed); the separated cap removes whole clusters of a separated partition with cluster mass < q^{C_2} (unit loadings). A member of a small cluster can have a heavy neighbourhood through adjacent clusters, and neighbourhood rarity does not give a partition, so neither implies the other.
