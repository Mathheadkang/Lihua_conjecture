## Purpose and provenance

The root introduced the global Parseval random-basis cover in idea_ee0372421118. Its exact two-column inclusion probability is c_i c_j det(Gamma_{ij}), where Gamma is the physical null Gram matrix. This suggests retaining determinants before estimating conditional Gaussian densities. The calculations below give a genuine determinant gain without inverse covariance and pass physical replication. They are agent draft derivations, not independent native verification or a main-target proof.

The scalar marked-tail inequality used here is rederived in idea_d15898e5f9f1, Section 2: for any jointly standard Gaussian Z,Y,D with Cov(Y,D)=0 and A>=1,
$$
\sup_y\phi(y)E[|D|1_{\{Z>=A\}}\mid Y=y]\le C\phi(A).
$$
Any additional actual-rank mark M in [0,1] can remain on the left. No independence between the mark, velocity or other test scores is required.

## Exact two-null determinant kernel

Let X_1,X_2 be standard Gaussian null noises with correlation rho, and let Y,D be any standard Gaussian exposure/velocity with Cov(Y,D)=0. For u>=1 and theta_1,theta_2<=0,
$$
(1-rho^2)\sup_y\phi(y)
E[|D|1_{\{X_1+\theta_1>=u,\ X_2+\theta_2>=u\}}M\mid Y=y]
\le \frac{C\phi(u)}{u^2}. \tag{DP2}
$$
Thus the determinant-weighted pair kernel has scale at most C barPhi(u)/u, rather than barPhi(u)u.

For rho>-1 the average has variance a=(1+rho)/2. Simultaneous null exceedances imply (X_1+X_2)/(2sqrt(a))>=u/sqrt(a). The scalar marked bound gives kernel at most C phi(u/sqrt(a)). Multiplying by det(Gamma)=1-rho^2=4a(1-a) gives
$$
C\phi(u)\,4a(1-a)
\exp\left[-\frac{u^2(1-a)}{2a}\right].
$$
Put x=(1-a)/a>=0. The factor is 4x/(1+x)^2 exp(-u^2 x/2)<=4x exp(-u^2 x/2)<=8/(e u^2). This proves (DP2), including negative rho. If rho=-1 the simultaneous positive-threshold event is empty; if rho=1 the determinant is zero. Thus singular endpoints are already covered.

The DPP pair inclusion factor is exactly det(Gamma)/p^2 for the root's uniform physical weights c_i=1/p. Consequently (DP2) is a concrete first nontrivial cofactor budget under that global cover, not an independently chosen covariance maximization.

## Higher-order determinant bound

For k>=2 nulls, let Gamma be their k-by-k unit-diagonal PSD Gram matrix and let
$$
a=k^{-2}{\bf1}^{\mathsf T}\Gamma{\bf1}\in[0,1].
$$
The normalized average direction has variance a. In a basis whose first vector is k^{-1/2}1, the first Gram diagonal entry is k a and the contrast block has trace k(1-a). Schur complement and arithmetic-geometric mean give
$$
\det\Gamma\le k a
\left[\frac{k(1-a)}{k-1}\right]^{k-1}. \tag{DG}
$$
If a=0, positivity forces det Gamma=0; the simultaneous positive-threshold event is also empty.

Simultaneous null exceedances again imply a unit Gaussian average is at least u/sqrt(a). Therefore
$$
\det\Gamma\ \sup_y\phi(y)
E[|D|1_{\{\forall i:X_i+\theta_i>=u\}}M\mid Y=y]
$$
$$
\le C k\left[\frac{k}{k-1}\right]^{k-1}
a(1-a)^{k-1}\phi(u)
e^{-u^2(1-a)/(2a)}.
$$
Using k/(k-1) to the power k-1<=e, x=(1-a)/a, and maximizing x^{k-1}e^{-u^2 x/2}, this is at most
$$
C k e\,\left[\frac{2(k-1)}{e u^2}\right]^{k-1}\phi(u). \tag{DPk}
$$
There is also the coarse C phi(u) bound because det Gamma<=1 and the average threshold is at least u. These estimates keep k-dependent constants explicit; they do not claim a k-independent factorial series bound.

## Replication and independent-frame checks

Uniform physical replication cannot place two identical columns in one nonzero determinant basis. The determinant in (DP2) vanishes for that duplicate pair. For distinct original labels the number of cloned pairs grows by K^2, while c_i c_j decreases by K^2, so the DPP pair contribution is unchanged after folding clones. This differs from counting any two selected physical copies as two independent constraints.

For independent nulls Gamma=I, determinant equals one and the actual simultaneous tail probability is t^k; (DPk)'s bound can be much looser than this. The determinant bound is therefore a near-coherence control to combine with genuine independent/large-packing estimates, not a replacement for all moment information.

## Exact generating function available for further resummation

Given the actual selected null set S(G), let K_B(G)=|B intersect S(G)| for the root's global random basis. DPP inclusion identities give, conditionally on G,
$$
E_B[z^{K_B(G)}]
=\det\left(I+(z-1)A_{S(G)}\right),\qquad
A_S=\frac1p\sum_{i\in S}v_i v_i^{\mathsf T}.
$$
The coefficient of (z-1)^k is the sum of det Gamma_T/p^k over k-subsets T of S. This is an exact global determinant polynomial, with the same actual BH rank retained outside it.

(DP2) and (DPk) control individual marked coefficients after multiplying by their determinants. They do not by themselves justify summing over all subsets or all physical ranks: naive combinatorial sums can still grow with m/p or lose the actual null/alternative mass resource. The signed FDP jump, its explicit Mills drift, and the importance factor p/(R vee1) remain part of the next allocation. No global budget, uniform inverse conditional variance, or main-target plan is asserted by these first cofactor bounds.
