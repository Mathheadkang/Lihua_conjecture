Precise remaining bridge after approved global particle certificate. Its analytic identity is Pcal(theta,v,1)-Pcal(theta,v,0)=T(theta,v). Therefore at a true-zero contact, Psi(0,v)=partial_theta Pcal(0,v,0)+partial_theta T(0,v), not merely the derivative of the false-particle potential. The finite inequality for adding (h,v,0) bounds its increment by T(0,v)+eta*qU*h²; it does not itself bound the true normal derivative. The tag derivative is nonnegative, equal to E[1{R>a} chi(R)/R * k_(0,v)(Q_X(R))]. Even using the fixed-private C² bounds and Taylor expansion leaves inverse powers of s, a, sigma; controlling integral T(0,v)<=F^a does not justify differentiating that value budget. No counterexample to either theorem is asserted: this identifies why the new structural certificate still needs a uniform Gaussian/rank estimate. Any next main-bound proof must keep this tag-derivative term, the signed rank terms and all regularizer dependencies explicit.
