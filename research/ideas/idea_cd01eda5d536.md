From the 5a probe of plan_gaussian_bh_one_sided_free_center_pooled_shell_20261106 (heuristic, not proved). In H>=2 exchangeable one-factor blocks (n0 nulls loading r, anti-loaded mates), a tilt of block g at level v in state y needs (i) its deficit filler D(v) to exceed its own null count n0*PhiBar((v-ry)/s) when that is >=1, and D(v)>=1 (integers); (ii) every other block, in a typical state, counts at least D(v) copies of its mates at all levels below v (carrier lemma 5b), so (H-1)D(v)<k(v). Both branches give, on the tilt line v=y/r, y^2<=2log(1/(q pi0))+O(log U), i.e. y<=U+O(log U/U): no shell gain for any H and n0. A rigorous version for exchangeable blocks (with Chernoff for the self-count and the sparse-extreme event) looks within reach and would be a clean intermediate lemma; the open part is heterogeneous blocks, where 'the other blocks would tilt in the same background' is counterfactual.
