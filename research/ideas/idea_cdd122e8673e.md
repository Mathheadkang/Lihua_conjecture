Raised by the researcher while reviewing which teaching-window ideas had actually been tried. Their question was: “为什么不回被逐条尝试？都是没有被验证的idea为什么不可以全部尝试？” They are asking why the remaining unverified ideas cannot all receive a trial, rather than accepting unexplained omission.

The teaching chat checked the current implementation: an approach is saved as reusable draft context for planning, not automatically as a pending proof attempt. Search slots automatically consider related test_case ideas, subject to finite round width and count; agent-origin test cases can come after generic directions. Consequently delivery alone does not ensure coverage. This card conveys the researcher's request for coverage, rather than creating another mathematical route.

## Requested treatment of the existing ideas

At the next appropriate planning or reassessment point, give EVERY still-untried idea below a concrete initial test and record its result. They need not all become long proof programs. A test should resolve an actual claim, calculation, or obstruction and produce evidence, rather than just reading the title or saying it is lower priority.

The last audit found no executing-window adoption or verification record for these five (exclude the teaching chat's own audit context_request events):
- idea_3bc65c83bcb2: test the unweighted zero-null boundary payment on independent Gaussian nulls. Check the rank-one event, Chernoff bound and Mills weighting, with actual labels preserved.
- idea_37aee864797e: derive or reject the finite-positive-definite identity q partial_q F=sum_i,r m(u_r)mu_i,r, including every signed face and triggering coordinate; then test whether its algebra helps or fails to close the desired estimate.
- idea_b24de4397205: test the proposed deterministic-transport cone comparison and joint-BV bridge at fixed positive rank cutoff and common loading. Prove or refute a sharply scoped preliminary lemma before attempting the main bound.
- idea_18bd38404716: check whether opposite-label avalanche pairing genuinely produces a rectangular matrix problem or still has a three-index tensor obstruction. A derived obstruction is a useful completed first test.

The other recently handed-back IDs were idea_014983d6daea, idea_8a445ee0b200, idea_eaffb9d8734f and idea_155959ee6e26. Keep their recorded contributions, outcomes and still-open scope. Do not redo a succeeded or failed test under a new name.

## Evidence and disposition

For each idea, retain one explicit outcome with a link to its native search round, plan, derived result, or precise obstruction:
- tested and useful: what new step survived and the next proof obligation;
- tested and unsuccessful: the calculation, counterexample or irreparable gap;
- overlaps an existing completed test: link the actual equivalent test and explain equivalence;
- genuinely deferred: the specific prerequisite or resource limit, and the condition for revisiting.

“Not yet validated” is a reason to test, not a mathematical reason to discard. If a statement is false, record that result; if a tool does not apply, exhibit the failed hypothesis. Do not present an idea as independently verified merely because it was read, saved, marked resolved, or showed no numerical counterexample.

The teaching chat suggests doing the cheap boundary and q-identity diagnostics before the longer BV, tensor and overlap investigations. This is a proposed order; the researcher's substantive preference is that none of the untried ideas disappear without an explicit treatment.
