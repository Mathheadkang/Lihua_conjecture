The free-level entropy bound (J=1, beta=b_G, kappa'~kappa sqrt(C+1)) gives only the total q pi_G(O(1)+log(1/pi_G)), i.e. part (b) of the partial bound; it cannot isolate the off-strip events, so part (a) (O(q pi_G) with no log) is not implied. Conversely the partial bound has unit loadings, one cluster, b_G fixed and pi_G>=q^C, so it does not give the free-level multi-cluster form with the gamma term.
