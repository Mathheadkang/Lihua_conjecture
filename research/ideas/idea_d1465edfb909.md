A distinct possible continuation is suggested by the original arXiv record https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02513v1 (Xianyang Zhang, Exact Simes inequalities for two-sided Gaussian tests, submitted 2 Nov 2026), whose HTML Appendix A.4 and D.3 were inspected. The source's global-null/fixed-dimension or independent-outside hypotheses do NOT prove this project's arbitrary-mean unrestricted target, and its theorems have not been independently verified here. Use only the following methodological pattern as inspiration, rederiving every needed assertion: compare the FULL signed gain/loss of actual rejection shares under contraction of genuinely near-identical null cohorts at the scale (1-rho)U^2, retaining original physical masses and the same joint outside Gaussian vector. This differs from representing many weakly correlated nulls by finitely many inherited carriers, whose loss was already established.
Concrete first gate: for one chosen null cohort with original means in the shrinking boundary strip, replace its loadings by its normalized mean direction on the SAME original probability space, retain every outside null and alternative exactly, and write the full actual-FDP difference including rank dilution on gained/lost crossings. Do not assert monotonicity or condition away the outside vector. Derive a local joint tail/difference estimate retaining the marginal tail factor; the source's A.7 suggests exponential tilting of a bivariate (score,difference) Gaussian pair, but the actual marked means and exposures require their own proof. Test whether nearly singular outside gates can make the expected contraction error fail to vanish uniformly; if they do, exhibit that exact obstruction. If a contraction estimate survives, construct a deterministic critical-scale partition and pay all remaining spread packets by the approved fractional-frame kernel without a number-of-clusters/entropy term. Fixed-dimension compactness, independent block sharing, or a free threshold envelope is insufficient. No global rate or local contraction claim is asserted, and no new completion root is requested.
