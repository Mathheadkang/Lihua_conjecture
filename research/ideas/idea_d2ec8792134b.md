New planning calculation; needs a full proof/independent verification before use as an established lemma. Work first with finite positive real weights summing to1, with fixed labels and a generic Gaussian model; exact rational cloning represents ordinary BH, and rational approximation is permitted off finite threshold hyperplanes (each Gaussian coordinate still has variance1 even for singular joint covariance). Put π0=sum of true-null weights, f=weighted FDP, F=Ef, G=E[f²]. Multiply all true-null weights by1+a and normalize the total by1+aπ0. Inside an actual BH cell, ∂a f=f(1−f). If an actual maximal-rank transition has after value f_+ and before value f_−, its threshold velocity is (f_+−π0)/h(u_r), where u_r=S^{-1}(q W_r). With κ_face the nonnegative unmarked Gaussian face density in the scalar threshold displacement convention, the proposed exact derivative is

∂a F = F−G +(q/2)∂q G −π0 q∂q F +(1/2)J,
J = sum_faces κ_face (f_+−f_−)²/h(u_r) >=0.

The algebra retaining signs is Δf·f_+=[Δ(f²)+(Δf)²]/2. All actual jumps and inverse-weight denominators must be retained. Calibrations N1/all true null and a true null diluted by an always-rejecting alternative give the predicted identity. At an all-null model π0=1, G=F and J=qF_q, so this identity alone is tautological. It uses no Gaussian structure and cannot by itself improve a distribution-free logarithmic loss.

A second proposed calibration: adding an independent zero-mean null atom of weight ε in a new orthogonal latent dimension, with all old weights multiplied by1−ε, gives ∂ε F=q(1−F)−(1−q)q F_q. The initially no-rejection region contributes a singleton-null source q·P(R=0); dropping it breaks the formula. At an all-model extremum this gives only qF_q>=q(1−F)/(1−q).

Possible new gate under audit: a Gaussian lower bound on J relative to G (e.g. G−J<=Cq/(1+U)) may exploit actual rank activation rather than facewise positive costs. It is unproved and could fail in smooth-FDP population limits. A separate closure is also necessary: stationarity at F-maximizers does not authorize differentiating G along moving optimizers, so K=π0F−G/2 cannot silently be treated as an optimized envelope.
