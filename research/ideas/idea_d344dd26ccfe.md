Compression and the clone-block bound control V_- of the full Simes size F_q along the Schur path; the budget controls V_- of the probability of the sparse sub-event S_q only. The downward variation of a sub-event's probability is not controlled by that of the whole event, nor conversely, so a full-event bound V_- <= 1/4 does not give the sparse budget.
