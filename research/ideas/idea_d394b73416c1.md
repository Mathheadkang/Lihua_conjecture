A bounded diagnostic for the draft OU integrated signed budget bridge: no rank-one admissible Gaussian model can refute its stronger BH gate. This is an observation with a complete elementary derivation, not an approved lemma or a claim about the unrestricted main theorem.

Let 0<q<=S(1), u=S^{-1}(q)>=1 and write every rank-one score as T_i=theta_i+sigma_i G, sigma_i in {-1,1}, G standard normal. Preserve the original null set theta_i<=0 and the actual largest BH rank with all multiplicities and non-strict comparisons. If f(G)>0, some rejected true null has theta_i+sigma_i G>=S^{-1}(qR/N)>=u. Since theta_i<=0, this implies sigma_i G>=u, hence |G|>=u. Thus f=0 on (-u,u), and F=E f<=P(|G|>=u)=2q. No ordering assumption, PRDS assumption, or replacement of mixed-label FDP is used.

For any Gaussian OU semigroup P_t in the stated convention and any 1-Lipschitz scalar h with finite second moment, direct semigroup differentiation and Gaussian integration by parts give
$$
Cov(f,h)=integral_0^infty E[(P_t f)' h']dt,
$$
and consequently |Cov(f,h)|<=J(f). To justify this for bounded measurable f and h=|x|, first integrate from epsilon to M using smooth Lipschitz approximations to |x|; Mehler smoothing justifies integration by parts. The identity is E[P_epsilon f h]-E[P_M f h]. Let epsilon decrease to zero and M increase to infinity by L2 semigroup convergence, and use Fatou for the norm bound. Therefore, with a=sqrt(2/pi),
$$
J(f)>=Cov(f,|G|)=E[f|G|]-aF>=F(u-a).
$$
For F>0 the exact gate diagnostic satisfies
$$
F(F-q pi_0)_+/(q J(f)) <= F/[q(u-a)] <= 2/(u-a) <= 2/(1-sqrt(2/pi)) < 10.
$$
If F=0 the diagnostic is zero; if J=0 then the preceding inequality forces F=0. This proves a uniform rank-one gate, including singular rank-one models and arbitrary negative true-null means. Adding unused independent latent coordinates leaves the calculation unchanged. It supplies neither a proof nor a refutation for increasing rank.

The complete signed-mixture identity can also be verified without taking positive jumps. The actual scalar FDP has finitely many distinct jump points b_j because every BH comparison can change only at one of the finitely many score equalities theta_i+sigma_i G=S^{-1}(qk/N). Merge all simultaneous changes into Delta_j=f(b_j+)-f(b_j-). Then f(x)=c_-+sum_j Delta_j 1{x>b_j} away from the finite boundary set, and F=c_-+sum_j Delta_j S(b_j). Put rho=exp(-t), s=sqrt(1-rho^2). Direct Mehler differentiation gives
$$
(P_t f)'(x)=(rho/s) sum_j Delta_j phi((b_j-rho x)/s).
$$
The product-of-Gaussians identity yields
$$
phi(x)(P_t f)'(x)=rho sum_j Delta_j phi(b_j) varphi_s(x-rho b_j).
$$
Taking the absolute value only after this complete signed sum, integrating over x and using dt=-d rho/rho proves
$$
J(f)=integral_0^1 ||sum_j Delta_j phi(b_j) varphi_{sqrt(1-rho^2)}(dot-rho b_j)||_{L1(dx)}d rho.
$$
Finite total variation makes all terms integrable. This is full actual FDP and full signed mixture; the positive mixture and the net or positive conditional-p-path charges are different diagnostics and are not identified with J here. No finite grid or numerical truncation is used.

Remaining gate: for higher rank, support is a union of rare null halfspaces; the rank-one witness |G| has no dimension-free direct replacement because Gaussian width can grow with the number and geometry of directions. An expectation-level full-BH mechanism is still required. Independent block replication is not automatically a counterexample: pooled BH has a different rank, and a deterministic limiting threshold has marginal null mass at most pi_0 times that threshold. No claim of a rigorous general replication limit is made.
