Round 4 supplies a restricted-family proof step, not an approved analytic black box or a proof of the general signed bridge. For independent Gaussian coordinates G_a, form 2m all-null scores G_a-delta and -G_a-delta, with delta=0 or delta=1/m. Since q<=S(1)<1/2, at most one score in each pair can be rejected. The original largest-count BH crossing is exactly the BH crossing among m folded values U_a=min{1,2 S(|G_a|-delta)} at level q. Independence is preserved by folding. At delta=0 these are iid uniform, giving full FDR F=q; at delta=1/m they are independent superuniform, giving F<=q. Thus these growing-rank and growing-null-count families have zero positive excess and zero bridge ratio. Any use of the independent-uniform/superuniform BH calculation in a later proof must include its direct verification; it is not an approved library prerequisite here. The useful step is the exact largest-count fold, not a rule extending to interacting blocks, mixed labels, or generic mixed-sign covariance.
