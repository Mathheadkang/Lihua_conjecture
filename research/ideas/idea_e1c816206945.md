Plan-stage side calculation (11 Nov 2026), unverified by a check stage. Global null, T_i=w_i^T Z, psi(x)=E(G-x)_+=phi(x)-x Phibar(x), m(x)=psi/Phibar=h(x)-x (Gaussian mean residual life), X(x)=(1/N)sum_i (T_i-x)_+, R*=sup_{x>=0} X(x)/psi(x). (1) Reduction. If N_{u_k}>=k with Phibar(u_k)=qk/N then X(x)>=(k/N)(u_k-x) for x<=u_k; taking x=max(Phibar^{-1}(k/N),0) and using log-concavity of Phibar gives R*>=c sqrt(log(1/q)) on E_q (and R*>=c log(1/q) for sparse ranks), so P(E_q)<=P(R*>=c sqrt(log(1/q))); by idea_8699fa13f1f6 any dimension-free bound P(R*>=a)->0 as a->infinity would prove the target. (2) Unlike the exponential kernel of idea_c36aadbe5f06 (Limit 1, Mills loss: soft Kullback version false for independent coordinates), the hinge kernel has no Mills loss: under independence the extreme-scale ratio is about s e^{-s}<=1/e. (3) Gibbs form with hard entropy: by LP duality X(x)>=a psi(x) iff some subset K has mean_K T>=x+a psi(x)N/|K|, so {R*>=a} is the union over K of half-spaces {mean_K T>=tau_a(|K|/N)}, tau_a(kappa)=h(Phibar^{-1}(kappa/a)) for kappa/a<=1/2 (the Gaussian mean of the top kappa/a fraction). The top-kappa average A_kappa=min_x[x+X(x)/kappa] satisfies E A_kappa<=h(Phibar^{-1}(kappa)) for EVERY correlation matrix (linearity only), so each threshold exceeds the mean by about log(a)/x; but A_kappa is only 1-Lipschitz, so Borell-TIS is useless at that gap. (4) Second moment: Cov((G1-x)_+,(G2-x)_+) is increasing in rho by Plackett's identity and vanishes at rho=0, so negative correlations LOWER Var X(x); positive ones raise it (full correlation gives Var/psi^2 about 2/Phibar(x), too big for Chebyshev at small kappa, where the truth is about kappa/a from Markov-type heavy tails). (5) Missing step: a scale-summable bound sum_j P(X(x_j)>=a psi(x_j))<=C/a over dyadic scales Phibar(x_j)=2^{-j}/a. It holds in the two extremes (independent: about 1/a from O(1) top scales; fully correlated: about sum 2^{-j}/a) and for independent-block mixtures (family masses add), but fails for arbitrary non-Gaussian dependence (BY construction gives 1/a per scale), so it must use Gaussian structure; no available tool supplies it.
