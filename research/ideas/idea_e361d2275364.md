Draft exact labeled-entropy refinement proposed by the root PLAN driver and rederived here as a mathematical partial, not a target proof or native verification verdict. It is related to idea_96ead5c9a062, whose selected-index entropy identity uses only the constant lower score U, and to idea_a7a7c3543b07 and idea_a86564a9c1fa, whose dynamic information estimates concern a signed Mills trace. The new calculation instead keeps the actual random cutoff u_R through the entropy calculation, cancels its square against the rank logarithm, and displays the remaining score-scale loss explicitly.

SCOPE AND EXACT TILTS. Fix the original finite one-sided Gaussian BH model at q<=barPhi(1), with fixed original true-null set I0, original largest nonstrict rank R and u_r=barPhi^{-1}(qr/N). Let alpha_i=1{i rejected}/(R vee1), m_i=E alpha_i and F=sum_{i in I0}m_i>0. Ignore zero-mass i. Set w_i=m_i/F, and let Q_i have density alpha_i/m_i relative to the original latent standard Gaussian gamma. Then Q=sum_i w_i Q_i has density FDP/F. Every conditional law is an actual BH-truncated law, not a Gaussian mixture component. The finite-model densities are bounded by 1/m_i, so their entropies and all score/rank moments used below are finite.

ENTROPY IDENTITY AND GAUSSIAN SCORE BOUND. On the support of Q_i, alpha_i=1/R. Hence
D(Q_i||gamma)=-E_{Q_i}log R-log m_i.
For X_i=v_i.G, the elementary entropy variational bound follows from E_gamma exp(lambda X_i)=exp(lambda^2/2): D(Q_i||gamma)>=lambda E_{Q_i}X_i-lambda^2/2 for every real lambda, hence D>= (E_{Q_i}X_i)^2/2. Rejection gives X_i>=u_R-theta_i>=u_R>=1, so
D(Q_i||gamma)>=(E_{Q_i}u_R)^2/2.
Extend w by zeros on alternatives and write D(w||Unif_N)=sum_i w_i log(Nw_i). Weighting the exact entropy identity gives
sum_i w_i D(Q_i||gamma)=E_Q log(N/R)-log F-D(w||Unif_N).
No selected-index entropy is dropped.

EXACT RANK-SQUARE CANCELLATION. For u>=1, integration by parts gives barPhi(u)>=u phi(u)/(u^2+1)>=phi(u)/(u+1). Since barPhi(u_R)=qR/N,
log(N/R)<=log q+u_R^2/2+log(u_R+1)+(1/2)log(2*pi).
Combining the preceding relations and E_Q u_R^2=sum_i w_i E_{Q_i}u_R^2 proves the concrete finite-model inequality
log(F/q) <= (1/2)log(2*pi)+E_Q log(u_R+1)+(1/2)sum_i w_i Var_{Q_i}(u_R)-D(w||Unif_N).
This keeps original R, its squared score, selected-label entropy and all original null means; negative null means only strengthen the intermediate score bound. It is an actual derived inequality, not an assumed q-scale gate.

DECISIVE PROOF-LOSS TEST. For independent all-zero nulls, exact BH calibration gives F=q, exchangeability gives m_i=q/N and w_i=1/N, and D(w||Unif_N)=0. For every i, Q_i(R=r)=P(R=r)/q, the same rank law as Q, because E[1{i selected}|R=r]=r/N. The exponential largest-rank tightness established inline in idea_2f80c1bddfbb therefore applies to all these conditional laws. At fixed r, u_r~sqrt(2log N). Uniform exponential rank tails imply
E_Q log(u_R+1)=(1/2)log(2log N)+o(1),
while Var_{Q_i}(u_R)->0 uniformly in i. For the latter, use 0<=u_1-u_R<=log(R)/U from the hazard lower bound h>=U, the uniform exponential tails of R under Q_i, and u_1-u_r->0 for fixed r; dominated splitting over fixed R then proves convergence in L2. The logarithmic expectation follows by the same fixed-r/tail splitting, or by first restricting R<=N^epsilon, using its exponentially small complement, then taking epsilon down to zero.

Thus the displayed entropy bound has a right side growing as (1/2)loglog N even though its left side is exactly zero and the desired fixed-q main bound is dimension-free. This is a loss of the score-variational/Mills certificate, not a counterexample to either the entropy inequality, the OU excess bridge or the main theorem. It demonstrates that keeping the exact rank entropy cancels the leading log(N/R) term but does not by itself pay the residual hazard scale. A new geometric or signed correlation estimate controlling this residual jointly with genuine FDR excess would be needed. Neither the static entropy identity nor the old dynamic label information bounds supply it.
