Scoped checked obstruction in round2 slot1 for gaussian_bh_one_sided_universal_order_upper, not a proof for unrestricted covariance. The candidate cand_gaussian_bh_one_sided_universal_order_upper_r2_s1_signed_edge_rank_budget uses q_j=exp(-j^2), d_j=ceil(exp(j^2)) independent standard Gaussian null directions, L_j=ceil(exp(3j^2)) exact null clones per direction, and M_j=d_j(d_j-1)/2 alternatives T_ab=U_j-(G_a+G_b)/sqrt(2), U_j=S^{-1}(q_j)>0. This gives growing null span and signed edge-adaptive alternatives, outside the preceding scalar common-factor and rank-two families. Its alternative fraction is asymptotic to exp(-2j^2)/2. Its stronger restrictive feature M_j<L_j blocks a counterexample.

General checked calculation: let d independent standard Gaussian coordinates each have L exact zero-mean null clones. Include M<L arbitrary alternative coordinates; they may depend on all null coordinates and may have unrestricted mutual dependence. Apply original largest-rank ordinary BH to all N=dL+M coordinates. If K=k>=1 null blocks are rejected, then R<=kL+M<(k+1)L, and the kth ordered null-block p-value P_(k) satisfies P_(k)<=qR/N<=q(k+1)/d. The original null block p-values are iid Unif(0,1). Thus FDR<=Pr(V>0)<=sum_{k=1}^d Pr(P_(k)<=q(k+1)/d)<=sum_{k=1}^d ((k+1)^k/k!)q^k, using the union over all k-subsets and their independent uniform probabilities. The coefficients a_k=(k+1)^k/k! obey a_1=2 and a_(k+1)/a_k=(1+1/(k+1))^(k+1)<e. Consequently FDR<=2q/(1-eq)<=3q for q<=1/16. Every step uses the same original BH rejection count, including all alternative and null multiplicities.

For the explicit edge family, PSD unit diagonal follows from the unit loading vectors e_a and -(e_a+e_b)/sqrt(2). Null labels are zero means, alternative labels are positive means U_j. The construction is finite at each j and needs no population approximation. It passes model hypotheses but fails NOT, since FDR_j/[q_j sqrt(log(1/q_j))]<=3/j.

Limits: the argument requires independent equally sized exact null blocks and M<L. A small alternative proportion by itself does not imply M<L, and arbitrary nonuniform null weights are not covered. It does not resolve growing-rank signed covariance without this rank-budget restriction, establish a general rare-alternative preservation bridge, or pay an unrestricted OU flux.
