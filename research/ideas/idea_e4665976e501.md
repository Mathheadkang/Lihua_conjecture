Checked elementary marginal lemma (independent worker derivation and root rederivation; not a native-approved asset). Write Q=Gaussian upper tail, u=Q^{-1}(q)>0. In the ORIGINAL ordinary BH rejection set, let D_J=E[#{rejected i in J}/(R∨1)] for any J with θ_i≤−a<0 and Gaussian N(θ_i,1) marginals; all other marginals and the joint dependence may be arbitrary. Then D_J≤q(|J|/N)e^{−au−a²/2}{(u+u^{-1})/a+a^{-2}}. Indeed rejected i implies p_i≤q and 1/R≤q/(Np_i), hence D_J≤(q/N)Σ_i∫_u^∞φ(x+a)/Q(x)dx. For x>0, Q(x)≥φ(x)x/(1+x²) by integrating φ(x)/x and bounding the residual integral by Q(x)/x². Therefore the integral is at most e^{−a²/2}∫_u^∞e^{−ax}(x+1/x)dx, which gives the displayed expression. Actual θ_i≤−a are dominated pointwise on x≥u. For u>1, a=(log u)/u gives D_J≤q(|J|/N)e^{−a²/2}{(u+u^{-1})/log u+u/(log u)²}=o(qu), uniformly in N, all correlations and all other means. Thus leading-order one-sided difficulty localizes to null means in (−(log u)/u,0]. This does NOT allow deleting/moving the far-negative coordinates: they continue to affect R, and designated near-boundary numerator bounds must allow arbitrary real nuisance means outside J.
