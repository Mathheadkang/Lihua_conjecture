Round 1 produced no candidates. A precise remaining proof step is to replace the arbitrary-dependence harmonic loss by a Gaussian-specific bound uniform over all finite dimensions, means and correlation matrices, including singular matrices. For every admissible model, super-uniform true-null p-values give FDR <= q (|I0|/N) H_N <= q(1+log N). Hence along any one sequence q_m decreasing to zero with log N_m/sqrt(log(1/q_m)) eventually bounded by a finite B, the limsup of FDR/[q_m sqrt(log(1/q_m))] is at most B. Such a sequence cannot have an unbounded normalized ratio. This does not give one universal constant over sequences with arbitrarily varying finite growth coefficients B. If the normalized ratio tends to infinity, log N_m/sqrt(log(1/q_m)) must tend to infinity as a necessary condition; no such admissible counterexample sequence was constructed. A complete approach must control the unrestricted supremum anew at every q, including arbitrary q-dependent N, theta, covariance, null proportion, rank, factor dimension and direction count, with one universal finite constant and a universal small-q interval. The harmonic gate neither supplies that bound nor proves any family is sufficient for a counterexample. This records an unclosed proof obligation, not a plan with an assumed budget or a proof of the target.
