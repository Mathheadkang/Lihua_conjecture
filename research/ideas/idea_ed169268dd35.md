Primary reference rechecked live: Monitirtha Dey, On limiting behaviors of stepwise multiple testing procedures, Statistical Papers65(2024),5691-5717, DOI10.1007/s00362-024-01613-6, official open text https://doi.org/10.1007/s00362-024-01613-6 , section4.2 Theorem4.3. It gives the fixed-rho>0, N->infinity all-null equicorrelated Gaussian BH limit as S(b_rho), where b_rho=inf_{0<r<1}[S^{-1}(qr)-sqrt(1-rho)S^{-1}(r)]/sqrt(rho); at rho=0 or1 finite/all-limit FDR equals q. This result has NOT been newly imported as an approved source node.

Candidate additional limit, not yet formally proved: setting x=S^{-1}(r), z=sqrt(rho)x and L=log(1/q), Mills expansion yields barrier approx z/2+L/z, suggesting b_rho->sqrt(2L) as rho->0. A short stable-log-tail diagnostic at q=.05 and rho=.1,.01,.001,.0001,.00001 supports limiting FDR S(sqrt(2log20)) about .0072, whereas independent finite BH FDR is exactly .05. Prove the global infimum asymptotic, including escape regimes, before using it as an established obstruction.

If verified, this shows a scalar-FDR discontinuity at sigma0 for the marked-array finite-size limit: arrays with off-diagonal rho converge weakly to the identity array, yet taking N->infinity first and then rho->0 differs from rho=0 first. It would also obstruct a uniformly vanishing ABSOLUTE random-submodel FDR error over all Gaussian models at sigma0: choose rho_m so the fixed m submodel is close to independent, then N_m huge so the full equicorrelated model is near its different population limit. This is outside the active theorem's FIXED POSITIVE sigma hypothesis, not its counterexample and not a counterexample to either main bound. It explains why the sigma->0 step must use finite-model/supremum VALUE comparisons, not pointwise continuity of the infinite-array functional or an unproved uniform smoothing rate.
