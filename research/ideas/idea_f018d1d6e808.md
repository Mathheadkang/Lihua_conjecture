## Constructive step and provenance

This extends the root-derived full-frame surface calculation in idea_ac8371a287d5 to a centered Gaussian velocity mark. The derivation below is new within the same native delegation and has not undergone native independent verification. It keeps a specific deterministic Mills drift instead of dropping it or charging the uncentered velocity by Cauchy-Schwarz. It supplies an explicit full-covariance marked-surface kernel, not the final BH aggregation.

## Setup and precise assertion

Let G be standard Gaussian in finite dimension. Let v_i,w,zeta be unit vectors, w orthogonal to zeta, and c_i>=0 satisfy sum_i c_i v_i v_i^T<=I. Put
$$
X_i=v_i\cdot G,\quad Y=w\cdot G,\quad D=\zeta\cdot G,\quad
\beta=\sum_i c_i,\quad
L=\sum_i c_i(v_i\cdot\zeta).
$$
Thus D is standard normal and independent of Y, although it may depend on every null score and on arbitrary additional marks. Fix u>=2, t=barPhi(u), h(u)=phi(u)/t, and A={X_i>=u for every positive-weight original i}. For every y,
$$
\phi(y)E\left[
|D-h(u)L|\,1_A\mid Y=y
\right]
\le 2e\,h(u+1)\,t^\beta. \tag{VM}
$$
The constant 2e is a convenient coarse universal choice. Since h(u+1)<=u+2, this is a dimension-free O((u+1)t^beta) centered velocity surface bound.

Adding any further event mark M with 0<=M<=1 inside the nonnegative expectation preserves the estimate. The mark need not be independent of position, velocity, or the nulls. Replacing the event by {X_i+theta_i>=u for all i}, theta_i<=0, also preserves it by inclusion in A. Thus arbitrary negative true-null means are covered without individual-tail factors.

## Unequal-threshold version needed before tilting

Repeat the completed-frame/hyperplane proof of idea_ac8371a287d5 with scalar original densities
$$
f_i(a)=\phi(a)1_{\{a>=a_i\}}/\barPhi(a_i),
\qquad a_i>=1.
$$
Their maxima are h(a_i). With b_i=v_i dot w, the exact same calculation gives
$$
\phi(y)P(X_i>=a_i\ \forall i\mid Y=y)
\le\sqrt e\,
\left[\prod_{\mathrm{original}\ i}h(a_i)^{c_i b_i^2}\right]
\left[\prod_{\mathrm{original}\ i}\barPhi(a_i)^{c_i}\right].
\tag{UT}
$$
The added slack-frame Gaussian maxima contribute phi(0) to their powers and may be discarded because phi(0)<1. The total exponent of the displayed h factors is at most one. In particular the prefactor is at most max_i h(a_i) when a_i>=1. Unlike the refuted stronger common-prefactor individual-tail proposal, (UT) retains the actual individual hazard factors.

## Exact Gaussian tilt

Because D and Y are independent, exponential Gaussian tilting in direction zeta gives
$$
\phi(y)E[e^{\lambda D}1_A\mid Y=y]
=e^{\lambda^2/2}\phi(y)
P(X_i>=u-\lambda z_i\ \forall i\mid Y=y),
\qquad z_i=v_i\cdot\zeta.
$$
For |lambda|<=1, every shifted threshold is at least u-1>=1. Apply (UT), bound all hazards by h(u+|lambda|), and use concavity of log barPhi:
$$
\log\barPhi(u-\lambda z_i)
\le\log t+\lambda z_i h(u).
$$
After summing with c_i,
$$
\prod_i\barPhi(u-\lambda z_i)^{c_i}
\le t^\beta e^{\lambda h(u)L}.
$$
Consequently the centered marked moment generating function satisfies
$$
\phi(y)E[e^{\lambda(D-h(u)L)}1_A\mid Y=y]
\le\sqrt e\,h(u+|\lambda|)\,t^\beta e^{\lambda^2/2},
\qquad |\lambda|<=1. \tag{CMGF}
$$
At lambda=1 and -1 the coefficient is e h(u+1). The elementary inequality |a|<=e^a+e^{-a} proves (VM). This argument never conditions on an inverse covariance or divides by a small conditional variance.

## General Gaussian speed and the retained drift

For a scalar Gaussian velocity \dot Y with variance sigma^2 and Cov(Y,\dot Y)=0, write \dot Y=sigma D. Its centered kernel obeys
$$
\phi(y)E\left[
|\dot Y-\sigma h(u)L|1_A M\mid Y=y
\right]
\le 2e\,\sigma h(u+1)t^\beta.
$$
The drift sigma h(u)L is explicit and generally nonzero. In an actual transport it must be collated with all signed whole-share increments before any absolute-value payment. It is not permissible to omit it, assume that it cancels, or silently identify it with an already paid global first-chaos term.

## Concrete next BH use and limitation

At an alternative's physical-rank face, take Y to be its standardized position, dotY its variance-preserving path velocity, y=u(qr/N)-mu_j, and u=u(qr/N). Fractional-frame event A is a selected set of null exceedances. The actual largest-rank compatibility indicator can be included as M, and the estimate is uniform in the alternative mean mu_j because y is arbitrary. The lemma supplies an actual velocity-mark bound, rather than only a probability or unmarked surface density.

What it does not supply is a fractional-cover decomposition of the signed whole-null-share jump
$$
K_j=V(t_{r_j^+})/r_j^+
-V(t_{r_j^-})/(r_j^-\vee1),
$$
with a summable total payment across all physical ranks, all faces and all model coordinates. The explicit drift must be included in that decomposition. No such global allocation is assumed here, and no pending main-target plan or completion claim follows solely from (VM). The main theorem retains every original mean, singular-covariance and q-dependent-model quantifier.
