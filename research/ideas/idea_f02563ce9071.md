Strategic direction from the 8 November 2026 brainstorm (ranked 4 of 8). This is the requested "extremization reduction" direction. UNPROVED; the conjecture below may be false and is written so that its falsification is informative.

1. Core idea and origin. Represent any admissible model as $T_j=\theta_j+v_j\cdot G$ with unit vectors $v_j\in\mathbb R^d$, $G\sim N(0,I_d)$ (singular $\Sigma$ included). Exact identity: $\mathrm{FDR}/q=\mathbb E[M(L)/\overline\Phi(L)]$ with $M(w)=N^{-1}\#\{i\in I_0:T_i\ge w\}$ and $L=u_R$ the BH level (a measurable function of $G$). Calibration: for a FIXED level $w$, $\mathbb E\mathbf 1\{T_i\ge w\}\le\overline\Phi(w)$ for every null, so all inflation is the dependence of $L$ on $G$. The sub-level sets $B_w=\{g:L(g)\le w\}$ form a nested increasing family. The contribution of null $i$ is governed by how the boundary layers $B_{w+dw}\setminus B_w$ concentrate in the half-space direction $v_i$: for a Borel set $B$ of given Gaussian measure, the Neyman-Pearson lemma says $\mathbb E[\mathbf 1_B(G)\mathbf 1\{v_i\cdot G\ge w\}]$ is maximized by the half-space $\{v_i\cdot g\ge c\}$ with the same measure. A nested family cannot consist of half-spaces in two different directions (two half-spaces with different normals are never nested). Quantifying this rigidity is the extremization: Conjecture (EXT): $\sup\mathbb E[M(L)/\overline\Phi(L)]$ over all finite models is within a universal constant factor of its one-common-factor value, which Lei's thm:one-upper and cor:one-sharp pin at $\tfrac12+U/\sqrt{2\pi}+O(1)$ for $\pi_0=1$; the order target would then follow from Lei's theorem. Discipline: Gaussian isoperimetry and noise stability (Borell: half-spaces are the most noise-stable sets; Mossel-Neeman: near-optimal sets are close to half-spaces), Ehrhard symmetrization (symmetrizing every $B_w$ in a direction $v^*$ preserves measures and nestedness, increases the tilt of nulls aligned with $v^*$ and decreases the others; the trade-off functional is what must be controlled).

2. Literature (verified links, read at abstract/snippet level): Mossel, Neeman, Robust optimality of Gaussian noise stability, JEMS 2015, https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.4126 ; van Handel, The Borell-Ehrhard game, https://web.math.princeton.edu/~rvan/begame160731.pdf ; Shenfeld, van Handel, The equality cases of the Ehrhard-Borell inequality, https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.00061 ; Latala, Matlak, Royen's proof of the Gaussian correlation inequality, https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.08776 (recorded to prevent repetition: GCI lower-bounds intersections of symmetric convex sets, while our sets are neither symmetric nor convex and we need upper bounds, so GCI is not directly usable). Extreme points of the elliptope (Grone-Pierce-Watkins 1990, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/002437959090006X ; Li-Tam, https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/S0895479892240683) have rank $k$ with $k(k+1)\le 2N$: since FDR is not convex in $\Sigma$ this does not reduce to low rank by itself, but it is the right vocabulary if a convexity-in-$\Sigma$ statement is ever available.

3. First testable proposition (NEST-2). Two null clusters along unit vectors $v_1,v_2$ at angle $\alpha$, masses $\pi_1,\pi_2$, arbitrary alternatives. Prove $\sup_L\sum_g\pi_g\mathbb E[\mathbf 1\{v_g\cdot G\ge L\}/\overline\Phi(L)]\le C(1+U)$ uniformly in $\alpha$, where the supremum runs over levels $L=L(G)\ge U$ that are REALIZABLE BH levels of some finite Gaussian model containing the two clusters, and identify the property of realizable $L$ that is used. Candidate property: $B_w$ is a union over $w'\le w$ of threshold-of-half-spaces sets $\{g:\#\{j:v_j\cdot g\ge w'-\theta_j\}\ge k(w')\}$. Known fact to keep in view: over ALL measurable $L$ the supremum is unbounded in $N$ (tracking the leader of $m$ independent directions gives order $\sqrt{\log N}$), so the realizability constraint is essential, and the test is whether threshold-of-half-spaces families can emulate leader tracking.

4. Why it bypasses the obstacles. Obstacle 1: the extremal problem is posed directly for the functional; the Gaussian input is Neyman-Pearson and isoperimetry, not an integration by parts. Obstacle 2a: crossings are irrelevant. Obstacle 2b: coherence is replaced by the geometry of nested level sets; the antipodal counterfamily has two opposite half-space families and nestedness forbids both from being tracked, consistent with its $O(qU)$ FDR. Obstacle 2c: no cells. Obstacle 2d: Gaussian isoperimetric statements are dimension-free.

5. Failure criterion. If threshold-of-half-spaces nested families can approximate half-spaces in many directions on disjoint level ranges with total tilt $\gg U$ (explicit construction), (EXT) is false as stated and this becomes a counterexample lead for the hierarchical probe idea. Also stop if (NEST-2) needs a property of realizable $L$ that cannot be stated without the full BH combinatorics.

6. Payoff: full (one-direction extremality up to constants, then Lei's theorem) / partial (two-direction theorem, new tools) / counterexample lead.
