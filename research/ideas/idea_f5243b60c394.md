Optional proof route/strengthening to check, not an added statement or an established estimate. With fixed q,sigma>0 and W1 coupling cost delta, integrating the common normal Z bounds fixed-rank selection-indicator disagreement by C_sigma delta. On r in[a,1], a finite rank grid plus monotonicity and the unconditional unit-variance density bound should give E sup_r|H_pi-H_n| and the analogous V discrepancy <=C(q,a,sigma)(K delta+1/K), uniformly in the parameter laws; the same estimate holds at Z shifted by any fixed eta because normal density maxima and unit-variance marginal density maxima are shift invariant. On the event discrepancies<=kappa, common-score shift sigma eta pays the threshold difference between r and r-2kappa once 2kappa sup_{r>=a/2}|u'(r)|<=sigma eta. Thus actual largest ranks satisfy a sandwich by R_pi(Z-eta)-2kappa and R_pi(Z+eta)+2kappa away from ranks below a. Since R_pi(z) is monotone in z and bounded by1, its expected increment over a 2eta shift is <=2eta/sqrt(2pi), uniformly in pi. Markov bounds for the grid discrepancies then give uniform L1 rank stability after first fixing a and then taking delta down to zero. For numerator cutoff stability, for each fixed parameter/residual realization g(z)=score(z)-u(q R_pi(z)) is increasing with g(z2)-g(z1)>=sigma(z2-z1) wherever R>0. Hence the set |g(z)|<=h has length<=2h/sigma, so E pi{|score-u(qR)|<=h,R>0}<=2h/(sigma sqrt(2pi)), even though cutoff and score are dependent. Combine this with rank difference control and quantile Lipschitzness on [a,1] to control numerator differences at positive macroscopic ranks. Split the comparison into both ranks<=2a, both>=a, and a crossing mismatch (which forces rank difference>=a). Uniform microscopic-FDP control from conditional normal marginals pays the first part, and L1 rank stability pays the mismatch. This may prove uniform continuity of full FDR over the tagged P1 domain at fixed q,sigma, stronger than the current sequential-continuity goal; do not assert it without checking every sandwich and splitting bound. It still has no modulus uniform as sigma down to zero, no finite attained optimizer, and no extension of the rare-clone first variation to non-atomic near-maximizers.
