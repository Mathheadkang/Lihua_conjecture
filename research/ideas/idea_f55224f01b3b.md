For the proposed finite positive-definite correlated-clone first-variation identity (idea_dee8f2040d8f), a possible elementary proof refinement is to condition on all unchanged original statistics T_{-i}, plus unused Gaussian coordinates if latent dimension exceeds N. Positive definiteness leaves a strictly positive Gaussian conditional variance for T_i. At fixed such background, the original FDP as a function of T_i is a bounded finite step function with possible jumps only at the deterministic original BH levels u_r. The minimum of the normalized perturbed clone values is, for sufficiently small positive epsilon at this fixed model and perturbation, a strictly increasing piecewise-affine function of T_i; its inverse crossings of u_r have displacement -epsilon min_b(w_b dot Y_face+c_b)+O(epsilon^2(1+norm(background))). Integrate the one-dimensional Gaussian mass of these displaced threshold intervals, then use a Gaussian-integrable linear-background envelope. This avoids treating intersections of other moving faces as if they had zero first-order contribution without proof: the other statistics are held fixed throughout the conditional step. A change of Gaussian coordinates must recover precisely phi(u_r-theta_i)gamma_perp(dY) on the face, with no lost Jacobian. Separately prove uniform-K exact-copy invariance and show a largest expanded BH rank partial in the perturbed group forces clone spread to exceed the fixed positive gap between a partial rank threshold and the next completed-group threshold, yielding exp(-a/epsilon^2) probability. This is a proposed proof mechanism, not a proved node or a dimension-uniform estimate.
