## Proposed proof step

Control the actual signed or weighted BH contribution before relaxing it to a contraction modulus, positive flux, or insertion increment. Any such relaxation needs a proved comparison that retains the actual activation conditions and exact rank/FDP weights, uniformly over the target's full model class.

## Evidence and obstruction

The supplied round-4 search report gives zero candidates for the three assigned ideas `idea_47b8626c3e53`, `idea_47c7d6819637`, and `idea_52a020fe86cd`. The reported two-test model has actual FDR below q despite failed contraction monotonicity. The specific moving-q fold model has FDR at most q/2 by its PRDS structure despite uncontrolled positive auxiliary flux. The tested insertion construction retains an exact actual-FDR coefficient bounded by 1/2 despite divergence of its auxiliary insertion quantity. These facts concern the reported models only; the summary has not independently reconstructed their calculations.

## Remaining obligation

Identify a comparison or cancellation that controls actual BH payments for arbitrary signed Gaussian covariance, means, singularity, dimension and rank. The fold model's PRDS property cannot be asserted for arbitrary signed Gaussian models. Auxiliary divergence alone does not refute the universal-order target, and bounded payments in these examples do not prove it.
