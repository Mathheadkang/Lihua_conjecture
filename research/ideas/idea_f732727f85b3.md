The full FDP tilt can have disconnected support, so global Poincare assumptions cannot be silently imposed. However, the finite hyperplane arrangement theta_i+v_i dot g=u_r, over all i and ranks r, partitions latent Gaussian space (up to zero-measure hyperplanes) into convex polyhedral cells C. Actual largest BH rank, selected set, f_C=V_C/R_C and b_C=B_C/f_C are constant on each positive-f cell. The terminal Q is exactly the mixture with weights alpha_C=f_C gamma(C)/F and component gamma(.|C). This retains every actual rank constraint; it is not the free empirical threshold envelope.

For a heat state y and s=1-t, cell posterior probabilities are pi_C=f_C P_s 1_C(y)/P_s f(y), component drifts u_C=grad log P_s 1_C(y), total drift v=sum pi_C u_C, and a=sum pi_C b_C. Direct differentiation gives K=sum pi_C (b_C-a) tensor (u_C-v). Thus the signed trace comes entirely from mixing constant cell fields. Convexity should give the dimension-free component covariance bound Cov(N(y,sI)|C)<=sI, hence -I/s<=Hess log P_s1_C<=0; any use must justify unbounded cells and smooth convex-barrier limits. There is no corresponding automatic global bound for the mixture.
