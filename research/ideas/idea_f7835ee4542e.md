Round 1 supplies a proof step, not an unrestricted proof: for any finite Gaussian correlation model and deterministic probability weights, let M_i=exp((Z_i^+)^2/2). Pointwise Lambda <= sum_i omega_i M_i. Marginal Gaussian integration gives F(t)=E min(t,M_i)=1/2+sqrt(log(t)/pi)+t barPhi(sqrt(2 log(t))) <= 1+sqrt(log(t)/pi), for t>=1. Subadditivity of truncation and Jensen then give E min(c,Lambda) <= 1+sum_{omega_i>0} omega_i sqrt(log(c/omega_i)/pi) <= 1+sqrt((log(c)+H(omega))/pi), with H(omega)=sum_{omega_i>0} omega_i log(1/omega_i). Thus bounded entropy is sufficient for a sharp truncated-moment bound with an entropy-dependent additive constant. In the two-coordinate family H<=log 2. Rank-one sign mixtures also reduce to two half-line weights p and 1-p, with a sharper binary calculation. These exclude their scoped families as counterexamples. A possible proof route is to retain the common maximizing w when replacing the coordinatewise envelope, so as to remove this entropy cost; the existing envelope alone does not accomplish that. The independent maximal-tail assertion still needs its own argument: a coordinate union bound costs support size.
