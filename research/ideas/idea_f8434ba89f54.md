The complete analytic argument is in [the folded-tail null-noise plan](../plans/plan_two_sided_null_noise_folded_completion_20261104.md), section Failure, frozen SHA256 4b56edc7597b9f85c35b26aa9504a70540c6e0135a5fd8a85d742c8af78d0392. This is a DRAFT method-obstruction record, not a new approved theorem or a refutation of the unrestricted target.

Fix a sufficiently small testing level q, let alpha=arcosh(2/q-1), N=2n, z_N=sqrt(0.02 log N), and rho_N=lambda/z_N^2, with a sufficiently small positive lambda fixed after q. Use an exactly jointly Gaussian equicorrelation model: n centered true nulls, n positive alternative means alpha/z_N, all offdiagonal correlations rho_N. The independent-null reference has exactly FDR=q/2 even though its alternatives remain correlated.

For the actual plan's weighted null-noise path, the offdiagonal integrated Hessian matrix K has weights 2r on null/null pairs, 1 on null/alternative pairs and 0 on alternative/alternative pairs, with the factor one half retained. Its exact identity is FDR(Sigma_N)-q/2=tr(Sigma_N K_N). Since K_AA=0 and diag K=0, equicorrelation gives tr(Sigma_N K_N)=rho_N tr(11^T K_N).

The conditional two-sided tail ratio uses a=sqrt(lambda)W and
$$
h(s,a,lambda)=e^{-lambda s^2/2}[\cosh(as)+\cosh((alpha+a)s)]/2.
$$
The first crossing near s=1 and its conditional FDP yield the localized averaged expansion with positive second-order coefficient
$$
\frac q2\lambda^2\left\{\frac3{alpha^2}-\frac1{alpha\sinh(alpha)}\right\}.
$$
The written finite-array transfer controls every larger BH rank, uses a uniform Gaussian-hazard estimate down to the fixed-q endpoint, and handles the omitted Gaussian event by a truncation sandwich. It gives a strictly positive fixed-q FDR gain for large N.

The fully diagonal primal min sum d_i subject to Diag(d)-K_N PSD admits every unit-diagonal PSD matrix in its dual. Gamma=11^T is therefore admissible and gives a positive liminf of the certificate cost divided by log N. This disproves the plan's dimension-free D2 certificate budget. It does not disprove the original FDR conjecture. It also does not refute a completion certificate that fixes the alternative block, because Gamma=11^T is then generally inadmissible.
