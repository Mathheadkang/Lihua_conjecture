Read-only adversarial scout /root/adversarial_actual_negative_flux_gate supplied this restricted proof candidate; it has not received fresh independent verification and is not a proof of the current full-covariance goal. Assume signed rank-one covariance Sigma=vv^T, v_i in {-1,1}, and all true-null means in each sign group equal one common theta_epsilon<=0; alternatives have arbitrary positive means. Write m_epsilon for true-group size and a_epsilon=m_epsilon/N. Along a true coordinate's standardized regression variable x, every same-sign coordinate has mean at least theta_i. An upward forced-rank jump s->r must have a same-sign entrant at t_r, giving p_i>=t_r at contact, so both h_i limits equal g_i; hence there are no negative h_i jumps, including simultaneous contacts. The actual-mean BV identity would then give P_i=I_i-1+D_i<=I_i since D_i<=1. Each true group has a common conservative p-value p. If rejected, its FDP share is m_epsilon/R<=min(1,a_epsilon*q/p)*1{p<=q}. This is a nonincreasing function of p, so its expectation is at most its Uniform(0,1) integral a_epsilon*q*[1+log(1/a_epsilon)]. Summing at most two groups gives FDR<=q*(1+log2), and hence sum_i E P_i<=N*(1+log2). Empty groups contribute zero. All expectations and finite BV identities still require explicit checking in the singular rank-one law, especially simultaneous contacts. Staggered true-null means are not covered; no actual NT or main-FDR counterexample was found. This is a supporting candidate only and does not meet the new unrestricted acceptance criterion.
