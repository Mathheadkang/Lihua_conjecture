New tentative route to audit, distinct from the now verified fixed-K minimum formula. Do NOT claim any limit or inequality proved. Uniformly copy every original coordinate K times, and give only coordinate i's copies independent private Gaussian perturbations: theta_i+d_epsilon+(v_i dot G+epsilon xi_b)/sqrt(1+epsilon^2). Every finite expanded vector remains jointly Gaussian with unit marginals. Take K->infinity FIRST at fixed epsilon, retaining the COMMON largest BH crossing and all original atom labels. Condition on G and unchanged scores. On a candidate active block with k=r-1 unchanged selected atoms and clone rejection fraction nu, write s=epsilon/sqrt(1+epsilon^2), u(t)=S^{-1}(qt/N), and m=u(k+nu)-s S^{-1}(nu). Then u'(r)=-A_r with A_r=1/[r h(u_r)]=q/[N phi(u_r)]. A possible largest-root birth near nu=1 is at phi(t_r)=s/A_r, t_r>0, and critical conditional location heuristically equals u_r+s(t_r+1/t_r)+smaller terms. Let T_s>0 solve phi(T_s)=s. Then t_r=T_s+log(A_r)/T_s+O(T_s^{-3}), suggesting compensating the common mean by d_epsilon=s(T_s+1/T_s) leaves a rank-dependent contact shift (s/T_s)log(A_r). At theta_i<0 or theta_i>0 this positive compensation is locally label-lawful; at theta_i=0 it is NOT lawful, so zero-null boundary mass remains a separate obstruction. A tentative derivative would be minus sum_r log(A_r) mu_{i,r}(total), but this must be derived, not assumed. In particular log(A_r)=constant+u_r^2/2 is only a quadratic normal test, so this alone does not pay nonlinear inverse-Mills weights. Essential checks: derive the infinite-copy BH largest crossing from finite K; justify root births/switches against every other original rank; control fractional-copy FDP rather than replacing it by an all-or-none group; show background degeneracies and truncation errors are negligible at scale s/T_s; retain signed rank jumps and all means. Finite-K exponential-tail estimates are not uniform in K and cannot prove this. Potential reason to investigate: a legal growing-copy limit could expose a family of rank-sensitive nonlinear variations unavailable to fixed-K common-Y tests, but no unrestricted bound or coupling has been obtained.
