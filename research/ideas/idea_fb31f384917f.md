New continuation direction after the compression obstruction: work with the original finite Gaussian vector and the forced-zero largest rank for each original null label. Derive the exact conditional Gaussian likelihood identity before introducing any threshold envelope. Try a stopped Gaussian/OU likelihood mixture whose scale is localized by that same forced-zero rank, and pay its first-crossing event rather than the supremum of individual conditional densities or an inherited-carrier representation. Concrete gates: (1) write an exact, measurable original-label FDR identity with actual reciprocal ranks, including singular models by a justified approximation; (2) expose one shared stopping/scale variable and prove, rather than assume, the required adaptedness under a Gaussian innovation or reversible OU filtration; (3) test the sharp distinction between a frozen Gaussian ray and a Brownian/OU path, including independence and rare-alternative lower-family calibrations; (4) attempt a dimension-free stopped expectation estimate of order q[1+sqrt(log(1/q))], retaining its q baseline and signed rank dilution; (5) if adaptedness or scale transfer fails, identify the exact counterdiagnostic or unpaid term and retain it. No theorem is asserted. This changes neither required goal nor any approved statement and does not treat the frozen-ray conjecture as proved. Unlike weighted compression, all original means/loadings/alternative gates remain; unlike an unconstrained frozen-ray supremum, the proposed likelihood must be tied to the original rank event. No new completion root is requested.
