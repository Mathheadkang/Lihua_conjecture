A proof step to check in any future marked-array variational completion (idea_4de18419c49d), not a new claim in an approved statement. In the lawful closed tagged domain, the only noncanonical tag is ell=0 at theta=0. Deterministically retag every such parameter as ell=1, keeping means and covariance/directions unchanged. Total BH counts and selected set do not change, while the full false-discovery numerator increases pointwise; the mean-square penalty is identical. Thus the retagged completed-law value dominates the original. At an exact penalized maximum it is also a maximum, so a maximum with canonical tags can be CHOSEN without claiming that every maximum has no false-zero mass. The same argument applies componentwise to each finite marked-array Gaussian model and passes to any valid limit of its expected full FDP. This gives a simple upper comparison with the original finite-model supremum; exact value approximants, if needed, still use strictly positive shifts for false-tagged zeros with moment control. It does not justify upward motion of zero-mean true nulls, a least-favorable-mean reduction, derivative transfer, or a parameter perturbation chosen after observing Gaussian background.
