Alternative after failure of cost-free stopping: use a deterministic spectral/Gram decomposition of the original null directions, never a reduced set of carrier variables. Try to separate coherent low-effective-rank rejection packets, paid by exact Gaussian conditioning/compound-count arguments, from spread packets, paid by the approved fractional-frame Gaussian surface kernel. The concrete object must be constructed from the full original covariance before observing G. All coordinates and alternative gates remain in ordinary BH; every packet retains the same actual largest rank R. Prove a deterministic bookkeeping identity for the joint selected packet counts and denominator before applying either payment. Establish the precise conditional product hypothesis before invoking compound calibration; a Schur complement is not automatically diagonal. Avoid summing free-threshold suprema over clusters (this reintroduces directional entropy), replacing scores by representatives, or demanding unsigned payments on separate rank faces (known failed mixed-trace route). Required new gate is a global cross-packet, same-rank allocation using the exact covariance, with no uncontrolled dimension/entropy term. Test perfect clones, antipodal unbalanced clones, independent nulls and the rare-alternative common-factor lower family. No such allocation or theorem is asserted here. The two original completion roots remain unchanged.
