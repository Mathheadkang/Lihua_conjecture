Compression bounds V_- of the unloaded Schur profile for every finite Sigma by the few-block supremum M(q)+1/8. The maximizer lemma bounds q_*+V_- of the loaded population path at maximizers only. Together with the clone-block bound, compression gives V_- <= 1/4 for all finite unloaded models at small q, but transferring this to loaded array maximizers would need approximation of arrays by finite models on the loaded path and control of V_- in the limit; V_- is only lower semicontinuous under pointwise convergence, which goes the right way, but the loaded path and the density of finite models in the all-null array space are not established. Not recorded as an implication.
