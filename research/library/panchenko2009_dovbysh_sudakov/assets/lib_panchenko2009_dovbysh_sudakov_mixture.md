# Dovbysh-Sudakov mixture representation of Gram-de Finetti laws

## Statement

The law $\mathbb P$ of every [Gram-de Finetti matrix in Panchenko](../definitions/lib_panchenko2009_gram_de_finetti_matrix.md) is a [mixture of iid-generated Gram laws in Panchenko](../definitions/lib_panchenko2009_mixture_iid_gram_laws.md): there are a probability space $(\Omega,\mathcal F,\mathrm{Pr})$ and a probability kernel $\mathbb Q$ on the array space $(M,\mathcal M)$ such that
$$
\mathbb P(A)=\int_\Omega\mathbb Q(u,A)\,d\mathrm{Pr}(u)
\qquad(A\in\mathcal M),
$$
and, for every $u\in\Omega$, $\mathbb Q(u,\cdot)$ is generated by an iid sample from some probability measure on $\ell^2\times[0,\infty)$, through
$$
\bigl(\langle h_l,h_{l'}\rangle+a_l\delta_{ll'}\bigr)_{l,l'\ge1}.
$$
The notation and probability-kernel conditions are those of the linked definition. This is a normalized paraphrase of Proposition 1 together with equations (1.1)-(1.2), preserving its component-law formulation.

## Source scope

The theorem includes singular finite principal submatrices and unbounded finite-valued entries, with no moment hypothesis. The diagonal is not assumed constant. The Hilbert-vector law and diagonal correction may be dependent within each iid pair.

The source proposition is recorded here as an external theorem, not an original proof target. It does not assert in this formulation a measurable selection of representing measures across mixture components, a marked-array representation, unit-ball support inferred solely from off-diagonal bounds, a Gaussian observation representation, canonical tags, or selection of an extremizing directing component. Those project-specific conclusions still need separate proofs.
