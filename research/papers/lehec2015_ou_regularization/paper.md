# Regularization in L1 for the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck semigroup

Joseph Lehec. The PDF title page prints May 7, 2019 and the arXiv margin identifies 1505.04083v1, 15 May 2015.

## 1 Introduction — retained normalization, equation (1)

Let $\gamma_n$ be standard Gaussian measure on $\mathbb R^n$. The Ornstein–Uhlenbeck semigroup is
$$
Q_t f(x)=\int_{\mathbb R^n} f\!\left(e^{-t}x+\sqrt{1-e^{-2t}}\,y\right)\,\gamma_n(dy).
\tag{1}
$$

## 2 Main results — retained Theorems 1 and 3

### Theorem 1

Let $f$ be a nonnegative function on $\mathbb R^n$ with $\int_{\mathbb R^n}f\,d\gamma_n=1$, and let $t>0$. For every $r>1$,
$$
\gamma_n\bigl(\{Q_t f>r\}\bigr)
\leq C\,\frac{\max(1,t^{-1})}{r\sqrt{\log r}},
$$
where $C$ is a universal constant.

### Theorem 3

Let $f$ be a positive function on $\mathbb R^n$ with $\int_{\mathbb R^n}f\,d\gamma_n=1$. Assume $f$ is smooth and, pointwise, for some $\beta\geq0$,
$$
\nabla^2\log f\succeq-\beta\,\operatorname{Id}.
\tag{3}
$$
Then, for every $r>1$,
$$
\gamma_n\bigl(\{f>r\}\bigr)
\leq C\,\frac{\max(\beta,1)}{r\sqrt{\log r}},
$$
where $C$ is a universal constant. Here $\succeq$ renders the source's pointwise symmetric-matrix inequality $\geq$.
