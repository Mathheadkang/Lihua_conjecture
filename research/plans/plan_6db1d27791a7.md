## Plan

1. [x] **Derive the representation from the actual globally largest crossing.** Fix any positive strictly decreasing quantile in the statement and write its alternative count as $A_y(r)$. For every $r=p+(1-p)c>p$, prove directly from the monotone quantile that
   
   $$
   p+A_y(r)\ge r\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad y\ge u(r(c))-\vartheta(c).
   $$
   
   The forced count $H^+_y(r)=p+A_y(r)$ always qualifies at $p$. Compactness of the sublevel set, with $p$ included separately, makes its largest qualifying rank exactly the defined $R=r_\vartheta(y)$, attained also when $R=p$ or $R=1$. Since the actual count is bounded above by $H^+_y$, every actual qualifying rank is at most $R$. If $X_0\ge u(R)$, the true atom is present at $R$, so $R$ itself is an actual crossing and the actual largest rank is $R$. Conversely, if the true atom is rejected at an actual positive rank $a\le R$, then $X_0\ge u(a)\ge u(R)$. Thus rejection is equivalent to the eligibility inequality and its FDP is $p/R$; on its complement the actual FDP is zero, even if an alternative-only positive crossing exists. Condition on $Y=y$, using $|\rho|<1$, to obtain precisely $\int k(y,R)\phi(y)\,dy$. Repeat this argument for each admissible perturbed quantile. Failure condition: do not proceed if one has only a local crossing, an unattained supremum, or an auxiliary-rank equality on the ineligible event.

2. [x] **Fix deterministic margins, then choose one $K$ and one small-$\epsilon$ regime for both signs.** Choose $\eta>0$, $\alpha,\beta,m>0$ such that $I=[c_*-\eta,c_*+\eta]\subset(0,1)$,
   
   $$
   \alpha(c-c_*)^2\le g(c)-b\le\beta(c-c_*)^2\quad(c\in I),\qquad \vartheta'(c)\le-m\quad(c\in I).
   $$
   
   Justify the derivative margin from $g'(c_*)=0$ and $\vartheta'(c_*)=(1-p)u'(r_*)<0$, rather than from strict decrease alone. The unique global minimum on the compact domain supplies the strictly positive gap
   
   $$
   d_\eta=\min_{|c-c_*|\ge\eta}(g(c)-b)>0.
   $$
   
   Choose $K$ with $\alpha K^2>1$, put $C_0=4\beta K^2+1$, and require simultaneously $2K\sqrt\epsilon<\eta$, $(C_0+2)\epsilon<d_\eta$, $\epsilon<\vartheta(1)/2$, and $\sqrt\epsilon\|\chi'\|_\infty/K<m/2$. Check that both perturbed derivatives remain negative throughout $I$ and that the quantiles agree with the original outside the bump support; combine these facts to obtain global strict decrease and positivity. For the minus sign use $g^-_\epsilon\ge g-\epsilon\ge b-\epsilon$, with equality only at $c_*$. For the plus sign split the domain into the plateau, the rest of $I$, and the complement of $I$: there $g^+_\epsilon=g+\epsilon$, or $g^+_\epsilon\ge b+\alpha K^2\epsilon>b+\epsilon$, or $g^+_\epsilon\ge b+d_\eta>b+\epsilon$, respectively. Conclude the exact unique minimum $b+\delta\epsilon$ at $c_*$. Couple each original parameter to the same $c$, direction, and tag after deformation to get
   
   $$
   W_1(\pi^\delta_\epsilon,\pi)\le(1-p)\epsilon\,4K\sqrt\epsilon.
   $$
   
   Failure condition: failure to obtain a fixed positive gap or the local derivative margin prevents this route; do not silently replace either with an assumption of global convexity.

Hence at every $y\ge b+C_0\epsilon$ this whole support is included in both sublevel sets, while off the support the barriers are identical. Establish the exact equality
   
   $$
   \{c:g(c)\le y\}=\{c:g^\delta_\epsilon(c)\le y\}\quad(y\ge b+C_0\epsilon).
   $$
   
   Below $b-\epsilon$ both sublevel sets are empty. Therefore the globally maximal auxiliary ranks can differ only in $[b-\epsilon,b+C_0\epsilon]$. Inside this window any qualifying point of either barrier satisfies $g(c)\le b+(C_0+1)\epsilon<b+d_\eta$, hence belongs to $I$ and obeys $|c-c_*|\le\sqrt{(C_0+1)/\alpha}\sqrt\epsilon$.
