## Goal

Target: [Sharp uniform upper-bound target for two-sided Gaussian BH](../assets/gaussian_bh_two_sided_sharp_upper.md).

**The only conclusion.** For every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $q_\varepsilon\in(0,1)$ such that for every $q\in(0,q_\varepsilon)$, every finite $N$, every $\theta\in\mathbb R^N$ and every unit-diagonal positive-semidefinite $\Sigma$ (singular allowed; $N,\theta,\Sigma$ may depend on $q$), ordinary BH with the largest qualifying rank applied to $p_i=2\overline\Phi(|T_i|)$, true nulls $\{\theta_i=0\}$, satisfies
$$
\operatorname{FDR}\le\Big(\frac1{2\sqrt\pi}+\varepsilon\Big)q\sqrt{\log(1/q)} .
$$
Block classes, loading-one backgrounds, multiplicities and fixed-$q$ families below are gates and tests only.

**This is a PROBE plan.** Its first KEY gate (step 5) is the sharpest formulation of the missing inequality on the extreme-value staircase family (the worst configuration found by the previous plan), in an exact form that does not use any outer admissibility condition. Executing it yields either a proof of that gate or a clean, reusable obstruction. The later KEY steps (6, 7, 9, 10, 11) are stated precisely but are to be attempted only if the gate is proved.

## Mechanism chosen, and why

**Baseline-subtracted pivotal identity (BSP), planning-time result, proof in step 3.** For a true null $i$, let $\mathcal M_i=\{j\ne i: c_{ij}\ne0\}$ be its *movers* (coordinates correlated with $T_i$; for block models, the other members of its block). Let $R_i$ be the forced-zero rank ($p_i$ replaced by $0$) and let $R_i^{\flat}$ be the forced-zero rank when, in addition, every mover's p-value is replaced by $1$ ($N$ unchanged). Then:
- $R_i\ge R_i^{\flat}$ (BH rank is monotone in each p-value, $N$ fixed);
- $R_i^{\flat}$ is a function of $Y_i$ restricted to non-movers, hence independent of $T_i$, so $\mathbb E\,a(T_i;\tau_i^{\flat})=1$ exactly, with $\tau_i^{\flat}=qR_i^{\flat}/N$;
- therefore, exactly,
$$
\Delta_i=\mathbb E\big[a(T_i;\tau_i)-a(T_i;\tau_i^{\flat})\big]\le\mathbb E\Big[\frac{\mathbf 1\{\tau_i^{\flat}<p_i\le\tau_i\}}{\tau_i}\Big].
$$
In a block model $\mathfrak B$ this reads $\Delta_i=\mathbb E[\rho_{r_i}(u_{R_i},Z_g)-\rho_{r_i}(u_{R_i^{\flat}},Z_g)]$, and the integrand is zero unless the block-mates raise the rank above the rank of the background alone.

**What this removes.** If $R_i$ is independent of $Z_g$ (in particular if the null is alone in its block, as in the previous plan's extreme-value family), then $\Delta_i=0$ exactly. The previous plan's pointwise display, which was false for the (212)-outer sets in that family, concerned a quantity that is irrelevant there: with the true rank the background-only crossing cancels identically. What survives is exactly the *mates-pivotal event* $\{R_i>R_i^{\flat}\}$, on which the mates must supply a positive deficit $k-1-B(k)$ against the background count $B$. There is no sum over carriers: the background enters only through the deficit function $D_x(k)=k-1-B_x(k)$, a single nonnegative function of $x$.

**Alternatives tested at planning time and why they are not the mechanism.**
- (A) Block-level budget with an averaged background: adopted, in the exact form BSP above (the averaging is exact, not heuristic, because the baseline has mean exactly $1$). Open part: bounding the mates-pivotal tracked set (steps 5-8).
- (B) Sharpened admissibility $1\pm\epsilon$: not adopted as the mechanism. The recorded numbers $0.81$-$1.04$ straddle $1$, and the one pointwise display that would have to be below $1$ is a statement about *outer* sets; a "no double witness" bound would be needed, which is the unproved idea_c270c9b9e4f3. BSP shows the extreme-value family with the null alone does not even test it. Sharpened constants are kept only as a tool in step 7, after BSP.
- (C) Global exchangeability: tested through the two-copy form (step 3(iii)): $\Delta_i=\mathbb E[\rho_Z(u(Z))-\rho_Z(u(Z'))]$ with $(Z,Z')$ i.i.d. and the same background. By supermodularity of $\rho$ in $(|z|,u)$ the supremum over all maps $z\mapsto u(z)$ is the comonotone tilt-matching at both signs, which gives $(1-r)U/(\sqrt{2\pi}\sqrt{1-r^2})$, twice the target (the factor-two loss of Lei's Theorem 4.2(a)). The factor one half comes only from the reflected-sign exclusion, which is block (movers) structure. So (C) alone cannot give the constant; it is kept as an identity.

## First KEY gate (step 5), stated exactly

Family $\mathfrak E$ (defined in step 4): the extreme-value staircase family with mates. Required inequality: there are $q_0>0$ and $\eta(q)\to0$ (depending on $q$ only) such that for every $q\le q_0$, every $r\in(0,3/5]$ and every model of $\mathfrak E$ (any $d$, any staircases, any mates measure),
$$
\Delta_i=\mathbb E_{Z,X}\big[\rho_r(u_{R},Z)\big]-1\le\frac{U}{2\sqrt{2\pi}}+\eta(q)\,U,\qquad U=p^{-1}(q),
$$
and the same with $\rho_r(u_R,Z)-\rho_r(u_{R^\flat},Z)$ restricted to $\{|Z|\le U\}$. Failure of this inequality for a family member, with the parameter that breaks it, ends the plan (see Stop rule).

## Plan

**Fixed notation.**
- $p(u)=2\overline\Phi(u)$, $\phi$ standard normal density, $U=p^{-1}(q)$, $t_k=qk/N$, $u_k=p^{-1}(t_k)$ (so $u_k$ decreases in $k$, $u_N=U$), $J=\lceil\sqrt U\rceil$, $K=8\log U$, $L=K+\sqrt U$.
- $a(x;t)=\mathbf 1\{p(|x|)\le t\}/t$. For a true null $i$: $c_i=\Sigma_{-i,i}$, $Y_i=T_{-i}-\theta_{-i}-c_iT_i$, $\rho_i(x,y)$ is the largest qualifying BH rank of the vector with $0$ at $i$ and $p(|\theta_j+c_{ij}x+y_j|)$ at $j\ne i$, and $\tau_i=q\rho_i/N$.
- A *universal constant* $C$ does not depend on $q,N,\theta,\Sigma$, block data or loadings. Every $\eta(q)$ depends on $q$ only and tends to $0$.
- Class $\mathfrak B$ (independent one-factor blocks, $G\ge1$ arbitrary, $T_j=\theta_j+a_jZ_{g(j)}+\sqrt{1-a_j^2}\varepsilon_j$), $J_g$, $r_i=|a_i|$ and $\rho_r(u,z)=F^{(2)}_{r,z}(p(u))/p(u)$ are as in plan_two_sided_exogenous_pivotal_blocks_20261105, Part B. Recall $\rho_r$ is even in $z$ and $\int\phi(z)\rho_r(u,z)\,dz=1$ for every $u$.
- *Mates* of a null $i\in J_g$: the other members of $B_g$. *Background*: coordinates outside $B_g$.

### Part B - the extreme-value staircase gate (first KEY gate)
